## 角色概述

Roxy 是 Rockstar 官方公布的支援角色，与 [Bae-Luxe](/zh/characters/bae-luxe) 共同组成说唱双人组 Real Dimez。两人的故事自高中时代开始：从街头收债的历练，到与本地说唱歌手 DWNPLY 合作的早期爆红单曲，再到签约 [Dre'Quan Priest](/zh/characters/drequan-priest) 的 Only Raw Records——一条完整的「从街头到舞台」路径。

## 身份与定位

- **Real Dimez 成员**：与 Bae-Luxe 共同组成的说唱双人组，Vice City 音乐产业线的门面人物
- **街头叙事的代言人**：官方介绍强调组合「把向毒贩收债的年头换成了冷冰冰的现金」——歌词内容预计大量取材街头经历
- **流量时代的幸存者**：五年浮沉、历经麻烦仍在等第二次爆红，是 Leonida 残酷名利场的缩影

## 组合叙事与游戏世界观的耦合

Real Dimez 的存在把 Vice City 的嘻哈文化从「电台歌单」升级为「有人物、有利益、有冲突的产业故事」。在实机演示确认的社交媒体生态中，组合的走红路径（单曲、直播、话题）本身就是可被叙事调用的系统资源。预告片 2 中两人引燃夜间聚会的场面，也预示音乐场景在游戏中的可视化呈现密度。

## Overview

Roxy is one of Rockstar's officially revealed supporting characters, forming the Real Dimez rap duo with Bae-Luxe. Their story starts in high school, runs through years of shaking down local dealers, an early hit single with rapper DWNPLY, and lands at a deal with Dre'Quan Priest's Only Raw Records — a complete streets-to-stage arc.

### Identity & Role

- **Real Dimez member**, the public face of the game's music-industry thread
- **Street narrator**: the official bios make clear the duo traded their debt-collecting years for cold, hard cash — expect lyrics drawn from lived experience
- **A survivor of the attention economy**: five years and a whole lot of trouble, still waiting for lightning to strike twice

### Why the Duo Matters

Real Dimez upgrades Vice City's hip-hop culture from a radio playlist into a story with people, stakes, and conflicts. Inside the social media ecosystem confirmed in the Extended Look, the duo's rise path — singles, streams, virality — doubles as narrative fuel, and their Trailer 2 scene igniting a phone-waving crowd hints at how densely the music scene will be visualized in the game itself.
