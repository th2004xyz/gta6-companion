## 角色概述

卡尔·汉普顿（Cal Hampton）是 Rockstar 在预告片 2 时期通过官方角色介绍公布的支援角色之一，也是 Jason Duval 名列前茅的挚友。官方描述他「在家喝着啤酒、开着几个私密浏览器标签页监听海岸警卫队通讯时，才感觉最安全」——一个身处人生低潮却乐在其中的阴谋论者。

## 身份与定位

- **Jason 的挚友**：与主角 Jason 关系密切，同时也是 Keys 走私头目 [Brian Heder](/zh/characters/brian-heder) 的同伙，处于双主角犯罪网络的信息交汇点
- **窃听好手**：长期监听海岸警卫队（Coast Guard）通讯——对一群靠海路走私谋生的人来说，这一技能的价值不言而喻
- **阴谋论者**：官方介绍明示其偏执多疑的性格底色，为 Leonida 的荒诞世界观再添一层讽刺

## 系列定位：GTA6 的「Lester」

Cal 的定位被广泛对照《GTA5》的 Lester Crest——技术型盟友，用信息而非武力为劫掠提供支撑。区别在于：Lester 是精于算计的操盘手，Cal 则是自愿退出竞争、窝在信息茧房里的旁观者。这种「低欲望技术宅」的塑造，与 Jason、Lucia 的野心叙事形成互补。

在实机演示确认的「罪犯档案」与通缉生态下，监听执法频道的能力可能直接服务于抢劫规划与逃亡路线选择。

## 与游戏系统的关联

- **抢劫规划**：预告片 2 确认的团队劫案回归，情报型盟友预计是组队核心
- **罪犯档案系统**：执法 AI 的追踪生态下，预警类情报价值提升
- **Keys 走私线**：与 Brian Heder 的关联指向海路走私任务链

## Overview

Cal Hampton is one of the supporting characters introduced through Rockstar's official bios around Trailer 2, and Jason Duval's close friend. The official description: he "feels safest snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open" — a paranoid conspiracy theorist, comfortable at the low tide of life.

### Identity & Role

- **Jason's friend and Brian Heder's associate**, positioned at the information crossroads of the duo's criminal network
- **Eavesdropping specialist** monitoring Coast Guard channels — invaluable for a crew that moves product by sea
- **Conspiracy theorist**, adding another layer of satire to Leonida's absurd world

### The "Lester" of GTA 6

Cal invites direct comparison to GTA 5's Lester Crest — the tech-savvy ally who supplies information instead of muscle. Where Lester is a calculating operator, Cal is a bystander who opted out of the grind, happily marinating in his own information bubble — a complementary energy to Jason and Lucia's ambition narrative.

With the Criminal Record system and smarter law-enforcement AI confirmed in the Extended Look, an ally who hears the cops coming before anyone else is a very valuable friend to have.
