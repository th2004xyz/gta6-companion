## 角色概述

Bae-Luxe 是 Rockstar 官方公布的支援角色，与 [Roxy](/zh/characters/roxy) 共同组成说唱双人组 Real Dimez。官方介绍勾勒的轨迹是：一对从高中时代就认识的姑娘，把在街头向毒贩收债练出的胆识与生意头脑，转化为辛辣的说唱作品与不遗余力的社交媒体经营。与本地说唱歌手 DWNPLY 合作的一首早期单曲让她们声名鹊起——五年之后，历经无数麻烦的两人签约 [Dre'Quan Priest](/zh/characters/drequan-priest) 的 Only Raw Records，等待第二次爆红。

## 身份与定位

- **Real Dimez 成员**：与 Roxy 共同组成双人组，两人自高中起便是伙伴
- **厂牌艺人**：签约 Only Raw Records，厂牌—艺人关系是本作音乐产业线的重要一环
- **病毒式成名的化身**：爆红单曲、社交媒体运营、话题人物——Real Dimez 是 Vice City「流量经济」叙事的具象化

## 「病毒式成名」与 GTA6 的时代叙事

Real Dimez 的设计直指本作与《罪恶都市》时代最大的分野：2002 年的成名靠电台与唱片，2026 年的成名靠流媒体、病毒视频与社交媒体人设。预告片 2 中 Real Dimez 在夜间聚会引爆全场、观众纷纷举手机的画面，与实机演示确认的内置社交媒体系统互相印证——「被围观的生活」本身就是本作的世界观底色之一。

## Overview

Bae-Luxe is one of Rockstar's officially revealed supporting characters, forming the Real Dimez rap duo with Roxy. Per the official bio: high-school friends who turned the nerve and savvy from shaking down local dealers into spicy rap tracks and a relentless social media presence. An early collaborative single with local rapper DWNPLY took them to new heights — and after five years and a whole lot of trouble, they're signed to Only Raw Records, hoping lightning strikes twice.

### Identity & Role

- **Real Dimez member**, partnered with Roxy since high school
- **Only Raw Records artist**, a key node in the label storyline alongside Dre'Quan Priest and Boobie Ike
- **The embodiment of viral fame** — streaming-era stardom as a narrative pillar

### Viral Fame and the Modern Vice City

The Real Dimez speak to the biggest divide between this Vice City and the 2002 original: fame used to run through radio and record deals; now it runs through streams, viral clips, and persona maintenance. Their Trailer 2 appearance — lighting up a nighttime gathering while the crowd raises phones — dovetails with the in-game social media system confirmed in the Extended Look. Being watched is the worldview.
