## 角色概述

洛莉·赫德（Lori Heder）在 Rockstar 对 [Brian Heder](/zh/characters/brian-heder) 的官方角色介绍中被点名——这位 Keys 老练的毒品走私头目「与第三任妻子 Lori 一道，通过自己的船厂运转生意」。她是少数在官方材料中留下姓名的家族成员型角色。

## 身份与定位

- **Brian 的第三任妻子**：官方介绍明示的婚姻关系，Keys 走私帝国的家庭化构成
- **生意合伙人**：船厂走私业务的共同运营者，而非单纯的配偶角色——GTA 系列中罕见的「夫妻档犯罪」设定
- **幕后角色**：目前仅见于文字介绍，预告片与实机演示中尚未有可确认的登场画面

## 系列对照：家族化的犯罪经济

GTA 系列历来擅长用「家庭」结构呈现犯罪经济：《罪恶都市》的 Lance 与 Pete Vance 兄弟、《GTA5》的 Michael 一家（虽不参与生意，却是叙事核心）。Lori 与 Brian 的「夫妻船厂」延续这一传统——走私生意被家庭关系绑定，意味着风险共担与利益分配的叙事张力，也为 Keys 海路任务链提供了天然的剧情支点。

## Overview

Lori Heder is named in Rockstar's official bio of Brian Heder — the veteran Keys smuggler "moves his product through the boat yard" alongside Lori, his third wife. She is one of the few family figures to be named in official materials.

### Identity & Role

- **Brian's third wife**, per the official character description
- **A partner in the operation**, not a passive spouse — a rare "mom-and-pop crime" setup for the series
- **Off-screen so far**: named in print but not yet visibly confirmed in trailers or gameplay footage

### Family-Run Crime, a Series Tradition

GTA has always known how to weaponize family: the Vance brothers in Vice City, Michael's household in GTA 5. A husband-and-wife boat yard ties the smuggling economy to domestic stakes — shared risk, shared profit — and hands the Keys maritime storyline a natural dramatic anchor.
