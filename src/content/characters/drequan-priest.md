## 角色概述

德雷奎恩·普里斯特（Dre'Quan Priest）是 Rockstar 官方公布的支援角色之一。官方介绍称他「骨子里是生意人多过匪徒」——即便还在街头讨生活的年代，进军音乐圈就始终是他的目标。如今他已创立 Only Raw Records，签下了说唱组合 [Real Dimez](/zh/characters/bae-luxe)，把全部赌注押在 Vice City 的嘻哈场景上。

## 身份与定位

- **厂牌掌门人**：Only Raw Records 创始人，Vice City 音乐产业叙事的中心人物之一
- **Real Dimez 的伯乐**：签下 Bae-Luxe 与 Roxy 组成的说唱双人组，赌她们能再造一次病毒式走红
- **布比·艾克的合作方**：旗下艺人长期在 [Boobie Ike](/zh/characters/boobie-ike) 的场地演出，是连接夜店资本与音乐才华的桥梁——官方暗示这段合作关系未必长久

## 音乐产业线意味着什么

Dre'Quan、Real Dimez 与 Boobie Ike 三方构成的厂牌—艺人—场地关系网，几乎确定了一个完整的音乐产业叙事线。Vice City 自《罪恶都市》起便是流行音乐与电台文化的代名词，本作把音乐从「背景氛围」升级为「产业生态」——艺人经纪、厂牌运营、夜店演出、病毒视频，全部进入叙事版图。

在实机演示确认的社交媒体生态下，音乐传播路径（爆红单曲、直播、病毒视频）与游戏系统天然耦合，音乐产业线可能是本作「活的世界」最具代表性的切面。

## Overview

Dre'Quan Priest is one of Rockstar's officially revealed supporting characters — "more of a hustler than a gangster," who treated his street days as a runway toward the music business. He now runs Only Raw Records, has signed the Real Dimez duo, and is betting everything on the Vice City scene.

### Identity & Role

- **Label boss**: founder of Only Raw Records, a central node in Vice City's music-industry narrative
- **The Real Dimez' backer**: signed Bae-Luxe and Roxy, gambling on lightning striking twice
- **Boobie Ike's partner**: his acts book into Boobie's venues — a bridge between nightclub capital and musical talent, and per the official bios, not necessarily a partnership built to last

### Why the Music Thread Matters

The Dre'Quan–Real Dimez–Boobie Ike triangle — label, artists, venue — maps out a full industry storyline. Vice City has been synonymous with radio and pop culture since 2002; GTA 6 upgrades music from background texture to a living economy of artist management, label politics, club bookings, and viral video. With the in-game social media ecosystem confirmed in the Extended Look, the music thread is arguably the clearest lens on Rockstar's "living network" vision for Leonida.
