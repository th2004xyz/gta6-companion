## 概述

宠物互动是实机演示确认的街头可交互内容之一。演示展示的莱昂尼达街头生态中，连抚摸路边的宠物狗都是完整的可交互行为——并且这不只是卖萌：抚摸行为会影响角色的**荣誉值**，与《荒野大镖客 2》的道德系统一脉相承。

## 已确认细节

- **街头宠物可交互**：抚摸路边宠物狗为确认的交互内容
- **影响荣誉值**：交互并非纯装饰，而是接入荣誉/道德系统
- **系统渊源**：与《荒野大镖客 2》的荣誉机制思路一致，都市化落地

## 系统价值

把荣誉值这类道德计量接入街头小交互，意味着 GTA6 的「罪恶都市」同样存在善恶光谱——双主角的犯罪生涯（罪犯档案系统记录你的风格）与日常善举（荣誉值累计）将共同塑造世界对玩家的反应。从抚摸一条狗到焚毁一辆作案车，两个系统遥相呼应：**这个城市记得你做过的每一件事。**

## Overview

Pet interaction is one of the street-level interactive behaviors confirmed in the Extended Look. In the living-street ecology of Leonida, even petting a dog by the roadside is fully interactive — and it isn't just for charm: the interaction feeds your **honor**, directly echoing Red Dead Redemption 2's morality system.

### Confirmed Details

- **Street pets are interactive**: petting dogs is confirmed content
- **Honor impact**: the interaction plugs into the honor/morality system
- **Heritage**: RDR2's honor design, urbanized

### Why It Matters

Wiring morality into small street interactions means Vice City runs on a moral spectrum too: the Criminal Record system logs your crime style while honor accumulates your small kindnesses — and both shape how the world reacts to you. From petting a dog to torching a getaway car, two systems echo each other: this city remembers everything you do.
