## 概述

双主角共处互动是实机演示确认的全新机制。Jason 与 Lucia 不只是「任务里并肩」的搭档——购物、小酌、居家生活等日常共处时光会增强两人的**关系纽带**，而这条纽带的强度将**直接影响剧情分支**。GTA 系列第一次把「过日子」做成了核心玩法。

## 已确认细节

- **共处场景**：购物、小酌、居家生活等日常互动
- **关系纽带**：共处时光累积双主角羁绊
- **叙事影响**：羁绊强度直接触发剧情分支

## 系统价值

这是 GTA 叙事设计的一次范式转移：《GTA5》的三主角靠剧情硬切，《GTA6》的双主角靠玩家的日常经营。当抢劫计划、通缉逃亡与「今晚谁做饭」共享同一套资源与注意力时，邦妮与克莱德式的故事才真正成立——每一次共处都是在为某个未来的剧情分叉积累筹码。

对玩家而言，这意味着「陪伴」本身成为玩法：想要最好的结局走向，光做任务不够，还得把日子过好。

## Overview

Shared exploration is a brand-new mechanic confirmed in the Extended Look. Jason and Lucia aren't just mission partners — everyday time together (shopping, drinks, home life) strengthens their **bond**, and the strength of that bond **directly branches the story**. For the first time in the series, living together is core gameplay.

### Confirmed Details

- **Shared scenes**: shopping, drinks, domestic life
- **Bond accumulation**: time spent together builds the couple's connection
- **Narrative weight**: bond strength triggers story branches

### Why It Matters

This is a paradigm shift for GTA storytelling: GTA 5's trio was stitched together by scripted switches; GTA 6's duo is stitched together by how you live. When heist planning, wanted-level escapes, and "who cooks tonight" draw on the same pool of player attention, a Bonnie-and-Clyde story actually works — every shared evening is a chip placed on some future fork in the road. For players, companionship itself becomes gameplay: the best story outcomes won't come from missions alone, but from living well together.
