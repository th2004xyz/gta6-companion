## 中文正文

在新采访中，Rockstar 谈到了 GTA 系列招牌式讽刺的打造方式，尤其是 GTA6 是否要直接介入并嘲讽真实世界政治。

其态度很明确：内容"必须更夸张、更疯狂、更直白"（more over the top, more insane, more in your face），而不是复刻真实新闻。换言之，GTA6 用放大荒诞来回应现实，而非直接点名政治人物或事件。

这与"不追潮流"的思路一脉相承：Rockstar 选择把讽刺建立在可被反复解读的通用母题上，而非保质期极短的时事梗。

## Overview

In a new interview, Rockstar discussed its revered approach to crafting the satire the Grand Theft Auto series is famous for — particularly relating to GTA 6 and whether the game should include, and lampoon, real-world politics. The answer: it has to be more over the top, more insane, more in your face. Rather than replicating the news, GTA 6 amplifies the absurd to respond to reality.
