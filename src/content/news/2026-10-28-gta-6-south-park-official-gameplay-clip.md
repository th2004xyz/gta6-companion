## 中文正文

最新一集《南方公园》里出现了 GTA6——**不是恶搞动画，是真实机画面**。

第 29 季第 4 集 **《Guinea Pig on a Stick》** 于 10 月 28 日在 Comedy Central 播出。接近结尾处，角色 Ruth 把男孩们拉进会议室，声称要给他们看「十一月即将到来的真相」，随后打开电视。

屏幕上出现的是约 **17–20 秒的 GTA6 实机片段**：

1. Jason 与 Lucia 开车穿过罪恶都市
2. 镜头切到人潮密集的海滩
3. 一只德国牧羊犬在沙滩上排泄，主人用袋子收拾
4. Jason 朝狗主人扔了点东西，对方过来理论
5. **Jason 捡起那袋狗屎，抡在对方脸上**，袋子破裂，内容物飞溅
6. 周围的路人围观起哄

画面是标准的第三人称视角、角落有小地图，看起来就是真实游玩而非过场动画。该集**片尾字幕明确写着「Grand Theft Auto VI footage courtesy of Rockstar Games, Inc.」**，确认素材由 Rockstar 官方提供。

**为什么说这不只是个恶搞**

抛开笑点，这几秒钟透露了两件正经事：

- **海滩支持高密度、会反应的 NPC 人群**——这不是空旷的布景，是真正挤满了会起哄的围观者
- **环境物件可以被捡起当作临时近战武器**，而且被攻击者会**还手**。Rockstar 的系统性设计思路在这里很清晰：一个微不足道的遭遇，可以通过若干自然步骤自行升级

**为什么选《南方公园》**

这是一次相当精明的营销。该剧本季（被创作者戏称为《South America》）的一条主线梗就是「GTA6 一到十一月，所有社会问题都会解决」。这次不再是隔空调侃，而是 Rockstar 直接提供当前版本的真实画面。

好处显而易见：Comedy Central 的观众与 GTA 核心的 18–34 岁人群高度重叠，而 Rockstar 不必为此剪一支新预告片。上一个这么做的大概是 2006 年《Make Love, Not Warcraft》那集，由暴雪员工协助在《魔兽世界》内实拍。

还有个彩蛋：为片中角色配音的 **Tish Hicks**，正是 2013 年 Rockstar 官方 GTA5 实机演示的旁白。

**不变的**：发售日、平台与定价都没有变化——11 月 19 日，PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，标准版 79.99 美元，11 月 12 日开放预载。

## Overview

The latest episode of South Park featured GTA 6 — **not a parody animation, but real in-engine gameplay**.

Season 29 episode 4, **"Guinea Pig on a Stick"**, aired on Comedy Central on 28 October. Near the end, the character Ruth pulls the boys into a meeting room, claims she has "the truth" about what's coming in November, and switches on a television.

What plays is roughly **17–20 seconds of GTA 6 gameplay**:

1. Jason and Lucia driving through Vice City
2. Cut to a crowded beach
3. A German Shepherd relieves itself on the sand and the owner bags it
4. Jason throws something at the owner, who comes over to confront him
5. **Jason picks up the bag and smacks him across the face with it**, the bag bursting on impact
6. A crowd of pedestrians reacts around them

It's the standard third-person view with a minimap in the corner, so it reads as actual play rather than a cutscene. The episode's **end credits read "Grand Theft Auto VI footage courtesy of Rockstar Games, Inc."**, confirming Rockstar supplied it.

**Why this is more than a gag**

Two substantive things come out of those seconds:

- **Vice City's beaches support dense, reactive NPC crowds** — not an empty set, but genuinely packed with bystanders who egg things on
- **Environmental objects can be picked up and swung as improvised melee weapons**, and the victim **fights back**. Rockstar's systemic approach is legible here: a trivial encounter escalates on its own through several natural steps

**Why South Park**

It's a shrewd bit of marketing. The season — jokingly rebranded "South America" by its creators — has run on one gag all year: every problem in society will be solved once GTA 6 arrives in November. This time it isn't a joke told from a distance; Rockstar handed over real current-build footage.

The upside is obvious: Comedy Central's audience overlaps heavily with GTA's core 18–34 demographic, and Rockstar didn't have to cut a new trailer to reach them. The last comparable stunt was 2006's "Make Love, Not Warcraft", filmed inside World of Warcraft with Blizzard's help.

One bonus detail: **Tish Hicks**, who voices the character introducing the footage, also narrated Rockstar's official GTA 5 gameplay reveal back in 2013.

**What hasn't changed**: release date, platforms and pricing — November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, $79.99 Standard, preload from November 12.
