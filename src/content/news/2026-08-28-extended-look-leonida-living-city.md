## 从《大表哥 2》的荒野到罪恶城的街区

《荒野大镖客 2》用系统级细节在稀疏的荒野封神，而把同等密度的模拟搬进一座现代大都市，是难度更高的工程问题。演示给出了一份让人信服的答卷——IGN 直接称画面是「PS5 最佳」。

## NPC 生态：比你想象的更像活人

- **街区与时间双维度**：人群行为随社区和时段变化，白天海滩与深夜街区是两套生态
- **可交互密度**：连路边抚摸宠物狗都是可交互内容，且会影响荣誉值——这套系统与《大表哥 2》的思路一脉相承
- **物理模拟**：面粉动态飘散、车辆涂装随光线变化、路面磨损与雨水痕迹，整个世界是「物理上可反应的」，而不仅仅是视觉密集

## 城市与叙事的无缝衔接

过场动画与自由探索**消除镜头切换痕迹**——叙事与操作节奏高度统一。游戏内置社交媒体系统：主角可以开网红直播、关注 NPC 的成长并触发支线任务，整个莱昂尼达州像一张会自我更新的关系网。

## 为什么这很重要

GTA6 的开发投入已突破 10 亿美元，Rockstar North 自 2020 年以来的薪资支出超过 21.1 亿美元。26 分钟实机不只是画面展示，更是 R 星对「游戏可以像一座会呼吸的城市」的宣言——不需要像电影，游戏可以把人直接放进城市里。

## Overview

Red Dead Redemption 2 earned its reputation for systemic detail across sparse frontiers. Pulling that same simulation density into a gridlocked modern city is a categorically harder engineering problem — and the Extended Look makes a strong case that Rockstar has done exactly that. IGN calls the visuals the best on PS5.

### An NPC Ecosystem That Acts Alive

- **Neighborhood + time-of-day dynamics**: crowd behavior shifts by district and hour — the daytime beach and the late-night blocks run on different rules
- **Interaction density**: petting street dogs changes your honor, echoing RDR2's system
- **Physical reactivity**: flour drifts dynamically, vehicle paint responds to light, roads show wear and weather marks — the world is physically reactive, not just visually dense

### Seamless Narrative

Cinematics flow into free exploration without camera cuts. An in-game social media system lets the protagonists livestream, follow NPCs' lives, and unlock side stories — Leonida behaves like a living network that keeps updating itself.

### Why It Matters

Development spend has crossed $1 billion, with Rockstar North salaries alone exceeding $2.11 billion since 2020. The 26 minutes aren't just eye candy — they're Rockstar's thesis that a game can put you inside a city that breathes.
