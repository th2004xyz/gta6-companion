## 中文正文

2026 年 11 月的游戏圈只有两个主题：GTA 6，以及其他所有游戏如何逃离 GTA 6 的「爆炸半径」。但《哥斯拉：毁灭怪兽大乱斗 复刻版》（Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered）偏偏逆流而上，定档 **11 月 3 日**——距 GTA 6 的 11 月 19 日发售仅剩半个月。

## 「选日期时压根没想过 GTA 6」

面对 GameSpot/IGN 的采访，开发商 Pipeworks 高级设计师 **Simon Strange** 透露，GTA 6 的存在从一开始就不在定档考量里：

> 「说实话，我们定发售日期的时候脑子里**完全没有这回事**。那不是我的业务，我做游戏，不太关心别人什么时候发售。如果 Atari 跑来说『不行，这会和 GTA 撞车影响销量』，我们当然会讨论——但从没有人提过。」

## 为什么 11 月 3 日这么重要

Strange 解释，11 月 3 日对哥斯拉粉丝是个特殊的日子——**1954 年的这一天，初代《哥斯拉》电影在日本上映**，被视为哥斯拉的「官方生日」。复刻版选在这一天发售，本身就是一个致敬：

> 「哥斯拉粉丝非常期待这款游戏，他们不管哪天发售都会买。把发售日放在哥斯拉生日这天，只是一种尊重。」

## 唯二「勇士」

游戏社群津津乐道的一点是：整个流行文化里，敢和 GTA 6（以及它背后的 Rockstar）正面硬刚的只有两个 IP——怪兽之王**哥斯拉**和**芭比**（同月发售的《Barbie Rewind》经典游戏合集）。此外发行商 Devolver Digital 还宣布了一款玩梗作品《Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed》，将在 11 月 16 日与 GTA 6 前后脚登陆 Steam——「勇敢」程度更胜一筹。

## Overview

Most upcoming games have looked to steer clear of the behemoth that is Grand Theft Auto 6 and avoid the month of November entirely. The upcoming remaster of 2002's Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee is a notable exception, and as it turns out, the developers didn't think about GTA 6 at all when choosing its release date. In an interview with IGN, senior designer at developer Pipeworks Simon Strange revealed GTA's looming release of November 16 wasn't a factor when choosing Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered's November 3 release date. Like others online, the development team got a kick out of kaiju fans celebrating the fact that the King of the Monsters (and Barbie) were the only two pop culture icons brave enough to face off against developer Rockstar's open-world juggernaut.

"Honestly, [it] wasn't even in our heads at all when we were picking the release date," Strange said. "That's not my business. I make the video games. I'm not too worried about when they come out. If Atari had come to us and said, 'Wow, we can't because we think it's going to affect the sales with GTA', we would've had that discussion. But no one ever said anything."

As Strange points out, November 3 is a special date for the Godzilla fandom. It was on that same day in 1954 that the original Gojira film was released and is regarded as Godzilla's official birthday. Strange said the upcoming remaster arriving on the same day was "respectful," and that at the end of the day, Godzilla fans would show up no matter when it was released.

"I wasn't looking at the calendar and being worried in any way, or thinking about what other games are coming out because the Godzilla fans are very excited for it," Strange said. "They'd buy it on any day that it came out, and putting it out on Godzilla's birthday, that's just respectful."

While Godzilla Destroy All Monsters Melee: Remastered and Barbie Rewind, a collection of classic Barbie games, are both releasing in close proximity to GTA 6, there is one game brave enough to release on the exact same day. Publisher Devolver Digital recently revealed Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, a "remaster" of a nonexistent remake that will release on Steam November 16. For those eagerly awaiting GTA 6, Rockstar recently posted a lengthy gameplay presentation that revealed tons of new details.
