## 中文正文

11 月 19 日发售前两个月，Take-Two 举行了年度股东大会。Rockstar 本身没有在会上放新料，Zelnick 也直说"更具体的营销信息当然由 Rockstar 来给"，但会上有三个信息值得记：

**一、发售端没有持续付费。** 被问到 GTA Online 在 GTA6 里的位置时，Zelnick 明确表示：**"就本次发售而言，我们不预期会有 recurring consumer spending。"** recurring consumer spending 是 Take-Two 自己的财报术语，指鲨鱼卡、虚拟货币、附加内容这类售后收入。他同时说 GTA5 的 Online "仍然是一门很扎实的生意"。措辞是"不预期"而非"没有"，他也拒绝描述发售之后会怎样——Rockstar 至今没有公布任何 GTA6 在线模式的信息。

**二、PC 版依旧没日期。** Zelnick 只说了 Take-Two "会在任何有可观受众的平台上发行"，以及"PC 对我们公司越来越重要"。没有公布 PC 版本、没有时间窗、也没有承认在开发。目前预购仍只有 PS5 和 Xbox Series X|S。作为参照，GTA5 PC 版比主机晚约 19 个月，《荒野大镖客 2》晚约 12 个月。

**三、一次罕见的当众失态。** 一位股东直言"许多股东已经忍受了多年的延期、错过的预期，以及对少数几款大作的持续依赖"，并问董事会是否还认为现任管理层称职。Zelnick 特意把问题逐字念了一遍再反驳："我要挑一下你问题里的表述。"他随即列出：2007 年 3 月这届管理层接手时，公司营收不到 10 亿美元、股价 17 美元、被至少四个政府机构调查、13 个月没发财报，几乎破产；如今全年营收指引 80 到 82 亿美元，自 2007 年以来股价上涨约 1200%（同期标普 500 为 440%）。

## Overview

Take-Two held its annual shareholder meeting on September 17, two months before Grand Theft Auto VI ships on November 19. Rockstar announced nothing there, and CEO Strauss Zelnick noted that "more specific marketing information will, of course, come from Rockstar Games". Still, the meeting produced three things worth logging.

**No recurrent spending at launch.** Asked about GTA Online's place in GTA VI, Zelnick said: "There's no recurrent consumer spending anticipated with regard to this launch," adding that GTA Online in GTA V "continues to be a robust business for us". Recurrent consumer spending is Take-Two's own term for post-sale revenue — Shark Cards, virtual currency, add-ons. He said "anticipated" rather than a flat no, and declined to describe what happens after launch, consistent with Rockstar having published nothing about a GTA VI online mode.

**Still no PC commitment.** Zelnick said Take-Two launches "for any platforms for which there is a meaningful audience" and that "the PC is becoming more and more important to our company over time". He gave no date, no window, and no confirmation that a PC version is in development. Pre-orders remain PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. For reference, GTA V reached PC about 19 months after its console launch; Red Dead Redemption 2 took roughly 12 months.

**A rare flash of irritation.** An investor put it to the board that many shareholders "have tolerated years of delays, missed expectations, and continued dependence on a small number of major franchises". Zelnick read the question back in full before answering. "I would, however, quibble with your characterization in the question," he said, then detailed his own record: when the current team took over in March 2007 the stock was $17, revenue under a billion dollars, the company under investigation by at least four government entities and nearly bankrupt; revenue this year is guided to $8–8.2 billion, with stock appreciation of roughly 1,200% since 2007 against 440% for the S&P 500.
