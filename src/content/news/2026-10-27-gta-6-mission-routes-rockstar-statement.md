## 中文正文

10 月 27 日，Rockstar 就 8 月《加长实机演示》中的任务玩法给出**官方说明**，确认部分剧情任务支持多种完成方式，玩家不必拘泥于演示里看到的那条固定路线。

开发负责人 **Rob Nelson** 在接受 IGN 采访时解释，团队的目标是在不损害电影化质感的前提下给玩家更多自由。他举了两个例子：

- **公寓楼逃脱**：演示中杰森与露西娅从公寓楼撤离，但离开那座塔楼实际有**多条路线**。
- **Raul 那段任务**：开头是固定的——杰森射击、露西娅开车；随后切换为玩家驾驶，系统会提示最佳逃脱路线，**但玩家不照着走也能逃出去**。

Nelson 表示，凡是任务可以被系统化处理的环节——涉及通缉等级或洗劫系统的部分——都会尽量与开放世界的运作方式保持一致。这意味着 GTA6 的开放世界在某些情境下会比 GTA5 更灵活：玩家可以用不同方式接近目标而不破坏任务，重复游玩时能尝试不同路线、获得更多变数。

这也回应了此前 Bloomberg 长文中提到的开发取舍。Bloomberg 记者 Jason Schreier 转述，Rockstar 在 2019 至 2022 年间一直在把《荒野大镖客 2》的沉浸系统跟 GTA 的爽快节奏拧到一起，方向直到 2022 年底至 2023 年初才真正清晰。任务多路线设计，是这套平衡落到玩家手上的样子。

## Overview

On October 27 Rockstar published its **official explanation** of how the missions shown in August's Extended Look actually play: some story missions support multiple completion routes, and players aren't locked to the single path seen in the footage.

Development lead **Rob Nelson** told IGN the team is trying to give players more freedom without losing cinematic quality. Two examples:

- **The apartment escape**: the demo shows Jason and Lucia leaving the tower, but there are **several routes** out of it.
- **The Raul sequence**: the opening is fixed — Jason shoots, Lucia drives — then control returns to the player, who gets a suggested best escape route but **can ignore it and still get away**.

Nelson says anything that can be systematised — wanted levels, the robbery system — is kept consistent with how the open world itself works. In practice GTA 6's world is more flexible than GTA 5's in certain situations: you can approach objectives differently without breaking the mission, and replays can play out differently.

It also lines up with the development story in Bloomberg's feature. Jason Schreier reported that Rockstar spent 2019-2022 trying to fuse Red Dead Redemption 2's immersive systems with GTA's arcade pace, with the direction only settling around late 2022 to early 2023. Multi-route mission design is what that balance looks like in the player's hands.
