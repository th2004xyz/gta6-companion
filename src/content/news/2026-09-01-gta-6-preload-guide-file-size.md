## 核心时间线（confirmed，据 11/3 官方公告）

| 日期 | 事件 |
|---|---|
| **11 月 10 日** | **数字版预载开放**：PS5 按各时区零点；Xbox Series X\|S 全球按 GMT 零点统一开放。 |
| 11 月 12 日 | **盒装下载码版**在零售店开始提货 / 发货，兑换后同样可预载。 |
| 11 月 19 日 | **正式解锁**：各时区零点，游戏内容解禁。 |
| 11 月 20 日 | 预购特典「罪恶都市复古包」（Vintage Vice City Pack）领取截止。 |

> **本站更正**：此前版本写「11 月 12 日开放预载、容量约 210GB」，来自第三方读数与当时的报道口径。Rockstar 11 月 3 日的官方公告已把数字版预载提前到 **11 月 10 日**、容量定为**最多 212GB**；11 月 12 日现在专指盒装下载码版提货。详见[官方公告解读](/zh/news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-official-november-10-212gb)。

## 各平台预载说明

**PlayStation 5**

- 各地**当地时间 11 月 10 日零点**开放，不是全球同一时刻
- 预购后可在库中手动开始下载；开启**自动下载**并保持开机或待机（Rest Mode），可让下载自动开始
- GTA 6 需完整解锁，预载期间只能看到进度条

**Xbox Series X|S**

- 全球统一 **GMT 11 月 10 日 00:00** 开放，换算为美东 11/9 19:00、美西 11/9 16:00、**北京时间 11/10 08:00**
- 需开启**自动更新**并保持开机或睡眠（Sleep mode）
- 支持 **Smart Delivery**，一次下载适配对应机型；可在「管理队列」中调整优先级

**关于解锁时刻**：Rockstar 已确认两个平台均按**各时区 11 月 19 日零点**解锁，新西兰最早。预载只是下载加密文件，**不等于提前游玩**；所谓改主机地区的「新西兰技巧」不是官方支持的方式。

## 容量：官方最多 212GB（confirmed）

**Rockstar 在 11 月 3 日的公告与官方支持 FAQ 中首次给出容量**：预载文件**最多需要 212GB 可用空间，视设备而定**。官方没有按机型分别列出，也没有说明是否含首日补丁。

作为历史对照，10 月 27 日追踪 PS 商店后台的账号 **PlayStation Game Size** 曾报出 PS5 版 **210GB**（Kotaku、ComputerBase 等跟进），该读数属于第三方爆料，现已被官方数字取代。

| 作品 | 平台 | 安装容量 |
|---|---|---|
| GTA 5（次世代版） | PS5 / XSX | 约 110 GB |
| 荒野大镖客 2 | PS5 / XSX | 约 150 GB |
| **GTA 6（官方 11/3）** | PS5 / XSX | **最多 212 GB（视设备）** |
| GTA 6（Xbox） | XSX / S | 官方未单列 |

这让它成为本世代体积最大的作品之一——但并非纪录，几款《使命召唤》含 DLC 后占用更多。真正的压力在 **Xbox Series S**：约 512GB 固态、可用约 364GB，212GB 会吃掉将近六成。

> 官方数字仍**不含后续更新、DLC 与尚未公布的在线模式**。详见[容量与预载专项报道](/zh/news/2026-10-27-gta-6-download-size-210gb-preload-november-12)。

## 预载前准备清单

1. **清出至少 250 GB 空间** —— 官方 212GB + 首日补丁 + 后续更新余量。PS5 可在 设置 → 存储 查看；Xbox 在 我的游戏和应用 → 管理。
2. **检查网络** —— 212GB 在 100Mbps 带宽下约需 4.7 小时，50Mbps 接近 10 小时。发售周服务器压力大，越早下完越好。
3. **更新主机系统** —— 发售周系统更新频繁，提前升级避免解锁日卡在更新。
4. **开启自动下载 / 待机下载** —— PS5 需自动下载 + 开机或待机；Xbox 需自动更新 + 开机或睡眠。
5. **确认购买账号一致** —— 用下单时同一个 PSN / Xbox 账号登录。
6. **实体版玩家** —— 11 月 12 日收到包裹后立刻兑换（早兑早下载）。
7. **检查 GTA+ 自动续费** —— 数字预购赠送 1 个月会员，记得在这 30 天前决定去留。

## 实体版与终极版升级包

- **实体版 = Code in Box**：盒内无光盘，仅一张下载兑换码。这是系列首次全面取消实体光盘。
- **终极版无实体版本**：终极版为纯数字发行
- **$20 升级包**：已购标准版（含实体版）的玩家，可在 PlayStation Store / Microsoft Store 花 20 美元购买升级包，获得与终极版完全相同的数字内容。

> 决策建议：想收藏实体盒装又要终极版内容的，选**标准版实体 + $20 数字升级包**，总价比直接买终极版更灵活（标准版 $79.99 + $20 = $99.99，与终极版同价，但多一个实体盒）。

## Overview

**The timeline (confirmed, per the November 3 announcement)**: digital preload opened **November 10, 2026** — local midnight on PS5, 00:00 GMT globally on Xbox Series X|S. Code-in-box copies reach retailers from **November 12**. The game unlocks at **local midnight on November 19**, and the Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus must be claimed by November 20.

> **Correction**: an earlier version of this guide said preload opened November 12 at around 210GB, based on third-party readings and reporting at the time. Rockstar's November 3 announcement moved the digital preload to **November 10** and set the size at **up to 212GB**; November 12 now refers only to code-in-box retail pickup. See our [breakdown of the official announcement](/en/news/2026-11-10-gta-6-preload-official-november-10-212gb).

**By platform**

*PS5*: opens at **local midnight on November 10** in each time zone, not one global moment. Start the download manually from your library, or enable auto-downloads with the console on or in Rest Mode. GTA 6 requires the full unlock, so preloading only shows a progress bar.

*Xbox Series X|S*: opens globally at **00:00 GMT on November 10** — 7 p.m. Eastern / 4 p.m. Pacific on November 9, 8 a.m. Beijing time on November 10. Enable automatic updates with the console on or in Sleep mode. Smart Delivery serves the right build; reprioritize in the manage queue.

*Unlock time*: Rockstar has confirmed **local midnight on November 19** on both platforms, New Zealand first. Preloading fetches encrypted files and is **not early access**; changing console region is not a supported way to play early.

**Size: officially up to 212GB (confirmed)**

Rockstar gave its first figure in the November 3 announcement and support FAQ: the preload requires **up to 212GB of free space depending on your device**. There is no per-console breakdown, and no word on whether a day-one patch is included.

For historical context, PS Store backend tracker **PlayStation Game Size** reported a **210GB** PS5 download on October 27, picked up by Kotaku and ComputerBase. That was a third-party reading and is now superseded.

| Game | Platform | Install size |
|---|---|---|
| GTA 5 (current-gen) | PS5 / XSX | ~110 GB |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | PS5 / XSX | ~150 GB |
| **GTA 6 (official, Nov 3)** | PS5 / XSX | **Up to 212 GB, device-dependent** |
| GTA 6 (Xbox) | XSX / S | No separate figure |

That makes it one of the biggest of the generation — though not a record, since several Call of Duty installs with DLC are larger. The real squeeze is **Xbox Series S**: ~512GB with roughly 364GB usable, so 212GB takes close to 60 percent.

> The official figure still **excludes future updates, DLC and the unannounced online mode**. See our [dedicated size report](/en/news/2026-10-27-gta-6-download-size-210gb-preload-november-12).

**Preload checklist**

1. **Clear at least 250 GB** — 212GB plus day-one patch and future update headroom. PS5: Settings → Storage. Xbox: My games & apps → Manage.
2. **Test your connection** — 212GB takes ~4.7 hours at 100Mbps, close to ten at 50Mbps. Launch-week servers will be busy.
3. **Update your console** — system updates land frequently during launch week; get ahead of it.
4. **Enable auto / rest-mode download** — PS5 needs auto-downloads plus on or Rest Mode; Xbox needs automatic updates plus on or Sleep mode.
5. **Use the purchasing account** — sign in with the same PSN / Xbox account used to pre-order.
6. **Physical buyers**: redeem the code the day your package arrives.
7. **Check the GTA+ auto-renew** — digital pre-orders include one month; decide before day 30.

**Physical & the Ultimate upgrade**

- **Code-in-box**: no disc, just a download code — the first fully disc-less physical release in the series.
- **No physical Ultimate edition**: Ultimate is digital-only.
- **20 dollar upgrade**: Standard owners (including physical) can buy the Ultimate upgrade on PlayStation Store / Microsoft Store for identical digital content.

*Our take*: collectors who want the box and the Ultimate perks should go **physical Standard + 20 dollar digital upgrade** (79.99 + 20 = 99.99, same as Ultimate, but you get the box too).
