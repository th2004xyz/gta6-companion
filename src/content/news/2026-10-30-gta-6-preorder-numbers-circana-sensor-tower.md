## 中文正文

距离发售还有三周，Rockstar 与 Take-Two 依旧没有公布任何预购数字。但两家第三方机构给出了相当具体的轮廓——**需要反复提醒：这些是估算，不是官方数据**。

**Sensor Tower：645 万份 / 6.3 亿美元（截至 10 月 20 日）**

| 平台 | 预购量 | 消费额 | 占比 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | 约 480 万份 | 约 4.68 亿美元 | 74% |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 约 166 万份 | 约 1.62 亿美元 | 26% |
| **合计** | **约 645 万份** | **约 6.30 亿美元** | 100% |

一周之前的读数约为 608 万份 / 5.94 亿美元，一周增长约 37 万份。该估算已扣除退款，**但不包含实体版，也不覆盖所有零售渠道**。

增速对比更直观：Sensor Tower 统计美国主机数字市场从发售前 149 天到发售前 45 天的预购表现，GTA6 比 2022 年的《使命召唤：现代战争 2》**高出 148%**。需注意这仅覆盖美国数字预购，不能外推成全球全渠道的领先幅度。

**Circana：29% 购买意愿，该机构追踪史上的最高值**

另一家机构 Circana 的 PlayerPulse 调查显示，**29% 的美国主机与 PC 玩家表示计划购买 GTA6**，这是 Circana 有记录以来任何游戏的最高购买意愿。

分析师 Mat Piscatella 称这次发售是「游戏行业罕见的文化与商业时刻」，并指出需求会外溢到游戏之外——**主机、外设与游戏订阅服务**。他认为许多仍在使用上一代主机的玩家会为了这一款游戏换新机；配合黑色星期五临近，GTA6 将成为四季度软件、主机换新与硬件消费的核心驱动力。

Circana 预测 2026 年美国游戏市场支出将上涨 3% 至 **628 亿美元**，打破 2021 年 617 亿美元的纪录。Take-Two 则预计截至 2027 年 3 月的财年净预订收入达创纪录的 **80 亿至 82 亿美元**。

**各家预测对照（口径各不相同，别混着看）**

| 来源 | 口径 | 数字 |
|---|---|---|
| Newzoo（7 月） | 预购首周全球数字销售额 | 约 2.6 亿美元 |
| Newzoo（基准情景） | 发售首周销售额 | 约 45 亿美元（按均价 88 美元折合约 5100 万份），区间 33–52 亿 |

**下一个官方节点：11 月 9 日**，Take-Two 发布 2027 财年第二季度财报（美股收盘后，分析师电话会美东时间 16:30）。但该季度截至 9 月 30 日，**不含任何 GTA6 销量**，公司指引为净亏损 1.40 亿至 1.57 亿美元。

## Overview

Three weeks out, Rockstar and Take-Two have still published no pre-order figures. Two third-party firms have nonetheless sketched a fairly specific picture — **and it bears repeating: these are estimates, not official data**.

**Sensor Tower: 6.45 million copies / $630 million (as of October 20)**

| Platform | Pre-orders | Spend | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | ~4.8M | ~$468M | 74% |
| Xbox Series X\|S | ~1.66M | ~$162M | 26% |
| **Total** | **~6.45M** | **~$630M** | 100% |

The reading a week earlier was roughly 6.08 million copies and $594 million — about 370,000 in a week. The estimate is net of refunds but **excludes physical copies and does not cover every retail channel**.

The pace is clearer in context: measuring US console digital pre-orders from 149 days before launch to 45 days before, Sensor Tower puts GTA 6 **148% ahead** of 2022's Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II. That comparison covers US digital pre-orders only and shouldn't be extrapolated to worldwide, all-channel performance.

**Circana: 29% purchase intent, the highest it has ever recorded**

Circana's PlayerPulse tracker finds that **29% of US console and PC players say they intend to buy GTA 6** — the highest purchase intent the firm has logged for any game.

Analyst Mat Piscatella calls the launch "a rare cultural and commercial moment for the video game industry" and argues demand will spill beyond the game itself into **consoles, accessories and gaming subscriptions**. He expects many players still on last-generation hardware to buy a PS5 or Xbox specifically for it, and, with Black Friday approaching, names it a core driver of fourth-quarter spending.

Circana projects US game spending up 3% to **$62.8 billion** in 2026, beating the $61.7 billion record set in 2021. Take-Two expects record net bookings of **$8.0–8.2 billion** for the fiscal year ending March 2027.

**Forecasts side by side (different windows — don't mix them)**

| Source | What it measures | Figure |
|---|---|---|
| Newzoo (July) | Global digital spend, first pre-order week | ~$260M |
| Newzoo (central case) | Launch-week sales | ~$4.5B (~51M units at an assumed $88 average), range $3.3–5.2B |
| Piper Sandler (June) | Day-one units | 45M+ (modelled on r/GTA6 traffic) |
| Real-world reference | GTA 5, day one, 2013 | 11.21M units |

**The next official checkpoint is November 9**, when Take-Two reports fiscal Q2 2027 after the market close, with a call at 4:30 p.m. Eastern. That quarter closed September 30 and **contains no GTA 6 sales**; the company has guided to a net loss of $140–157 million.
