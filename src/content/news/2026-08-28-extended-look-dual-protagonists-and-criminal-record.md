## 史上最高热度的游戏实机首播

北京时间 2026 年 8 月 28 日凌晨 3 点，Rockstar 通过 Netflix 全球独家首播《GTA6: An Extended Look》（分量十足的一瞥）——一段长达 **26 分钟、全程 PS5 原生实机画面**的加长演示，没有 CG、没有剪辑拼接。据 Streams Charts 统计，全球各平台主播转播峰值合计 **397 万人同时观看**，刷新 2026 年游戏直播纪录，Twitch 收到超过 1.7 万份宕机报告。

这也是对 8 月下旬泄漏风波的正面回应：R 星公开承认 CyberLeek 泄漏属实，但发售计划不变——**11 月 19 日，PS5 / Xbox Series X|S**。

## 双主角无缝切换

Jason 与 Lucia 可以随时无缝切换操控，被切换的角色会**自动接管行动**——继续驾驶、射击或协作，不会站桩等待。演示中展示了两人共同执行帮派任务的场景，配合新加入的「共同探索」机制：共处互动（购物、小酌、居家生活）会增强角色关系纽带，**直接影响剧情分支**。

## 「罪犯档案」系统

本次演示首次引入会随玩家行为动态变化的**罪犯档案（Criminal Record）**系统：抢劫时戴面罩隐藏身份、任务后焚毁车辆、更换衣着降低通缉等级——你的犯罪风格会被这座城市"记住"。六星通缉正式回归，执法 AI 的追踪与包抄策略比历代更具压迫感。

## 战斗手感：大表哥 × 马克思佩恩

战斗部分明显更硬：敌人会破门围攻、多方向包抄；主角新增**近战格挡**与**子弹时间弱点识别**；双主角可协同战斗（掩护射击、分工劫掠）。动作手感更接近《荒野大镖客 2》的扎实，又带着《马克思·佩恩 3》式的直接冲击力。

## Overview

At 3 a.m. Beijing time on August 28, 2026, Rockstar premiered "GTA 6: An Extended Look" exclusively on Netflix — a 26-minute showcase captured entirely on PS5, with no CG and no editing tricks. Per Streams Charts, co-streaming peaked at 3.97 million concurrent viewers, a 2026 record, and Twitch received over 17,000 outage reports.

The showcase was also a direct answer to the CyberLeek leaks: Rockstar confirmed the leaks were real, but the launch plan stands — **November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**.

### Seamless Dual-Protagonist Switching

Jason and Lucia can be swapped at any time, with the idle character automatically continuing to act — driving, shooting, or cooperating. A new "shared exploration" mechanic means time spent together (shopping, drinks, home life) strengthens their bond and directly branches the story.

### The Criminal Record System

The showcase introduced a Criminal Record system that evolves with player behavior: wear masks during heists to hide your identity, torch the getaway car and change clothes to shed heat. Six-star wanted levels return with law-enforcement AI that flanks, breaches, and pressures like never before.

### Combat: RDR2 Grit, Max Payne Punch

Melee blocks, bullet-time weak-point targeting, and coordinated duo combat (covering fire, split looting). Enemies breach doors and encircle from multiple angles — the closest feel to Red Dead Redemption 2's weight with Max Payne 3's directness.
