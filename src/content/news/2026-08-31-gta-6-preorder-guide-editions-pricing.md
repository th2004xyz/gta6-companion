## 核心信息速览

- **预购开启**：2026 年 6 月 25 日（全球同步）
- **标准版**：$79.99 —— 完整单人剧情 + 线上模式基础内容
- **终极版**：$99.99 —— 独家剧情支线、限定载具套装、双倍线上启动资金、专属角色外观
- **数字版预载**：11 月 12 日开放，发售当天即玩
- **预购特典**：11 月 20 日前预购送「罪恶都市怀旧包」（复古载具、80 年代主题服装、经典武器皮肤）
- **数字版加赠**：额外 1 个月 GTA+ 会员

## 标准版 vs 终极版怎么选

| 内容 | 标准版 | 终极版 |
|---|---|---|
| 完整单人剧情 | ✅ | ✅ |
| 线上模式基础内容 | ✅ | ✅ |
| 独家剧情支线 | — | ✅ |
| 限定载具套装 | — | ✅ |
| 双倍线上启动资金 | — | ✅ |
| 专属角色外观 | — | ✅ |

**我们的建议**：单机剧情党买标准版即可；打算长期玩 GTA Online 的玩家，终极版的双倍启动资金 + 载具套装在前期能明显提速，差价 $20 属于「一次性省肝投资」。

## 预载时间线（confirmed）

- **11 月 12 日**：数字版预载开放 + 实体版（含下载兑换码）上架
- **11 月 19 日**：正式解锁开玩
- 注意：实体版包装盒内是**下载兑换码（Code in Box）**，不是数据光盘；实体版玩家同样 11 月 12 日起可兑换并预载，但内容锁定至 19 日

## Overview

**Quick facts**: Pre-orders opened June 25, 2026. Standard is $79.99, Ultimate $99.99. Digital preload starts November 12, launch is November 19. Pre-order before Nov 20 for the Vice City Nostalgia Pack (retro vehicles, '80s apparel, classic weapon skins); digital pre-orders also get 1 month of GTA+.

## Standard vs Ultimate

| Content | Standard | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| Full story campaign | ✅ | ✅ |
| Online base content | ✅ | ✅ |
| Exclusive story missions | — | ✅ |
| Limited vehicle set | — | ✅ |
| Double Online starting funds | — | ✅ |
| Exclusive character outfits | — | ✅ |

**Our take**: story-only players are fine with Standard; if you plan to go deep into GTA Online, the Ultimate's double starting funds and vehicle set pay for themselves quickly.

## Preload Timeline (confirmed)

- **Nov 12**: digital preload opens; physical editions (code-in-box, no disc) also available
- **Nov 19**: unlock
- Physical copies use a download code in the box — no game data on the disc; the game stays locked until Nov 19

## Channels

PlayStation Store / Microsoft Store for earliest preload and the GTA+ bonus; physical retail for collectors.
