## 中文正文

发售前一个月，GTA6 的分级拼图已经集齐。以下是各主要市场截至 10 月 23 日的读数：

| 地区 / 机构 | 分级 | 内容描述 |
|---|---|---|
| 北美 ESRB | **M（17+）** | 血腥、强烈暴力、成人幽默、裸体、粗口、强性内容、烟酒药品；含内购 |
| 欧洲 PEGI | **18** | 毒品、性、暴力；含内购 |
| 德国 USK | **18** | 明确暴力、毒品、犯罪榜样；含内购 |
| 日本 CERO | **Z（18 岁以上）** | 犯罪、暴力 |
| 韩国 GCRB | **19+** | 未成年人不可 |
| 澳洲 | **R18+** | 高强度暴力、性场面、毒品使用 |
| 巴西 ClassInd | **18** | 毒品、不当语言、性内容、暴力 |

**先厘清一个常见误读。** 近期不少标题把 GTA6 称作「成人限定（Adults Only）」。在北美，ESRB 确实有一个名为 **AO（Adults Only 18+）** 的等级，但那是与 M 完全不同的另一档，主机厂商和大型零售商通常不会上架 AO 作品。**GTA6 拿到的是 M，不是 AO。** 准确的说法是：它在所有主要市场都落在主流渠道愿意销售的最高常规成年级别。

**韩国的判定有额外分量。** 自 2025 年 11 月 1 日起，成人级 PC / 主机游戏的分级权限从政府机构 GRAC 移交给了私营的 **GCRB**，GTA6 是移交后最受关注的一部送审作品。参照系两极分化：GTA5 当年拿到成人级并于 2013 年在韩国发售；《弹丸论破 V3》则在 2017 年因反社会描写被拒绝分级、无法正式发行。GTA6 通过，意味着韩国市场已无监管障碍。

**日本还有一个未决问题。** CERO 的 Z 级本身只是年龄门槛，但 GTA5 的日版曾对部分性场景与血腥内容做过修改。截至目前，**没有任何官方说法表明 GTA6 日版会有地区性删改**——任何相关断言都属于推测。

## Overview

A month out, GTA 6's ratings are complete. Here is where each major market stood as of October 23:

| Region / board | Rating | Content descriptors |
|---|---|---|
| North America (ESRB) | **M (17+)** | Blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, strong sexual content, drugs/alcohol; in-game purchases |
| Europe (PEGI) | **18** | Drugs, sex, violence; in-game purchases |
| Germany (USK) | **18** | Explicit violence, drugs, criminal role models; in-game purchases |
| Japan (CERO) | **Z (18+)** | Crime, violence |
| South Korea (GCRB) | **19+** | Not for minors |
| Australia | **R18+** | High impact violence, sex scenes, drug use |
| Brazil (ClassInd) | **18** | Drugs, inappropriate language, sexual content, violence |

**One misread worth clearing up.** Recent headlines call GTA 6 "adults only." The ESRB does have a tier literally named **Adults Only (AO)**, but it is separate from M, and console makers plus major retailers generally won't carry AO titles. **GTA 6 is rated M, not AO.** The accurate framing: it sits in the strictest standard adult tier that mainstream stores will carry, in every major market.

**Korea's ruling carries extra weight.** Since November 1, 2025, adults-only classification for PC and console games moved from the government-run GRAC to the private **GCRB**, and GTA 6 is the highest-profile title reviewed since. The precedent cuts both ways: GTA 5 got an adult rating and launched there in 2013, while Danganronpa V3 was refused classification in 2017 over antisocial depictions and could not be officially distributed. GTA 6 passing means no regulatory hurdle remains in that market.

**Japan still has an open question.** CERO Z is only an age gate, but GTA 5's Japanese release was edited for certain sexual scenes and graphic content. As of now **nothing official says GTA 6's Japanese build differs** — treat any claim of a censored version as speculation.
