## 中文正文

进入发售前最后 17 天，GTA6 的宣传从「媒体投放」转成了「物理占领」。

**全球地标被刷上 GTA6**

Take-Two 与 Rockstar 的国际宣传已经铺开：GTA6 的**官方图像与标志出现在多国建筑外墙与体育设施上**，形式包括巨型 LED 显示屏、大幅乙烯基海报和专门制作的广告装置，投放点涵盖现代体育场与知名历史建筑。社交平台上，不少用户正在转发被相关横幅覆盖的建筑照片。

这是 10 月中旬美国本土那轮街头营销的升级版——当时好莱坞大道、布鲁克林、芝加哥与时代广场同步出现了手绘墙绘与Spotify 原声广告牌。

行业内有人据此推测：这类大规模户外投放通常意味着最终版本正在为实体渠道铺货做准备，**延期可能性随之降低**。需要说清楚：这属于从业者的经验判断，**不是开发商的正式保证**。

**沃尔玛「神秘礼包」传闻**

另一条正在流传的消息：Rockstar 可能与沃尔玛合作推出线上预购活动，玩家在沃尔玛官网下单并到指定门店自提时，可能获得一个名为 **「surprise and delight bag」** 的神秘礼包。

目前可以确定的事实边界：

- 沃尔玛官网在 PS5 与 Xbox 版实体预购页面上**确实标注了「Surprise and Delight」字样**，并注明「送完即止」
- 页面**同时明确写明礼包内不含实体光盘**
- 礼包被描述为一只 GMD（日用百货配送）袋，内装「好东西」，**没有更多细节**
- 领取时间为 **11 月 12 日起**，与预载窗口一致

尚不确定的部分：礼包内容、参与门店、库存规模、是否覆盖所有版本，**沃尔玛与 Rockstar 均未正式公布**。社区猜测从薯片汽水到独立地图、徽章、钥匙扣都有，但这些只是猜测。

需要区分的是：这个礼包与售价 400 美元的**收藏版（Collector's Edition）**是两回事，后者才确实包含实体地图与罪恶都市主题收藏品。

**别搞错 11 月 12 日**

再强调一次：11 月 12 日是**预载开放日**，不是提前开玩。预载下载的是加密安装文件；实体版盒内也只有下载码、没有光盘。全球统一开放游玩时间是 **11 月 19 日午夜**。

## Overview

With 17 days to go, GTA 6's marketing has shifted from media buys to physical occupation.

**Landmarks go GTA 6**

Take-Two and Rockstar's international push is now visible: official GTA 6 **artwork and logos have appeared on building facades and sports venues across multiple countries**, via giant LED screens, large-format vinyl banners and purpose-built installations, with sites ranging from modern stadiums to historic buildings. Photos of the wrapped buildings have been circulating widely on social media.

It's an escalation of the mid-October US street campaign, when hand-painted murals and Spotify soundtrack billboards went up at once on Hollywood Boulevard, in Brooklyn, across Chicago and in Times Square.

Some in the industry read the scale of the outdoor spend as a sign that the final build is being staged for physical distribution, and that **the odds of another delay are falling**. To be clear: that is practitioners' inference, **not a guarantee from the developer**.

**The Walmart "surprise bag" rumour**

Also making the rounds: Rockstar may be working with Walmart on an online pre-order promotion, where customers who order on Walmart.com and collect in store receive a **"surprise and delight bag"**.

Here is the boundary between what's established and what isn't:

- Walmart's site **does list a "Surprise and Delight" tag** on physical pre-order pages for both PS5 and Xbox, marked "while stocks last"
- The page **also states explicitly that the bag contains no physical disc**
- The bonus is described as a GMD (General Merchandise Delivery) bag packed with "goodies", **with no further detail**
- It ships from **November 12**, in line with the preload window

What remains unknown: contents, participating stores, stock levels and whether it covers every edition. **Neither Walmart nor Rockstar has announced it formally.** Community guesses run from crisps and soda to a standalone map, pins or keyrings — but those are guesses.

One distinction worth drawing: this is separate from the **$400 Collector's Edition**, which genuinely does include a physical map and Vice City-themed collectibles.

**Don't get November 12 wrong**

Stated once more: November 12 is the **preload date**, not early access. Preloading fetches encrypted install files, and the boxed edition carries a download code rather than a disc. Play opens globally at **midnight on November 19**.
