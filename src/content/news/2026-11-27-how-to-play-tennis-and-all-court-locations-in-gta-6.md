Tennis was one of the most beloved minigames from Grand Theft Auto 5, and by all indications, it appeared that Rockstar Games was bringing it back for GTA 6 . However, players are dozens of hours into the game by this point, and the tennis minigame has still eluded everyone. While there are plenty of golf courses and racing challenges for players to participate in, no tennis icon appears in GTA 6. All tennis court locations in GTA 6 Courtland Park Stadium Port Vice City Ocean Beach Washington Beach Rialto Islands (Catalan Island) Can you play tennis in GTA 6? Technically, tennis is a playable minigame in GTA 6. However, it's not nearly as complete as the other minigames, such as golf, billiards, and racing. Instead, tennis looks to be in a similar spot to the basketball minigame. You can freely pick up rackets and tennis balls in GTA 6, but no location offers you the ability to play against NPCs or other characters in full-on matches. You're only able to hit the ball with the racket, and this doesn't even have to be on a tennis court. Compared to GTA 5, which lets you play hour-long matches against Michael's wife or other characters, this is quite the step down when it comes to tennis. However, Rockstar Games has said certain minigames simply couldn't be implemented in time, and it looks like they prioritized other content over tennis in GTA 6. Still, with minigames like tennis and basketball included in GTA 6, future updates or even DLC could expand on them. After all, there are plenty of tennis courts to make use of in Leonida. All tennis court locations in GTA 6 There are several tennis courts you can find and enter in GTA 6. It's at these courts where you'll be able to find a tennis racket and ball to pick up and practice your serves with. However, no matter how long you stand around, no opponent will show up to offer you a match. Still, whether you want to see the courts or maybe remember them for the future, you can see every tennis court location in GTA 6 below. Courtland Park Stadium This is the largest and most recognizable tennis court in GTA 6. Located in the center of Catalan Key in Vice-Dale County, this is a massive tennis stadium that also has a few separate tennis courts surrounding it. If tennis ever comes to GTA 6 as a full minigame, this is likely where it will start. Port Vice City Strangely enough, there's a random tennis court in the center of Port Vice City. There seems to be no reason for it to be there, but you've likely driven by it several times without even realizing. Ocean Beach With a neighborhood as wealthy as Ocean Beach, you have to expect at least one tennis court. This one is located right outside of a large building across the street from Lucciano's poolside bar and restaurant. Washington Beach Washington Beach is another rich neighborhood that features a tennis court. You can find this court right near the Y Hotel beach in the central part of Washington Beach. Rialto Islands (Catalan Island) The final tennis court we were able to find in GTA 6 is located in the Rialto Islands.
