## 中文正文

距发售只剩五周，媒体评测的节奏开始浮出水面。据可靠业内消息源 NateTheHate 称，**面向媒体的评测版将在 10 月底前发放**，而评测 embargo（解禁日）会在 **11 月 19 日游戏正式发售日之前解除**——这意味着玩家有望在发售前读到权威媒体的评分与实机评价。

这条消息与早前 Insider Gaming 的一篇报道相互印证（该文随后被删稿），后者提到评测禁令会在发售日前放开。需要说明：Rockstar 从未官方公布评测安排，以下均来自业内消息，属「据报道」范畴。

为什么这件事值得关注：

- **泄密阴影仍在**。臭名昭著的泄密者 Cyberleek 持续放出《GTA6》内容，成为 Rockstar 最头疼的麻烦；尽管对方声称已收手，评测流程本身仍可能剧透。
- **无光盘反而更安全**。本作不提供 Xbox 与 PS5 实体光盘（实体版为盒装下载码），相较传统寄盘方式，泄露概率更低。
- **Take-Two 仍看重媒体评价**。CEO Strauss Zelnick 今年 5 月强调，专业媒体评价依然重要；他指出 Rockstar 作品在 Metacritic 常年稳定在 95 分上下，评分对口碑转化有意义。
- **评测窗口很长**。按当前安排，评测人员将有三周甚至更久探索罪恶城及其周边；多数玩家预估主线至少 50 小时，莱昂尼达（Leonida）地图规模超过 Rockstar 以往任何开放世界。

背景：巴西记者 Pedro Henrique Lutti Lippe 曾称，Rockstar 会邀请媒体前往保密地点试玩以最大化降低泄露风险；但集中隔离试玩对一款 50 小时体量的游戏可行性不高，因此更可能走数字版评测码。

## Overview

With five weeks to launch, the review cadence is coming into focus. Per reliable industry insider NateTheHate, **review copies will go out by the end of October** and the review embargo lifts **before the November 19 launch** — so players could read trusted scores and hands-on verdicts ahead of release.

That lines up with an earlier Insider Gaming report (later deleted) saying the review embargo would open before launch day. Caveat: Rockstar has never officially published its review plan, so everything below is reported, not on the record.

Why it matters:

- **The leak cloud still hangs.** The notorious leaker Cyberleek keeps drip-feeding GTA 6 content; even if they claim to be done, the review process itself can spoil.
- **No disc means less risk.** The game ships without Xbox or PS5 physical discs (the boxed edition is a code), lowering leak exposure versus mailed media.
- **Take-Two still values pro reviews.** CEO Strauss Zelnick stressed in May that professional critics matter; he noted Rockstar titles sit around 95 on Metacritic, where scores drive word-of-mouth conversion.
- **A long review window.** As currently framed, reviewers get three weeks or more to explore Vice City and beyond; most players expect a 50-hour-plus campaign, and Leonida is larger than any Rockstar open world to date.

Context: Brazilian journalist Pedro Henrique Lutti Lippe previously said Rockstar would fly media to a secure location to minimize leaks — but quarantining critics for a 50-hour game is impractical, so digital review codes are the likelier route.
