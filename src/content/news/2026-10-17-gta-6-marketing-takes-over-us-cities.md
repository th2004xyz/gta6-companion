## 中文正文

上市预告放出后的第一个周末，GTA6 的户外广告在美国几座大城市同时铺开。VICE 的追踪汇总如下：

- **好莱坞大道**：Jason 与 Lucia 的巨幅壁画已经完工，成为沿街地标。
- **布鲁克林**：Colossal Media 手绘了一幅占据整面建筑立面的 GTA6 墙绘。
- **芝加哥**：双层观光巴士披着 GTA6 粉色+蓝色的配色穿城而过。
- **纽约**：时代广场大屏投放数字广告，Spotify 原声企划广告牌同步上墙；有玩家把自己改装成 GTA4 警车的爱车开到 GTA6 广告牌前合影。
- **洛杉矶**：Spotify 音乐相关广告牌上线；早前 Netflix 曾在日落大道亮出「I ♥ VICE CITY」。

这套打法与之前的飞艇、球馆灯牌、杂志封面、DAZED 专题、Game Informer 封面报道串成一条线：R 星在发售前两个月把户外、音乐、时尚、体育四条线同时拉满。迈阿密-戴德县一名专员称营销投入至少 1.5 亿美元。

**顺带一条趣闻。** 10 月 27 日，Xbox 官方账号发了一张配文「我们的秋天会是什么样子」的宣传图，图中是 GTA6 出现在主页的画面。推文获得 271 万次浏览，玩家的注意力却全在细节上——**图里的 GTA6 没有「预购」字样**。有人调侃「这兄弟有提前购买资格」，也有人指出图中不该出现 Rockstar 标志。Xbox 与 R 星均未回应，这属于社区猜测，没有证据表明有人提前玩上了。

## Overview

On the first weekend after the launch trailer, GTA 6 outdoor advertising landed in several US cities at once. VICE's tracking lists:

- **Hollywood Boulevard**: the giant Jason and Lucia mural is finished and already a landmark.
- **Brooklyn**: Colossal Media hand-painted a GTA 6 wall covering an entire building face.
- **Chicago**: double-decker sightseeing buses wrapped in the game's pink-and-blue livery toured the city.
- **New York**: Times Square digital screens plus Spotify soundtrack-campaign billboards; one fan parked their GTA 4 police-cruiser conversion in front of the GTA 6 mural for photos.
- **Los Angeles**: Spotify music billboards, after Netflix's earlier "I ♥ VICE CITY" display on Sunset Boulevard.

It connects to everything before it — the blimp, the arena roof sign, magazine covers, the DAZED feature, Game Informer's cover story. Two months out, Rockstar is running outdoor, music, fashion and sports channels at once; a Miami-Dade commissioner puts the spend at a minimum of $150 million.

**One sideshow.** On October 27, Xbox posted a promo image captioned "what our autumn is looking like," with GTA 6 on the home screen. The post drew 2.71 million views, but players noticed one detail: **the GTA 6 tile showed no "pre-order" label**. Replies ranged from "this guy has early access" to questions about why the Rockstar logo appeared where it shouldn't. Neither Xbox nor Rockstar has commented — it's community speculation with no evidence anyone is playing early.
