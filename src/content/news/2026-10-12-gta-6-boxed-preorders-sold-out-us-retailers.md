## 中文正文

发售进入倒计时最后五周，实体渠道开始吃紧。据 RockstarINTEL 观察，截至 10 月 11 日，Amazon 美国站的 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S「盒装兑换码版」均已下架预购入口；同时洛杉矶、纽约等人口密集地区的 Target 实体店多款现货售罄。

需要区分的是：这可能是「盒装兑换码版」的产能或配货节奏问题，而非官方调价。Rockstar 官方商店页面自 6 月起就一直标注「售完即止（while supplies last）」，说明官方对首批实体供应量本就谨慎。数字版不受影响，仍可在 PS Store、Xbox Store 与 Rockstar Store 预购。

## Overview

With five weeks to launch, physical channels are tightening up. RockstarINTEL notes that as of October 11, Amazon US had pulled the pre-order listing for both the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S code-in-a-box copies, and many Target stores in dense US areas — Los Angeles and New York among them — had sold out of in-stock stock.

Worth separating: this may be a supply or allocation hiccup for the code-in-a-box edition rather than an official price change. Rockstar's store page has read "while supplies last" since June, signalling the publisher was always cautious about first-wave physical supply. The digital edition is unaffected and still pre-orderable via PS Store, Xbox Store and the Rockstar Store.
