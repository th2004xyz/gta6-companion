## Overview

Those who grew up in the 80s and 90s would remember things known as gamebooks, the precursors to modern-day role-playing and narrative-driven games. You might recall the Choose Your Own Adventure series and R.L. Stine’s Give Yourself Goosebumps, which presented you with choices, making you turn page after page to see the outcome. Further back, we had classics like the Sorcery series and The Warlock of Firetop Mountain--co-written by authors Steve Jackson and Ian Livingstone, who coincidentally also founded Games Workshop (yes, the very company that owns Warhammer). Grand Theft Auto 6 pays homage to these works in the form of book swap locations, which you can find all over Leonida.
