## 中文正文

10 月 26 日至 27 日，爆料人 **NateTheHate** 在 X 上表示，Rockstar Games 已开始向媒体机构发放《GTA6》评测码，**评测解禁时间为 11 月 18 日**——也就是游戏发售的前一天。

这与此前节奏吻合。NateTheHate 在 10 月初曾说过「评测码还没发出，但月底前会发」，如今他表示这一步已经完成。Insider Gaming 称独立验证了 11 月 18 日这个日期，因此该信息目前有两个来源支撑，但**Rockstar 与 Take-Two 均未公开确认，也没有任何媒体公开表示已收到评测码**。

如果成立，拿到评测码的机构有约三周时间通关并撰写稿件。而对玩家来说，从 11 月 18 日看到评分到 11 月 19 日游戏解锁，参考窗口只有一天。有报道把解禁时刻估在美国东部时间 11 月 18 日早上 6 至 7 点，但具体时刻未获确认。

**为什么这么安排？** 发售前一天解禁是 R 星近两部大作的惯例：GTA5 评测在 2013 年 9 月 16 日解禁（发售前一天），荒野大镖客 2 在 2018 年 10 月 25 日解禁（发售前一天）。这样做的好处是让媒体在尽可能接近成品的版本上完成体验，代价是把口碑悬念留到最后一刻。

**玩家的应对很实际**：11 月 18 日当天看多家评测，若觉得不合胃口，仍有一天时间可以取消预购——前提是媒体和内容创作者没有同时被要求延后到 11 月 19 日发布。

**NateTheHate 的记录**：他被 Reddit 游戏泄露社区评为 2025 年度爆料人，此前关于 GTA6 的两个判断（11 月发售日会守住、8 月会有营销动作）均已应验。不过在官方或持码媒体开口前，这仍然只是一句爆料。

## Overview

On October 26-27, leaker **NateTheHate** said on X that Rockstar Games has begun distributing GTA 6 review code, with **the embargo set for November 18** — one day before the game ships.

That matches the earlier rhythm. Earlier in October he said codes hadn't gone out yet but would before the month ended; he now says that has happened. Insider Gaming says it independently verified the November 18 date, so the claim currently rests on two sources — but **neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it, and no outlet has publicly acknowledged holding a copy**.

If accurate, outlets with code get roughly three weeks to play through and file. For players, the window between reading scores on Nov 18 and the game unlocking on Nov 19 is one day. One report puts the lift at around 6-7am ET on Nov 18, though the exact time is unconfirmed.

**Why this schedule?** A day-before embargo is how Rockstar handled its last two majors: GTA 5 reviews landed on September 16, 2013 (a day before release) and Red Dead Redemption 2 on October 25, 2018 (a day before its Oct 26 launch). It lets critics play a build as close to final as possible, at the cost of holding verdicts until the last moment.

**What it means for buyers**: read several reviews on Nov 18 and you still have a day to cancel a pre-order if it isn't for you — provided outlets and creators weren't also asked to hold until Nov 19.

**On the source**: Reddit's gaming-leaks community named NateTheHate its 2025 Leaker of the Year, and two of his earlier GTA 6 calls held up (the November date would stick; an August marketing push). Until Rockstar or an outlet holding code says otherwise, though, this stays a leaker's word.
