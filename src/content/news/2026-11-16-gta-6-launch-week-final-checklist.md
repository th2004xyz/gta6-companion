## 中文正文

倒计时进入最后 72 小时。如果你已经预购，下面是这份「一样都别漏」的发售周最终清单——按时间顺序排列，照着做就行。

**现在到 11 月 18 日（解锁前）**

- [ ] **确认预载已经下完**。212GB 不是小数目，发售周服务器压力大，别等到最后一天。到游戏库看进度条是否显示已完成。
- [ ] **再腾出一点空间给首日补丁**。Rockstar 尚未确认补丁是否存在、有多大，稳妥起见在 212GB 之外多留 20GB 左右。
- [ ] **主机保持联网 + 开启自动更新**。PS5 开自动下载并保持待机，Xbox 开自动更新并保持睡眠模式，让最终校验和补丁在解锁前跑完。
- [ ] **确认账号一致**。用下单时的同一个 PSN / Xbox 账号登录，并把主机设为主机／常用主机。
- [ ] **盒装版玩家：先兑换码**。11 月 12 日起零售店可提货，盒里是下载码卡，兑换后才会开始下那 212GB。

**11 月 18 日（发售前一天）**

- [ ] **留意评测解禁**。按第三方爆料，媒体评分约在北京时间当晚 20:00 前后出现（Rockstar 未官宣，详见本站评测解禁追踪稿）。想避开剧透的话，这天尽量少刷社交平台。
- [ ] **Metacritic 会更新**。目前那个 6.5 用户分只有 5 个评分、毫无意义；真正的媒体均分要到这天才有参考价值。

**11 月 19 日（发售日）**

- [ ] **记牢自己时区的解锁时刻**。两个平台都是各时区零点解锁，新西兰最早。PS5 按当地时间，Xbox 本次也按各时区零点（预载则按 GMT 统一）。
- [ ] **去官网领免费物料**。可打印的封面海报 + Leonida 地图数字下载，免费。
- [ ] **Xbox One 玩家走云游戏**。11 月 19 日起可通过 Xbox Cloud Gaming 串流（Series S 画质），这是 Xbox One 唯一通路。

**11 月 20 日（奖励截止）**

- [ ] **预购奖励最后期限**。Vintage Vice City Pack（'55 Vapid Stanier、Shore Court 车库、两套服装发型）发给 11 月 20 日前购买的玩家。数字预购另含 1 个月 GTA+（注意自动续费）。

**PC 玩家**：很遗憾，这份清单里没有你能做的事——官方 announced 的平台仍只有 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，PC 版没有任何日期、配置或容量信息，所谓「11 月同步登陆 PC」或「通过 Xbox 串流登陆 PC」均已被否认或未获证实。

按这份清单走完，11 月 19 日零点你唯一要做的就是——进罪恶都市。

## Overview

Final 72 hours. If you have pre-ordered, here is the "nothing to miss" launch-week checklist, in chronological order.

**Now through November 18 (before unlock)**

- [ ] **Confirm the preload is finished**. 212GB is no small matter and servers are under pressure this week — do not leave it to the last day. Check the progress bar in your library.
- [ ] **Free a little more room for a day-one patch**. Rockstar has not confirmed whether one exists or how big it is; leaving ~20GB beyond the 212GB is the safe move.
- [ ] **Keep the console online with auto-updates on**. PS5: auto-downloads on and Rest Mode. Xbox: automatic updates on and Sleep mode. Let final verification and any patch finish before unlock.
- [ ] **Match the purchasing account**. Sign in with the same PSN / Xbox account used to pre-order, and set the console as primary / home.
- [ ] **Boxed buyers: redeem the code first**. Available at retailers from November 12; the box holds a download-code card, and the 212GB download starts only after redemption.

**November 18 (day before launch)**

- [ ] **Watch for reviews**. Per third-party leaks, scores land around 20:00 Beijing time (Rockstar has not announced an embargo — see our review-embargo tracker). To dodge spoilers, consider muting social feeds.
- [ ] **Metacritic will update**. The current 6.5 user score is five meaningless ratings; the real critic average appears this day.

**November 19 (launch day)**

- [ ] **Know your time zone's unlock moment**. Both platforms unlock at local midnight, New Zealand first. PS5 uses local time; Xbox also uses local midnight for unlock this time (preload was GMT-wide).
- [ ] **Grab the free extras**. A printable cover poster and Leonida map are free digital downloads on the official site.
- [ ] **Xbox One: use cloud gaming**. Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming starts November 19 (Series S settings) — the only route for Xbox One.

**November 20 (bonus deadline)**

- [ ] **Pre-order bonus last call**. The Vintage Vice City Pack ('55 Vapid Stanier, Shore Court Garage, two outfits and hairstyles) goes to everyone who bought before November 20. Digital pre-orders also include one month of GTA+ (watch the auto-renewal).

**PC players**: there is nothing on this list for you — the officially announced platforms remain PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date, specs or file size. Claims of a November PC launch or Xbox-streaming-to-PC are either denied or unconfirmed.

Follow this list and the only thing left to do at midnight on November 19 is — step into Vice City.
