## 中文正文

发售前两周，Rockstar 在官网放出了 **GTA6 Launch Gallery**——一次性的大批量官方静态截图，可在 `rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery` 免费浏览与下载 4K 原图。

官方公告本身很克制，只说「距离发售正式只剩两周，来看看 Vice City 及更远地方」，**没有公布任何新玩法**。Rockstar 没给数量，媒体按官网文件名统计为 **约 39 张 3840×2160 原图**（部分站点记 38 张，统计口径略有出入）。

**这批图里有什么**

按主题铺开，信息量比「又一批美图」要大：

- **犯罪行动**：Jason 与 Lucia 蒙面抢 **Fleeca 银行**（Jason 抱着成捆钞票与冲锋枪，Lucia 持枪压场）；Lucia 从敞篷车探身，在 Gruppe Sechs 押运车、摩托与警车之间开火；Jason 在商场走廊骑摩托持消音冲锋枪，另两名骑手掩护；雨夜压弯的摩托追逐，身后是警车与多架直升机
- **日常与生活感**：两人在 **The Rusty Anchor** 酒吧与当地人共舞；坐在山顶皮卡上俯瞰 Vice City 日落天际线，前景有鹿在吃草；Jason 赤膊卧推；夜晚敞篷车里的蓝紫色霓虹
- **Leonida 各地**：**Grassrivers** 湿地的夜间鳄鱼与气垫船、缠着蛇的猎人营火；**Leonida Keys** 水下潜水员与狮子鱼、珊瑚礁；**Port Gellhorn** 的霓虹歌舞舞台；被警车与警艇围住的游艇与豪宅
- **人物特写**：Lucia 雨窗蓝光侧影、从车门后举枪；Jason 带战术护目镜的狗、近距离持枪对峙、加油站持油枪时被警察举枪逼近

**为什么值得单独记一笔**

这批静态图没有新增玩法公告，但它把《Edge》430 期与实机演示里那些口头描述**逐一对上了画面**：

| 截图 | 对应已确认系统 |
|---|---|
| 加油时警察逼近 | 燃油消耗、引擎磨损、偷车/热度系统 |
| 雨中摩托 + 警车 + 直升机 | 《Edge》披露的警方「热度」记录与持续追踪 |
| 卧推 | 健身房活动 |
| 潜水、狮子鱼、气垫船、鳄鱼 | Leonida 水下与 Grassrivers 湿地生态 |
| 酒吧共舞、山顶皮卡 | Jason 与 Lucia 的四级关系量表 |

需要保持的边界：**这是宣传截图，不是实机演示**。它不展示换装怎么操作、遭遇战怎么打，那只戴护目镜的狗能否随行、有没有玩法作用，静态图也给不出答案。

## Overview

Two weeks before launch, Rockstar opened the **GTA 6 Launch Gallery** on the official site — a single large drop of promotional stills, free to browse and download in 4K at `rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery`.

The announcement itself is restrained: the game is "officially only two weeks away", here is "a stunning look at Vice City and beyond", and **no new gameplay is announced**. Rockstar gives no count; outlets tallying the official filenames arrive at **roughly 39 images at 3840×2160** (some report 38, depending on what gets counted).

**What's in the set**

There is more here than another batch of pretty pictures:

- **Crime**: Jason and Lucia robbing a **Fleeca bank** in bandanas, Jason clutching bundles of notes and an SMG while Lucia covers the room; Lucia firing from a convertible amid a Gruppe Sechs truck, motorcycles and police; Jason aiming a suppressed SMG from a motorbike inside a mall with two riders covering him; a rain-soaked bike chase with a patrol car and several helicopters behind
- **Everyday life**: the pair dancing with locals at **The Rusty Anchor**; sitting on a pickup above the Vice City skyline at sunset with deer grazing nearby; a shirtless Jason at a bench press; a night-time convertible washed in blue and violet neon
- **Across Leonida**: night alligators and airboats in the **Grassrivers** wetlands, a hunter with a snake around his neck by a campfire; a diver above a lionfish and coral in the **Leonida Keys**; a neon-lit cabaret stage in **Port Gellhorn**; a yacht and mansion ringed by police boats and cruisers
- **Character close-ups**: Lucia in blue light behind a rain-speckled window, aiming a handgun across a car door; Jason with a dog in tactical goggles, a close-range standoff, and a fuel stop where armed police close in

**Why it is worth a page of its own**

No new systems were announced — but the stills **put visual evidence against descriptions** from Edge issue 430 and the earlier gameplay reveals:

| Screenshot | Confirmed system it matches |
|---|---|
| Armed police at a fuel stop | Fuel consumption, engine wear, the heat system |
| Rain bike chase with cars and helicopters | The police heat record and persistent tracking detailed by Edge |
| Bench press | Gym activities |
| Diver, lionfish, airboat, alligator | Leonida's underwater and Grassrivers ecology |
| Bar dancing, hilltop pickup | The four-point Jason / Lucia relationship scale |

The boundary to keep: **these are promotional stills, not a gameplay demo.** They do not show how outfit changes work or how the pictured encounters play out, and a still cannot tell us whether the goggle-wearing dog accompanies the protagonists or has any gameplay role.
