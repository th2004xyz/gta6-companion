## 中文正文

发售前两个月，Rockstar 把多年"靠社区自己领会"的 Mod 政策写成了一份正式规则：官网 Community Resources 下的 **Mod Guidelines** 页面更新（页面标注日期 9 月 10 日），适用范围是 Rockstar 全线作品，**GTA6 也在其中**。官方依旧不背书第三方 Mod——官方支持文档同时写明"允许改装不等于授权，也不意味着对第三方项目的认可、背书或许可"。

规则分成四块：Respect Our Games、不得侵犯 Rockstar 或第三方 IP、不得伤害创作者社区、不得从事未经授权的营利行为。要点如下：

**单机还能玩，但边界写死了。** PC 单机改装继续被容忍，前提是：严格非商业、尊重第三方知识产权、不干扰 GTA Online 或 Rockstar 在线服务、不得包含影响多人游戏的作弊菜单或工具。

**剧情扩展与跨作混搭被禁。** 规则明确禁止"扩展或修改 Rockstar 官方的故事线、剧情、任务、角色或其他既定设定"，也禁止"把一个 Rockstar 作品的地图、地点、角色、剧情、游戏资产或 IP 混进另一个作品"。换句话说，把自由城、罪恶城搬进新作的那种长青 Mod 传统，在条文上被正式划到线外；NS 版 GTA5 移植、Wii / GameCube / 3DS 项目乃至近期的 iPhone 版，也都因"不得让游戏在未获官方发行的主机或硬件上运行"一条而失去授权依据。

**金钱必须过官方生态。** 多人改装被限定在 **Rockstar 官方授权的 Creator Platform**（Rockstar 持有 Cfx.re，因此包含 FiveM 与 RedM），或另行取得 Rockstar 直接授权；Creator Platform 是"唯一获得许可的变现场所"，其余改装必须严格非商业。禁止"表现或宣扬以现实中武装组织为题材的冲突扮演或军事模拟"，也会直接影响以这类题材为核心的 RP 服务器。

需要注意：这份规则是面向 Rockstar 全线作品的通用条款，**Rockstar 并未单独说明哪些 GTA6 Mod 会被允许**，也没有区分单机与线上——GTA6 的 Mod 生态会成什么样，目前还 ⚠️ speculation（推测）。

## Overview

Two months before launch, Rockstar has turned years of inferred practice into a written rulebook. Its Community Resources page now carries an updated **Mod Guidelines** document dated September 10, applying across the studio's whole catalogue — GTA 6 included. Rockstar still does not endorse third-party mods; the support text notes that permission to mod "is not a license and does not imply endorsement, approval, or authorization" of an external project.

The page is organised into four sections: Respect Our Games, do not infringe Rockstar Games or third-party IP, do not harm the creator community, and do not engage in unauthorized or abusive monetization. The key takeaways:

**Single-player survives, with hard edges.** PC single-player mods remain tolerated provided they are strictly non-commercial, respect third-party IP, don't interfere with GTA Online or Rockstar's online services, and contain no cheat menus or tools aimed at multiplayer.

**No story mods, no crossovers.** The rules bar mods that "expand or modify Rockstar Games' official storylines, plots, missions, characters, or other elements of our established continuity", and forbid combining maps, locations, characters, storylines or assets "from one Rockstar Games title into another". That is the formal end of the long tradition of porting Liberty City or Vice City into a newer GTA. A companion rule — do not modify the games to run on consoles, hardware or platforms where Rockstar hasn't officially released them — also covers the native GTA V Switch port, the Wii / GameCube / 3DS builds and the recent iPhone effort.

**Money runs through Rockstar's ecosystem.** Multiplayer modding is "limited to Rockstar Games' official licensed Creator Platform or else as otherwise licensed directly by Rockstar Games". Rockstar owns Cfx.re and therefore FiveM and RedM, and the Creator Platform is described as the exclusive licensed home for monetisation; everything else must be strictly non-commercial. Mods that "engage in or promote conflict roleplay or military simulations depicting real-world armed organizations" are also prohibited.

Caveat: these are catalogue-wide rules. Rockstar has not said which GTA 6 mods will be allowed, and has not drawn a full line between single-player and online modding — so what GTA 6's mod scene actually looks like remains ⚠️ speculation.
