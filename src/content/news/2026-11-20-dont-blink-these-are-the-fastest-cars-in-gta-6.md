## Overview

The state of Leonida is home to some of the rarest and most expensive cars we've ever seen in the Grand Theft Auto franchise. GTA 6 features over a dozen supercars, which are always the pinnacle of the vehicle food chain. While you'll likely take any supercar during the early or mid-game, you'll eventually want to drive the fastest car in GTA 6. Driving the fastest car in Leonida will require you to acquire a legendary vehicle or wait until your character can afford one later on in the story. With GTA 6's new car-stealing restrictions , you can't just take a supercar off the streets and make it your own. However you decide to get a supercar, you'll still want to know which ones go the fastest. Fastest cars in GTA 6 Technically, the fastest overall cars in GTA 6 will depend on what custom mods you have installed on certain cars. For example, you can bring a car's max speed to 9.0 or higher (out of 10) by upgrading its engine, transmission, and more, but that's not the car's default speed. For this list, we'll just be covering which cars are the fastest by default in GTA 6. This means looking at the car's stock speed when it has no additional upgrades installed. This list is also just cars, as we'll go over the fastest overall vehicles, including motorcycles, planes, and bikes, in a future list. Here are the top five fastest cars we've come across in GTA 6, ranked by their top speeds: 5. Pfister Comet S2 Coming in as the fifth-fastest car in GTA 6 is the Pfister Comet S2, a slight surprise since this vehicle isn't classified as a supercar. However, it's plenty fast, with a stock speed rating of 7.7, beating out some truly impressive supercars that look like they would be faster. Without spoiling anything from the story, the Comet S2 is an option to acquire later on in GTA 6. 4. Piccia Furia You knew some Italian muscle was going to be on this list, and the Piccia Furia comes in as the fourth-fastest car in GTA 6. Featuring a stock speed rating of 7.8, the Furia is a fairly rare car in Leonida, as you don't often see it parked at the luxurious NINE1NINE strip club or along the streets of the Vice City beaches. 3. Pegassi Vitello Pegassi has long been one of the best car brands in the GTA franchise, so it's no surprise to see it on this list. In GTA 6, the Pegassi Vitello is the third-fastest car in the game, with a stock speed rating of 8.2. There's a significant gap between the Vitello and the two slower cars on this list in terms of speed, acceleration, and handling. 2. Progen Emerus GTA Online players will immediately recognize the Progen Emerus, and it's made a return in GTA 6. When it's fully maxed out with upgrades and mods, the Emerus can reach a speed rating of 9.0 and above. However, even with all of its stock parts, you'll have a speed rating of 8.3 with the Progren Emerus. 1. Truffade Thrax Topping the list of the fastest cars in GTA 6 is the Truffade Thrax. This supercar is significantly faster than anything else we've found so far in Leonida, featuring a stock speed rating of 8.7. That's higher than most cars can reach with fully upgraded parts, so you're going to be flying with the Thrax if you can manage to fully upgrade it. For some honorable mentions, we have the Pegassi Tempesta (7.6 speed rating) and the Granforma Zorrusso (7.5 speed rating). New cars that haven't been discovered yet in GTA 6 could change this list in the future, but these are the fastest cars known to be in the game right now.
