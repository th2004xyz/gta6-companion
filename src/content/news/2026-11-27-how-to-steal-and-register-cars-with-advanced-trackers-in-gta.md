## Overview

Grand Theft Auto 6 has a massive world, and you can be sure that there are thousands of vehicles driving across the countryside at any given point in time. While the vast majority can be hijacked without worries--well, aside from cops that might see you--a select few have anti-theft devices that make them an unbearable hassle. Thankfully, there are ways to steal vehicles with Advanced Trackers. Advanced Trackers: How exactly do they work? The Advanced Trackers in GTA 6 can be seen if you scan most high-end vehicles like sports cars and luxury cars. Basically, if it looks expensive and sells for a high price, you can bet that it has an Advanced Tracker. Here’s how the device works: Stolen vehicles with a Standard Tracker ping their location only if (a) they’ve significantly slowed down or stopped, and (b) a police car is nearby. Advanced Trackers, meanwhile, ping their location if you go below a certain speed. You essentially need to put your pedal to the metal when driving them. Otherwise, the police would know your exact location. Given the risk, most fence NPCs in the game won’t even accept stolen vehicles with Advanced Trackers. Well, except for one fella: Randy. The legit method: Do missions for Randy Randy is the one NPC in the game that accepts stolen cars with Advanced Trackers, but there are some caveats: You meet Randy while tackling main missions in chapter 3. He’ll give you a task to steal a Zorrusso, which also nets you the Key Cloner, a tool that allows you to hijack keyless cars. At this point, Randy and various Pay ‘n’ Spray shops will start accepting stolen vehicles with Standard Trackers. He’ll also tell you that he has a “wishlist” of sorts: A dozen or so high-end cars that you need to boost and bring to his garage. Note that some targets do have Advanced Trackers. He’ll only accept these as long as they’re part of his wishlist. Since he’ll keep them, you won’t be able to own/register them at all. Again, you need to drive fast to prevent the tracker from triggering. But you also have to be careful since the sell value of the car will decrease depending on the damage. Now, let’s say you’ve completed all of Randy’s requests. Come chapter 4, Randy will give you one more mission. This time, you need to boost a Mamba GT. After that ordeal, you’ll be able to remove Advanced Trackers via Pay ‘n’ Spray shops in Leonida. Yes, you’ll finally be able to register/own all those high-end vehicles you keep seeing in Vice City. The exploit method: Have your partner drive it There’s also another method if you wish to steal cars with Advanced Trackers in GTA 6. It’s an exploit that involves fiddling around with partner controls. Here’s how to do it: Have your partner tag along while you’re exploring. Locate a car with an Advanced Tracker. Don’t do anything with it yet. Look for a car near it, one that only has a Standard Tracker or no tracker at all. Make sure you unlock that vehicle. Next, unlock the door of the car with the Advanced Tracker… and immediately exit it. Open the partner interface by holding L2/LT. Then, select “You Drive.” This will cause your partner to enter the last vehicle that you interacted with--i.e. the one with the Advanced Tracker. As for your own character, simply hop on to the other car that you jacked. Then, open the partner interface again and select “Follow.” (If your partner is driving the vehicle, they won’t trigger the tracker’s system.) Drive your lowly vehicle to Randy’s shop, and watch as your partner follows behind you at a leisurely pace. When you’re in front of Randy’s garage, simply switch to your partner and deliver the vehicle. That’s it. No police chases, no need to drive over the speed limit, and no panicking due to the tracker’s alarm potentially going off.
