## Overview

Between September 25 and 29, Game Informer announced GTA 6 as the cover story of issue 382; the digital edition went live on **September 29** with a batch of **12 brand-new official screenshots**. It is widely read as the heaviest media preview before launch — Rockstar chose a trusted games magazine for a "controlled" disclosure rather than dropping another trailer.
