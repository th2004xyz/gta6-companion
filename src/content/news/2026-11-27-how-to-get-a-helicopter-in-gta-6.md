You’ll spend so many hours driving across the state of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto 6 , that your feet will start to ache in the real world. But there are still other modes of travel that you can try out. And, yes, you can take to the skies as well on board a helicopter. In This Article Helicopter spawns in Grand Theft Auto 6 Early-game option: Buckingham Maverick Military helicopters in GTA 6 Amphibious helicopters Rooftop helipads in Vice City Helicopter spawns in Grand Theft Auto 6 As of the time of this writing, there’s no clear way to permanently own a helicopter in GTA 6. These vehicles can neither be saved near your safehouse, nor can they be delivered to wherever you are. That said, there are several areas where helicopters frequently spawn. For the most part, you should look at helipad markings on the map--i.e. “H” symbol on rooftops and facilities. We’ve got a few examples below. Early-game option: Buckingham Maverick The Buckingham Maverick is a great early-game pick if you just want to explore and reveal more of the map. To find it, simply travel along the main highway from Jason’s home and keep going southwest until you reach Southwind. To the right, you’ll see MHH Marque Hoppers, which has a Maverick conveniently parked on the pier. Military helicopters in GTA 6 While you’re in the area, you might be interested in getting a military helicopter in GTA 6. There are several options like the machine-gunning Buzzard, sleek Valkyrie, and beefy Cargobob. All of these are in the Mile Zero Base just a short distance away from Southwind. You must be careful, though, because this is a military base that’s on high alert. If you get spotted within its boundaries, you’ll be warned by police and soldiers. Dawdle for too long and you’ll be reported for committing a crime, earning an instant 4-star Wanted Level for good measure. Having countless troops, including those in armored vehicles, shooting at you is not a good way to start the day. Amphibious helicopters It’s also a good idea to find amphibious helicopters like the Nagasaki Sea Sparrow. One is near the speedway in the southwestern part of Hamlet, and the other is further west at the halfway point between the town and Watson Bay. (It’s also where you obtain the Dominator Buggy if you own the Ultimate Edition.) As the term implies, these types of choppers can take off/land on water, making them integral for thorough exploration of the seas surrounding Leonida. Rooftop helipads in Vice City There are many helicopters flying all over Vice City, and you can grab one of them by searching for the helipad icons on your map. Buildings with helipads on the roof tend to have interiors that you can enter, as well as elevators that can take you to the top floor. From there, you just need to hijack the vehicle and zip away to safety. These are just some of the GTA 6 helicopters that you can acquire. Keep returning to these locations if you want them to respawn. As for other vehicles that you can steal, don’t forget to check our articles about tanks , planes , and boats .
