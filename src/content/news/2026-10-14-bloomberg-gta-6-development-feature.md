## 中文正文

Bloomberg 记者 Jason Schreier 在 10 月中旬获 Rockstar 罕见放行，进入 Rockstar North 工作室采访多位核心成员，写出一篇万字级别的 GTA6 开发回顾。这是发售前少数几次真正的「幕后」视角。

**NPC 单开一座工作室。** 当 Rockstar 评估要写出并动捕多少 NPC 才能撑起他们想要的世界密度时，发现自己根本没有足够人手。联合工作室负责人 Rob Nelson 形容当时的产能预估「吓人」。解决方案是：在洛杉矶专门新设一间工作室，只招募编剧、制作人与导演，唯一任务就是把 NPC「做活」。这解释了为何本作 NPC 据称拥有超过 60 万套独特动画，并对玩家行为做出多种反应。

**官方确认「Project Americas」。** 此前业界一直传言 GTA6 起源于一个横跨南北美洲、多主角的「Project Americas」企划。本文中 Rockstar 首次正式确认了该起源，也解释了为何项目野心过大、不得不数次推迟。

背景：GTA6 官方平台仍为 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，11 月 19 日发售，PC 版尚未公布（见 PC 版发售日全知道）。

## Overview

Bloomberg's Jason Schreier secured rare access to Rockstar North in mid-October for a long-form GTA 6 development retrospective — one of the few genuine behind-the-scenes looks before launch.

**A whole studio for NPCs.** When Rockstar assessed how many NPCs it would need to write and animate to hit its world-density goals, it realized it simply lacked the workforce. Co-studio head Rob Nelson called the projections "horrifying." The fix: a dedicated new Los Angeles studio hiring only writers, producers and directors, with one job — bringing NPCs to life. That helps explain reports of 600,000+ unique NPC animations that react to what the player does.

**"Project Americas" officially confirmed.** Long-rumored as the game's origin — a sprawling, multi-protagonist plan spanning North and South America — "Project Americas" is now confirmed by Rockstar in this feature, which also explains why the over-ambitious scope forced several delays.

Context: GTA 6's official platforms remain PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, launching November 19; no PC version is announced (see GTA 6 PC release date: everything we know).
