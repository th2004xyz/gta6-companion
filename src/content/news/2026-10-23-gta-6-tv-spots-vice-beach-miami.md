## 中文正文

发售前一个月，GTA6 的营销已经从线上溢出到街头。本周几项进展：

**上市预告片登上了电视。** 据 Polygon，10 月 15 日的上市预告出现了两个电视版本，与原版相比有细微改动，其中包含更多 Raul 跳舞的镜头。电视投放是最终营销轰炸的标准动作，说明 R 星已经进入冲刺阶段。

**游戏里的快餐店开进了现实球馆。** 据 GameSpot 与 Radio Times，Burger Shot 与 Cluckin' Bell 的实体餐摊已经在迈阿密 Kaseya Center 内开始供餐——这两个品牌是 GTA 系列里的虚构快餐连锁，如今以实体形式出现在真实场馆中。同一座球馆将在 11 月 18 日变身「罪恶都市之夜」的现场。

**Vice Beach 的合作还有变数。** 据 Gameranx，迈阿密海滩的 Vice Beach 项目涉及向城市支付约 30 万美元、向特许经营商 Boucher Brothers 支付约 400 万美元；截至 10 月 15 日相关许可尚未获批，若 11 月 1 日前仍未获批，该活动可能被取消。这是一条**尚未落地的商业安排**，不是既成事实。

**整座城市在被改造。** VICE 与 VGC 追踪到，迈阿密-戴德县一名专员称 R 星在 GTA6 营销上的投入至少 1.5 亿美元。从屋顶的「Welcome to Vice City」灯牌、挂 Leonida 车牌的真车队在市区巡游，到热火球衣与 Court Culture 联名服饰，现实与虚构的边界正在被系统性抹平。

## Overview

A month before launch, GTA 6's marketing has spilled from feeds onto the street. This week's developments:

**The launch trailer hit TV.** Per Polygon, two TV versions of the Oct 15 trailer aired with small changes from the original, including more of Raul dancing. TV placement is the standard closing move, which says Rockstar is in its sprint phase.

**The game's fast-food chains are serving in a real arena.** Per GameSpot and Radio Times, physical Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands are now serving food inside Miami's Kaseya Center — the fictional franchises turning up as real concessions in the building that hosts "A Night in Vice City" on Nov 18.

**The Vice Beach deal isn't settled.** Per Gameranx, the Miami Beach arrangement involves roughly $300,000 to the city and $4 million to concessionaire Boucher Brothers; permits had not been approved as of October 15, and the activation could be cancelled if approval doesn't arrive by November 1. That is a **pending commercial arrangement, not a done deal**.

**The city itself is being converted.** VICE and VGC track the wider campaign, with a Miami-Dade commissioner putting Rockstar's GTA 6 marketing spend at a minimum of $150 million. Between the "Welcome to Vice City" roof signage, a convoy of real cars wearing Leonida plates cruising the city, and Heat jerseys plus Court Culture apparel, the line between the real Miami and the fictional one is being erased on purpose.
