## Overview

GTA 6 may still very much be a Grand Theft Auto game, but some fundamental changes--a stronger focus on realism, money being scarce --do a lot to shake things up. Driving might not feel totally different, but the decision-making behind choosing what to drive and how reckless to be on the road give that portion of the game a much different feel. But should you prefer not to drive yourself, you do have a choice, and it doesn't involve taking taxis or relying on public transportation. It can be easy to miss, due to how it's accessed, but there is an auto-drive option you can enable so your current character drives themselves. Here's how to enable it: Hold Down on the D-Pad while in a vehicle Press Options/Start to enable Cinematic Mode Hold Circle/B to turn on auto-drive By default, your character will seemingly just drive themselves around aimlessly. However, if you set a destination on the map, they'll instead drive there. That's useful not just on the roads, but also with other vehicle types. For instance, you can hop on a boat and mark the location for a diving mission, and spare yourself the oft-uneventful process of manually driving to it. Cinematic Mode gives you nice camera angles from which to enjoy the visuals, and not having to pay attention to where you're driving means you can spend more time gawking at all of the details littered throughout the world. Or you can just pretend you're the passenger and enjoy playing with the radio, without having to spend exorbitant sums on a taxi ride. Auto-drive can be canceled at any time, so you're not locked into a full trip if something catches your attention along the way. One thing you can't do is play around on your phone while in auto-drive. To do that, you'll need to be manually driving, which means getting pulled into a first-person perspective that makes the prospect even more precarious. While in auto-drive, you can adjust between three speed settings depending on the urgency with which you want Jason or Lucia to drive. That makes this more of a viable way of getting around quickly, though don't expect them to pull off the same extreme maneuvers you might use to speed around. Keep in mind, there is an extensive public transportation system available as well (if only we could all be so lucky), though that's less useful if you're insistent upon arriving at a very particular spot.
