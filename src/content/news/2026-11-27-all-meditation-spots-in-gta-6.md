## Overview

Sometimes, you just want to step away and relax from all the hustle and bustle in Grand Theft Auto 6 . By “hustle,” we mean ransacking the vaults of banks, and by “bustle,” we mean running over hundreds of people with trucks. So, why not head to the wilderness in search of Meditation Spots? You might even find inner peace while you’re at it. In This Article How Meditation Spots work in Grand Theft Auto 6 Meditation Spots in Mariana County Meditation Spot #1: Intro to Meditation Meditation Spot #2: Smooth Sand Meditation Meditation Spots in Kelly County Meditation Spot #3: Swamp Meditation Meditation Spot #4: Worn Stone Meditation Meditation Spot #5: Red Rocks Meditation How Meditation Spots work in Grand Theft Auto 6 Meditation Spots are a type of activity that you can do when you’re not tackling missions. You simply need to go to specific areas, sit down, and then press the corresponding button prompts to complete breathing exercises. As an aside, even if Jason and Lucia are together during a run, they have to do their meditation exercises separately. Try to be patient since each process can take a while. Meditation Spots in Mariana County There are a couple of GTA 6 Meditation Spots in Mariana County. They’re on opposite ends of the zone and one of them is technically missable. Meditation Spot #1: Intro to Meditation This Meditation Spot is actually the porch/deck of Jason’s house in Key Lento. To unlock it, play through chapter 1 normally until Lucia notices that Jason seems stressed out. She’ll eventually encourage him to meditate on the porch, so follow her to start the tutorial. Take note that this sequence is missable if you leave the house instead of listening to Lucia’s prompt. Be sure to finish this task prior to undertaking the important job/mission that you receive from Mattia. Meditation Spot #2: Smooth Sand Meditation You’ll find this Meditation Spot in the northern tip of Belpoint, an island west of Capri. The point-of-interest consists of neatly arranged stones on a sandbar. Meditation Spots in Kelly County You’ll head to Kelly County for the campaign’s second chapter. The Meditation Spots in the zone are actually far from areas that you tend to go to for missions, so be ready for a bit of a trek. Meditation Spot #3: Swamp Meditation Found in the swamplands in the southeastern part of Kelly County. You visit this area if you’re doing Moses’ mission to find a BF Bandito. Meditation Spot #4: Worn Stone Meditation This GTA 6 Meditation Spot is at the back of the dirt path in Camp Moonflower, which counts as a WAiNK Curiosity . It’s behind a parked hippie van. Meditation Spot #5: Red Rocks Meditation To reach this area, travel along Moonstone Highway leading northeast out of Port Gellhorn. You should see the map label for Manifest Canyon and, once you’re past that, look for a sloping path up the hill. You’re going to feel lost as you explore the cities, beachfronts, and seedy underbelly of Leonida.
