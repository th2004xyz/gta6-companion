## 中文正文

距离《GTA6》11 月 19 日发售还有两个月，Rockstar 先放出了一张实体唱片：9 月 17 日，官方正式公布 **《Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album》**，与 Atlantic Records 合作，收录 34 首为这款游戏原创录制的曲目，发售日与游戏本体同为 11 月 19 日。

官方描述这张专辑要"抓住罪恶都市与列奥尼达州那种带电般的能量"，阵容横跨嘻哈、电子、摇滚、乡村与拉丁——首批公开的六首先行单曲已经可以在主流流媒体试听：

- Yung Lean – That's It (feat. Future & Metro Boomin)
- Travis Scott – RHYNO（制作人 Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo，前 Daft Punk 成员）
- CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. & Etienne de Crécy – Sexy Magic
- Morgan Wallen – Last Thing You Need
- Rauw Alejandro – Macacoa 2000
- Keith Richards – Bright Lights, Big City

实体版分三档，仅在专辑官网限定发售：CD **19.98 美元**、标准黑胶 **49.98 美元**、限量充液黑胶 **124.98 美元**（两张透明洋红胶碟内灌蓝色液体，配 Vice City 霓虹风格的新绘制封面）。另外 Blood Records、Best Buy、Walmart、Amazon、Target、Barnes & Noble 等零售商也会上架不同配色版本。流媒体预存已开放。

需要注意的是，这张专辑是围绕 GTA6 世界做的独立音乐企划，**不等于游戏内电台曲库全表**—— Rockstar 表示后续还会公布更多关于动态配乐与"下一代电台"的信息。

## Overview

With two months to go before Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, Rockstar has revealed Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album — a companion soundtrack release made with Atlantic Records. The album collects 34 original tracks recorded for the game and arrives the same day as GTA VI itself on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Rockstar describes the record as capturing "the electric energy of Vice City and Leonida" across a genre-defying roster. The first six singles are available to stream today:

- Yung Lean – That's It (feat. Future & Metro Boomin)
- Travis Scott – RHYNO (prod. by Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo)
- CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. & Etienne de Crécy – Sexy Magic
- Morgan Wallen – Last Thing You Need
- Rauw Alejandro – Macacoa 2000
- Keith Richards – Bright Lights, Big City

Three physical editions are available exclusively through the album's official site: CD at $19.98, a standard vinyl at $49.98, and a limited liquid-filled vinyl at $124.98 — two transparent magenta discs filled with blue liquid, with new artwork echoing the game's neon Vice City look. Additional colour variants will be sold through retailers including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target and Urban Outfitters. Pre-saves on streaming platforms are live now.

One caveat: the album is a standalone musical project built around GTA VI's world, not necessarily the full list of songs you'll hear on in-game radio. Rockstar says more details on the dynamic score and the next evolution of in-game radio are still to come.
