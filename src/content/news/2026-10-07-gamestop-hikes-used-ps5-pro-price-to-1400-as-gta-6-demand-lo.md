## 中文正文

在 GTA6 发售临近之际，美国零售商 **GameStop** 将二手 PS5 Pro 的售价上调 **55%**，达到约 **1400 美元**（新品官方定价为 699 美元）。

这反映出主机稀缺与发售前抢购热潮——此前 8 月就有 PS5 Pro 在 GTA6 热度高涨中售罄的报道。对想赶在 **11 月 19 日**首发体验 GTA6 的玩家，二手市场正在被情绪推高。

提醒：二手涨价不等于官方调价，预购仍是更稳妥的入手方式。

## Overview

Ahead of GTA 6's release, US retailer GameStop has raised the price of a pre-owned PS5 Pro by 55% to around $1,400 (new MSRP is $699). It reflects console scarcity and the pre-launch buying frenzy — back in August, new PS5 Pros sold out amid GTA 6 fever. For players wanting to experience GTA 6 at launch on November 19, the second-hand market is being pushed up by emotion. A reminder: second-hand markups aren't official price changes; pre-ordering remains the safer route.
