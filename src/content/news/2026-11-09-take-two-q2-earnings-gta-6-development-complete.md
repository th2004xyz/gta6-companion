## 中文正文

11 月 9 日，Take-Two 召开 **2027 财年第二季度**（2026 年 7–9 月）财报电话会。这个季度本身不含任何 GTA6 销量，但它是发售前最后一个官方信息窗口，Zelnick 给的东西比财务数字更值得注意。

**三条硬表态**

1. **开发完成**。问答环节被问到开发进度时，Zelnick 表示 **Rockstar Games 已完成 GTA6 的开发，营销活动也已完全就绪**，现阶段公司的任务是在技术层面以极好的状态把游戏交到用户手中。
2. **不会再延期**。在同期 IGN 的采访中被直接问及延期风险，他的回答是一句干脆的：**「它不会再延期了。」**
3. **预购远超预期**。原话是「《GTA6》预购走势远高于我们的初始预期，很快数据本身就会说明一切」。他同时留了余地：「我们绝不会在成功真正到来之前就宣称自己已经成功了。」

**财务指引**

| 指标 | 数值 | 对比 |
|---|---|---|
| FY2027 全年净预订额 | **80–82 亿美元**（维持） | 上一财年 67.2 亿 |
| Q3（10–12 月）净预订额 | **33.5–35 亿美元** | 去年同期 17.6 亿，接近翻倍 |
| Q3 营业费用 | 预计增长约 **21%** | 主因 GTA6 营销 |
| 持续性消费者支出 | 预计增长 **12%** | 主要由终极版拉动 |

多数预购玩家选择的是 **99.99 美元终极版**（标准版 79.99 美元），20 美元差价被计入「持续性消费者支出」，是收入预期上修的重要组成部分。CFO Lainie Goldstein 给出上述 Q3 区间，总裁 Karl Statoff 则称这次发售将是「全球娱乐产业的标志性时刻」。

**营销成绩单**（财报中一并披露）

- Netflix 独家首播的 26 分钟《An Extended Look》，**前四天触达 3100 万观众**，成为当周该平台收视冠军
- 官方原声带已有 **3 首曲目进入 Billboard Top 10**
- 与 NBA 合作推出 **「A Night in Vice City」**：11 月 18 日迈阿密热火以「Vice City Heat」之名迎战密尔沃基雄鹿，发售前一天

**副作用也在财报里**

9 月发售的 **NBA 2K27 售出 400 万份，未达内部预期**（仍是该系列史上第二大首发）。Zelnick 解释，部分玩家为等 GTA6 推迟了购买，而竞品为避开 GTA6 扎堆在 9 月发售加剧了档期竞争，他自嘲：「如果要有人吃掉你的午餐，我宁愿那个吃的人是我们。」

关于发售当天的下载压力，Zelnick 说公司「非常关注，会尽全力提供顺畅的下载体验」，财务执行副总裁 Hannah Sage 补了一句：**预载次日开始，正是为了尽量减小冲击**。

**两个空白**

- **没有官方预购数字**。流传的 645 万份 / 6.3 亿美元仍是 Sensor Tower 等第三方估算，本次财报未公布任何具体份数。
- **没有 PC 版日期**。财报全程未提 PC，此前所有 PC 时间点都是媒体推测。

Zelnick 还留下一个悬念：Rockstar 将专注于「接下来要做的事」，并在适当时机公布。报道把这句话原样留着——可能指后续更新、尚未公开的线上内容，也可能指别的，目前没有任何进一步说明。

## Overview

On November 9, Take-Two held its **fiscal Q2 2027** earnings call, covering July through September 2026. The quarter contains no GTA 6 sales, but as the last official window before launch, what Strauss Zelnick said matters more than the balance sheet.

**Three firm statements**

1. **Development is complete.** Asked in the Q&A about progress, Zelnick said **Rockstar Games has finished developing GTA 6 and the marketing campaign is fully ready**, with the company's remaining task being to deliver the game in excellent technical shape.
2. **No more delays.** Asked directly by IGN about delay risk, he answered plainly: **"It's not going to be delayed."**
3. **Pre-orders far above forecast.** His words: GTA 6 pre-orders are tracking "well above our initial expectations" and "the numbers will speak for themselves soon". He kept a caveat: "We would never declare success before it actually arrives."

**The guidance**

| Metric | Figure | Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| FY2027 full-year net bookings | **8.0–8.2 billion dollars** (reaffirmed) | 6.72 billion last fiscal year |
| Q3 (Oct–Dec) net bookings | **3.35–3.5 billion dollars** | 1.76 billion a year ago, nearly double |
| Q3 operating expenses | Up about **21%** | Driven mainly by GTA 6 marketing |
| Recurrent consumer spending | Up **12%** | Largely the Ultimate Edition |

Most pre-orders are for the **99.99 dollar Ultimate Edition** against 79.99 for Standard; the 20 dollar gap counts as recurrent consumer spending and is a meaningful part of the raised outlook. CFO Lainie Goldstein gave the Q3 range, while president Karl Statoff called the launch a landmark moment for the global entertainment industry.

**Marketing scorecard, also in the results**

- The 26-minute **An Extended Look**, premiered exclusively on Netflix, reached **31 million consumers in its first four days** and was the platform's most-watched title of the week
- **Three tracks** from the official soundtrack debuted in the **Billboard Top 10**
- An NBA partnership, **"A Night in Vice City"**: on November 18 the Miami Heat play the Milwaukee Bucks as the "Vice City Heat", one day before launch

**The side effects showed up too**

September's **NBA 2K27 sold 4 million copies, below internal expectations** — still the franchise's second-biggest launch. Zelnick attributed it partly to players holding off for GTA 6 and partly to competitors crowding September to avoid it, quipping that if someone has to eat your lunch, he would rather it be Take-Two.

On launch-day download strain, Zelnick said the company is "very focused" on a smooth experience; EVP and chief accounting officer Hannah Sage added that **preload begins the next day specifically to soften the impact**.

**Two gaps**

- **No official pre-order number.** The 6.45 million copies and 630 million dollars in circulation remain third-party estimates from Sensor Tower and others; the call published no unit figure.
- **No PC date.** PC was not mentioned at all; every circulating PC timeline remains press speculation.

Zelnick also left one thread hanging: Rockstar will focus on "what comes next" and announce it in due course. Reporting leaves it unexplained — it could mean post-launch updates, unannounced online content, or something else entirely.
