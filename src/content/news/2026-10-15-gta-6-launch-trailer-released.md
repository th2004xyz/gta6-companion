## 中文正文

Rockstar 在 10 月 15 日上午（太平洋时间 6:00）毫无征兆地放出了《GTA6》最终上市预告片。这是自 2025 年 5 月第二支预告以来首支全新预告，也是发售前最后一块官方营销拼图——距离 11 月 19 日发售仅剩五周。

预告片时长约 90 秒，Rockstar 明确表示「全程基于 PlayStation 5 实机录制」，并非 Pro 机型。画面以杰森（Jason）与露西娅（Lucia）的双主角关系为主线，穿插银行抢劫、警匪追逐、街头交火、直升机坠毁、跃入泳池等桥段。

几个被玩家热议的细节：

- **露西娅的脚踝电子监控脚环**在淋浴镜头中出现，呼应此前封面与剧情暗示的「假释」背景；Rockstar 未做官方解释，目前属画面解读而非剧情定论。
- **杰森的军旅过往**首次在预告中披露——开场他提到「离开军队时并不体面」，为角色动机补上关键一笔。
- 配乐是电子组合 **Justice** 的新单曲《Flower in a Hurricane》。多家媒体称客串阵容包括 Tame Impala、SZA 与 King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard，并由 Justice 负责本作原创配乐。这些客串信息出现在媒体通稿而非 Newswire 正文，暂以「据报道」看待，待 Rockstar 正式确认。

发售信息保持不变：11 月 19 日登陆 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，支持官方中文。数字版预购用户可在 11 月 12 日当地零点开启预下载；实体版为「盒装 + 下载码」形式，不含光盘，同样需要完整下载。所有在 11 月 20 日前预购的玩家可获赠「罪恶都市怀旧包」（Vintage Vice City Pack）。

背景：官方平台仍为 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，PC 版发售时间未公布（见 PC 版发售日全知道）。工作室负责人 Rob Nelson 本周对《Famitsu》表示游戏将按时发售，本支预告的 Newswire 文案也重申了 11 月 19 日档期。

## Overview

On October 15 at 6:00 a.m. Pacific, Rockstar published the Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer with zero advance notice. It is the first new trailer since Trailer 2 in May 2025 and the final marketing beat before the November 19 launch — five weeks out.

The cut runs about 90 seconds, and Rockstar states plainly that "this trailer was captured entirely in-game from a PlayStation 5" — a standard console, not the Pro. The montage centers on the partnership between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, cutting between bank heists, police chases, street firefights, a helicopter crash, and the two leaping into a pool.

Details driving the discussion:

- Lucia wears a monitoring anklet in an intimate shower scene, reinforcing the parole backstory hinted at by the cover art. Rockstar has not explained it, so treat it as a reading of the footage rather than a plot fact.
- Jason's military past is revealed for the first time — the opening voiceover notes he "left the army but not on the best of terms," filling in a key piece of his motivation.
- The soundtrack is a new Justice single, "Flower in a Hurricane." Reports credit guest features from Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, with Justice composing the game's original score. Those feature credits appear in press coverage rather than the Newswire text, so we list them as reported until Rockstar confirms.

The release line is unchanged: November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with official Chinese-language support. Digital pre-orders can preload at local midnight on November 12; the physical edition ships as a box with a download code rather than a disc, requiring the same download. Every pre-order placed before November 20 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Context: the official platforms remain PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC release date is announced (see GTA 6 PC release date: everything we know). Co-studio head Rob Nelson told Famitsu this week the game is shipping on time, and the trailer's Newswire copy reaffirms the November 19 date.
