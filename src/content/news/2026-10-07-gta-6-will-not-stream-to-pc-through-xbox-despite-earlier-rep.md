## 中文正文

此前《The Verge》援引微软全员大会消息称，Xbox **获得了 GTA6 独占云游戏串流权**，引发 PC 玩家对其能否在发售日借 Xbox Cloud Gaming 畅玩 GTA6 的热议。该说法源自 Xbox CEO Asha Sharma 对员工称，微软正围绕 GTA6 准备"其他平台方都做不到的事"。

随后 **Xbox 首席战略官 Matthew Ball 正式否认**。他的完整声明是："GTA6 与 Xbox 的热情令人欣喜，但也导致了误报。我们期待 11 月 19 日与 Xbox 一同发售 GTA6，不过游戏不会以独占形式、也不会串流到 PC。"

换言之，PC 玩家**不应**指望在 11 月 19 日打开 Xbox Cloud Gaming 就能在 Vice City 闲逛——没有主机依然玩不到。Xbox 云游戏本身 11 月也有改动（引入按量付费等），但与 GTA6 无关。

背景：GTA6 官方平台仍为 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，PC 版尚未公布（参见 PC 版发售日追踪）。

## Overview

A Verge report citing an Xbox all-hands meeting claimed Microsoft had secured exclusive cloud-streaming rights to GTA 6 — sparked by CEO Asha Sharma telling staff Xbox was preparing to do "something no other platform holder is doing" around GTA 6. Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball has now denied it. His full statement: "While the excitement for GTA 6 and Xbox is great to see, it has also led to some misreporting. We are looking forward to launching GTA 6 on Xbox November 19, though the game will not be streaming exclusively or to PC." So PC players shouldn't expect to fire up Xbox Cloud Gaming on November 19 and roam Vice City without a console. (Xbox Cloud Gaming itself gets November changes like pay-as-you-go, unrelated to GTA 6.) GTA 6's official platforms remain PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC version is announced.
