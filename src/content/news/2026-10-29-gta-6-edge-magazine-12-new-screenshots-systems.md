## 中文正文

发售前整整三周，Rockstar 做了一件过去很少做的事：让《Edge》杂志记者 Simon Parkin 走进爱丁堡的 **Rockstar North**，然后由联合工作室主管兼开发主管 **Rob Nelson** 拿起手柄，**在没有脚本、没有公关念稿的情况下连续玩了 90 分钟**。成文即《Edge》430 期 16 页封面专题，10 月 29 日上架，全文可在 GamesRadar+ 免费阅读。

**12 张全新截图**

专题共收录 14 张 Rockstar 提供的图片，其中 12 张为首次公开。画面横跨 Vice City 的密集海滨天际线与霓虹夜景、小镇街道、拖车公园、工业外围与 Leonida 的乡野沼泽；其中一张深入水下，展示一头**锤头鲨游过两侧长满珊瑚的通道**——Rockstar 早就确认 GTA6 有潜水内容，但此前极少放出水下画面。

人物侧则包括 VCPD 警官 **Méndez** 的特写（此前仅在实机演示的发布会上露过面）、Lucia 的白背心金链造型、Jason 的长发造型、24/7 便利店抢劫、摩托车逃亡、飞车党列队骑行与泥地赛。

**警察会记住你什么**

最关键的系统变化，是执法部门掌握的信息变多了。据《Edge》，警方会记录**外貌描述、是否独行或有同伙、穿着、武器，以及监控摄像头是否拍到过你**，这些信息以图标形式出现在屏幕上方。

对应的逃脱逻辑也随之改变：不再只是等通缉星星冷却，而是**换掉衣服、丢掉武器、与同伴分头行动**，让自己越来越难被认出来。偷车同样变危险——有的司机慌张逃跑，有的持械反击，较新的高档车可能装有会暴露你位置的追踪器，车主也可能在事后报警。

**犯罪档案与四级关系**

独立于警察系统之外，游戏会追踪一份**犯罪档案（Criminal Profile）**，评级从 **Professional** 到 **Irredeemable**，Rockstar 确认玩家的行为会影响 Jason 与 Lucia 的故事走向。部分剧情任务要求两人先筹钱，手段包括抢劫加油站、珠宝店行窃，以及为销赃者偷指定车辆。

Jason 与 Lucia 的关系则是一条**四级量表**，通过共同活动（一起逛街、去健身房、探索 Leonida）逐步提升，玩家对亲密程度有一定影响力。Rockstar **拒绝回应两人能否结婚**，这个回避立刻引发了玩家猜测。

**世界密度与细节**

其他已被确认的系统：

- 武器存放在**汽车后备箱**而非菜单里；背在背上的霰弹枪比拿在手里的更不容易引起惊慌
- **Scooter Bros** 手机服务可以把你拥有的车送到身边；每辆车都会消耗燃油，引擎会随时间磨损
- Leonida 有**可搭乘的公交车与火车**
- **更长的昼夜循环**；局部天气（雾、风、季节）会影响道路、水面、飞行器与行人行为
- NPC 反应按**攻击性等性格特质**分化，有的掉头就跑，有的主动找麻烦；Rockstar 为此在洛杉矶组建了专门团队（编剧、导演、制片），全作约 **60 万条独特动画**
- Garbut 提到按键出汗系统（Jason 与 Lucia 会按跳舞强度出汗），以及一套最初只为单场戏做的「厌恶反应」——他走进一间陌生屋子，墙上挂着狗的照片，隔壁房间却被狗折腾得一团糟，Jason 自己做了个鬼脸

关于规模，Garbut 给出的新说法是**室内空间比前作「多一个数量级」**。需要说明的是：GTA5 约 70–80 个可进入室内（该数字由 ComicBook 统计、非 Rockstar 公布），据此推算约 700–800 个属于**媒体推算，不是官方数字**。

**哪些其实是旧闻**：地图约为 GTA5 两倍、陆地面积接近 GTA4 + GTA5 + 荒野大镖客 2 之和，以及 60 万条动画，这三项都来自 8 月底《纽约时报》探访后的报道，本次属于复述。真正的新增信息是上述系统细节与 12 张截图。

Nelson 对发售日的态度很明确：游戏**「已经准备好了」**。

## Overview

Three weeks out, Rockstar did something it rarely does: it let Edge's Simon Parkin into **Rockstar North** in Edinburgh, where co-studio head and head of development **Rob Nelson** picked up a controller and **played for 90 minutes with no script and no publicist reading from one**. The result is a 16-page cover story in Edge issue 430, on sale 29 October and free to read on GamesRadar+.

**Twelve new screenshots**

The feature carries 14 Rockstar-provided images, 12 of them new. They range across Vice City's dense waterfront skyline and neon nightscapes, small-town streets, trailer parks, industrial outskirts and rural Leonida — and one goes underwater to show a **hammerhead shark moving along a coral corridor**, a biome Rockstar has described far more often than it has shown.

On the character side: a close-up of VCPD officer **Méndez** (previously only hinted at in the Extended Look), Lucia in a white tank top and gold chain, Jason with long hair, a 24/7 store robbery, a motorcycle getaway, bikers riding in formation and a mud race.

**What the police remember**

The significant systemic change is how much the authorities retain. Per Edge, police log **your description, whether you were alone or with a partner, your clothes, your weapon, and whether CCTV caught you**, surfaced as icons at the top of the screen.

Escape logic changes with it: rather than waiting for wanted stars to cool, you **change clothes, drop the weapon and split up from your partner** to become progressively harder to identify. Stealing cars is riskier too — some drivers panic and flee, others are armed and fight back, newer premium cars may carry a tracker that gives away your position, and the owner may call the police afterwards.

**Criminal Profile and the four-point relationship**

Separate from the police, the game tracks a **Criminal Profile** rated from **Professional** to **Irredeemable**, and Rockstar says player behaviour affects how Jason and Lucia's story unfolds. Some story jobs require the pair to raise money first, through gas-station robberies, jewellery store heists and stealing requested vehicles for fences.

The relationship between Jason and Lucia is a **four-point scale**, raised through shared activities — spending time together, the gym, exploring Leonida — with players having some influence over how intimate it becomes. Rockstar **declined to say whether the pair can marry**, which immediately fed fan theories.

**Density and detail**

Other confirmed systems:

- Weapons live in the **car trunk** rather than a menu; a shotgun slung across your back worries people less than one held in your hands
- **Scooter Bros**, a phone service, delivers your owned vehicles to you; every car burns fuel and engines wear over time
- Leonida has **usable buses and trains**
- A **longer day/night cycle**; localised weather (fog, wind, season) affects roads, water, aircraft and pedestrian behaviour
- NPC reactions vary by **traits such as aggression** — some run, some come looking for trouble. Rockstar built a dedicated team in Los Angeles (writers, directors, producers) for pedestrians, with roughly **600,000 unique animations** across the game
- Garbut mentions a perspiration system (Jason and Lucia sweat based on how hard they dance) and a disgust reaction originally built for a single scene: he walked into an unfamiliar house with photos of a dog in one room and the next room trashed by the animal, and Jason grimaced on his own

On scale, Garbut's new line is that there are **"an order of magnitude more interiors"** than before. Caveat: GTA 5 shipped with an estimated 70–80 enterable interiors — a ComicBook count, never published by Rockstar — so the resulting 700–800 figure is **media arithmetic, not an official number**.

**What was already known**: the map being roughly twice GTA 5's size with a landmass close to GTA IV + GTA V + RDR2 combined, and the 600,000 animations, both came out of the late-August wave after the New York Times visit. This feature restates them. What's genuinely new is the systems detail above and the 12 screenshots.

On the date itself, Nelson's position is unambiguous: the game is **"ready"**.
