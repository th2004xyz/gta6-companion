## 中文正文

**这是本周最需要更正的一条信息。** Rockstar 已在 11 月 3 日通过 Newswire 与官方支持页发布正式公告，把此前流传的预载时间表整个改了：

| 项目 | 此前流传（第三方） | **官方口径（11/3 公告）** |
|---|---|---|
| 数字版预载开始 | 11 月 12 日 | **11 月 10 日** |
| 预载容量 | 约 210GB（PS 商店后台读数） | **最多 212GB（视设备而定）** |
| 盒装下载码提货 | 11 月 12 日 | 11 月 12 日（**不变**） |
| 正式解锁 | 11 月 19 日零点 | 11 月 19 日零点（**不变**） |

换句话说：**11/12 并没有消失，它现在专指「实体盒装下载码版到店提货」，不再是数字版预载日**；而 210GB 作为爆料读数，已被官方的 212GB 取代。本站此前若干页面沿用了旧口径，已同步回填。

**具体时间怎么算**

- **PS5**：按你所在时区的 **11 月 10 日零点**开放，不是全球同时
- **Xbox Series X|S**：全球统一按 **GMT 11 月 10 日 00:00** 开放，换算为美东 11/9 19:00、美西 11/9 16:00、**北京时间 11/10 08:00**
- **11 月 19 日**：两个平台均按各时区零点解锁，新西兰最早

由于 Xbox 按 GMT 统一、PS5 按时区各自开放，实际上海外部分地区会比别人早几个小时开始下载——但**都只是下载，不是解锁**。

**预载前要做的四件事**

1. **清出至少 250GB** — 官方给的是「最多 212GB，视设备而定」，没按机型分别列出。首日补丁是否存在、有多大，Rockstar 尚未确认，留余量最稳。Xbox Series S（约 512GB，可用约 364GB）压力最大。
2. **开启自动下载** — PS5 需开启自动下载并保持开机或待机（Rest Mode）；Xbox 需开启自动更新并保持开机或睡眠（Sleep mode）。
3. **确认购买账号一致** — 用下单时同一个 PSN / Xbox 账号登录。
4. **别把预载当提前游玩** — 下载的是加密文件，内容锁定至 11/19。所谓「新西兰技巧」是改主机地区绕过解锁，不是官方支持的玩法。

按 100Mbps 带宽估算，212GB 约需 4.7 小时；50Mbps 接近 10 小时。发售当周服务器压力大，提前一周下完是这次预载的全部意义。

**11/19 还有免费物料**：Rockstar 确认当日起在官网提供**可打印的封面海报 + Leonida 地图**免费数字下载；双面实体版海报稍后在 Rockstar Store 单独售 $0.99，或随任意购买赠送。

**PC 版仍无消息** — 官方此次公告只覆盖 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，没有给出任何 PC 日期、配置或容量信息。

## Overview

**This is the correction that matters most this week.** Rockstar published a formal announcement on November 3 via Newswire and its support pages, and it rewrites the preload timetable that had been circulating:

| Item | Earlier (third-party) | **Official (Nov 3)** |
|---|---|---|
| Digital preload start | November 12 | **November 10** |
| Preload size | ~210GB (PS Store backend read) | **Up to 212GB, depending on device** |
| Code-in-box pickup | November 12 | November 12 (**unchanged**) |
| Unlock | Midnight, November 19 | Midnight, November 19 (**unchanged**) |

Put plainly: **November 12 has not disappeared — it now refers only to physical code-in-box copies reaching retailers, not the digital preload date.** And the 210GB leak has been superseded by the official 212GB. Several pages on this site used the old figures and have been updated.

**The exact timings**

- **PS5**: opens at **local midnight on November 10** in each time zone — not a single global moment
- **Xbox Series X|S**: opens globally at **00:00 GMT on November 10**, i.e. 7 p.m. Eastern / 4 p.m. Pacific on November 9, and **8 a.m. Beijing time on November 10**
- **November 19**: both platforms unlock at local midnight, New Zealand first

Because Xbox uses one global GMT moment while PS5 follows local time, some regions start downloading hours earlier than others — but **all of it is download, not access**.

**Four things to do before preload**

1. **Clear at least 250GB** — Rockstar says up to 212GB depending on device, with no per-console breakdown. Whether a day-one patch exists, and how large, has not been confirmed. Xbox Series S (~512GB, ~364GB usable) is squeezed hardest.
2. **Enable automatic downloads** — PS5 needs auto-downloads on and the console powered on or in Rest Mode; Xbox needs automatic updates on and the console on or in Sleep mode.
3. **Use the purchasing account** — sign in with the same PSN / Xbox account used to pre-order.
4. **Do not treat preload as early access** — the files are encrypted and stay locked until November 19. The so-called New Zealand trick changes console region to bypass the unlock and is not supported.

At 100Mbps, 212GB takes roughly 4.7 hours; at 50Mbps, close to ten. With servers under pressure during launch week, getting it done a week early is the entire point.

**Free extras on November 19**: Rockstar confirms a printable cover art poster and a Leonida map as free digital downloads on the official site from that date. A double-sided physical poster follows shortly after from the Rockstar Store, 0.99 dollars on its own or free with any purchase.

**Still nothing on PC** — the announcement covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date, requirements or file size.
