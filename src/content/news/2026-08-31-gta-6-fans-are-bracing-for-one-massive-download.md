## 中文正文

「预载 11 月 12 日开启、11 月 19 日解锁」——官方时间表公布后，玩家圈开始认真讨论一个此前被忽略的问题：**GTA 6 到底要下多大？**

## 粉丝的猜测：100GB 起步，250GB 封顶

在 ResetEra 的投票里，多数玩家把 GTA 6 的安装容量押在 **100GB-250GB** 区间。这个猜测并非空穴来风，它精确对齐了 R 星近作的历史数据：

| 作品 | 平台 | 安装容量 |
|---|---|---|
| 荒野大镖客 2（2018） | PS4 / Xbox One | 约 105-150GB |
| GTA 5 次世代版 | PS5 / XSX | 约 110GB |
| **GTA 6（玩家猜测）** | PS5 / XSX | **100-250GB** |

## 为什么这次可能特别大

- **纯数字发行**：GTA 6 实体版是「盒装兑换码」（Code in Box），没有光盘兜底，完整游戏数据全靠下载
- **内容密度跃升**：实机演示展示的 NPC 生态、物理粒子与光线细节远超以往——这些都要容量买单
- **Leonida 地图规模**：R 星史上最大的开放世界，面积与内容密度双双膨胀

## 行业参照

近年 3A 大作的容量膨胀有先例：使命召唤系列曾因统一启动器、共享材质包与携带内容，安装体积一度逼近 **300GB**；好消息是《黑色行动 6》之后动视大幅优化了安装结构，漫威争锋等新作也在主动压缩体积——如果 R 星也做了类似优化，最终数字可能比悲观预期温和不少。

官方容量数字预计随 11 月 12 日预载开启前的商店页面更新公布。我们已准备好完整的[预载与硬盘准备指南](/zh/news/2026-09-01-gta-6-preload-guide-file-size)，建议提前清出至少 200GB 空间。

## Overview

We're only a few months away from the November launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, and following the recent preview of the game on Netflix, fans have even more questions about Rockstar's highly anticipated game--namely, just how big is the download going to be? We already know that you can preload the game a week before launch, but is that because you'll need multiple days to download all the files? GTA 6 fans are currently speculating just how big the install is going to be, with the majority of people taking part in a ResetEra poll guessing that it'll clock in between 100GB to 250GB. That's a fairly generous margin--and highly likely--given the history of Rockstar's previous games and the current state of game-install sizes. Considering that GTA 6 is a download-only game--physical copies are just a code in a box--you're going to want to make some space available on your PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.

For example, Red Dead Redemption 2 requires approximately 105GB to 150GB, depending on your platform, and was released in 2018. Video games have only gotten bigger and even more detailed since then, leveraging the power of modern hardware to create detailed worlds. High-resolution graphic textures and detailed character models only add to the install-size bloat, with some games even offering options to reduce that digital footprint by selectively installing parts of the experience. Call of Duty was a prime example, as the game typically offers an option to install it piecemeal. Modern Call of Duty games used to be up to 150GB in size, and once you started slotting in extra content like the Call of Duty HQ unified launcher, shared texture packs for older titles, Call of Duty: Warzone, and carry-forward content, the install size neared 300GB. Fortunately, things started to change with the release of Call of Duty: Black Ops 6, as the developers made big changes to the installation process. Other game developers have also made an effort to better optimize their titles, as Marvel Rivals is now considerably smaller on PC and consoles, so there's hope that Rockstar Games has used some technical wizardry to make the download more manageable--after all, with the rising price of memory, not everyone has the budget to give their console a quick SSD upgrade just for a single game.
