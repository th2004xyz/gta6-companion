## 中文正文

Netflix 首播实机演示才过去两周，Rockstar 就要在发售前两个月站上被告席。**9 月 10 日（周四）**，Rockstar 与 31 名被解雇的 Rockstar North 员工之间的**最终听证正式开庭**，一直排到 **10 月 16 日**——距离 11 月 19 日发售只剩一个多月。

争议要回溯到去年 10 月：Rockstar 以"严重不当行为"（gross misconduct）为由一次性解雇 **34 名员工**，其中 31 人来自苏格兰的 Rockstar North，当场生效并被带离办公楼。公司的说法是这些人在 IWGB 的私密 Discord 频道里讨论公司事务、违反保密协议；而员工方面（通过 People Make Games 的采访）坚称他们讨论的是**薪资与 HR 问题**——也就是工会频道里本来就会聊的东西。

代表这些员工的英国独立工人工会（IWGB）提出的指控是：Rockstar **非法解雇**并因他们的工会身份实施**"拉黑"**。今年早些时候 Rockstar 曾试图让这些指控被驳回，但雇佣法庭没有支持。

这次最终听证上，IWGB 代表员工要求**复职或赔偿**，并要求法庭认定解雇不公。IWGB 主席 Alex Marshall 在开庭前的表态很直白——"我们认为 Rockstar 一直在试图逃避责任，这简直是 Grand Theft Employment Rights"（Rockstar 想偷走的不是车，是劳工权利）。

前 Rockstar 员工 Jason Lewis 则说，经过近一年的抗争，"真相终于有机会被记录在案"。

这起事件此前已经闹到英国议会，时任首相 Keir Starmer 也曾公开表示"每个工人都有权加入工会"。风波之后，**Rockstar 游戏工人联合会（Rockstar Game Workers Union）** 成立并持续壮大，还陆续提出了强制加班、奖金机制被工具化、性别薪酬差距等问题。

对玩家而言，这场官司不会改变 11 月 19 日的发售日，但它很可能决定这场发售会以什么样的舆论底色开场。想第一时间收到发售相关提醒，可以[订阅通知](/zh/subscribe)。

## Overview

Two weeks after Grand Theft Auto 6's Netflix reveal, and only a couple of months before the game's release, Rockstar Games has an employment tribunal to fight. The final hearing in Rockstar vs. the 31 fired Rockstar North employees begins Thursday 10th September and runs through to 16th October.

Rockstar must face allegations that it "unlawfully" fired those 31 employees and "blacklisted" them for union membership — claims brought by the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB). Rockstar tried to have the claims thrown out earlier this year, but the employment tribunal ruled against it.

Rockstar fired 34 employees in October last year — 31 of them at Rockstar North in Scotland — for alleged "gross misconduct", saying they breached confidentiality agreements by talking about the company in a private IWGB Discord channel. The employees maintain they were discussing salaries and HR issues. The IWGB seeks reinstatement or compensation, "as well as a finding from the tribunal that their dismissals were unfair".

Ahead of the hearing, IWGB president Alex Marshall said: "We believe Rockstar has tried to get away with Grand Theft Employment Rights." Former Rockstar employee Jason Lewis added: "For almost a year, the truth about what happened to us has been buried by Rockstar... Now we finally get the chance to put it on the record."
