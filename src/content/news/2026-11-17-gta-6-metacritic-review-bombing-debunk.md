## 中文正文

如果你今天（11 月 17 日）打开 Metacritic，会看到 GTA6 的用户评分赫然挂着 **6.5**。在发售倒计时只剩两天、全网期待拉满的当口，这个分数看起来很扎眼。但把页面往下拉，你会发现问题所在——**它只来自 5 个评分，其中只有 2 个是正面的。**

**为什么这 5 个分数说明不了任何事**

- GTA6 要到 **11 月 19 日零点**才解锁，目前预载的是加密文件，没人能真正玩到
- 媒体评测要到 **11 月 18 日**才解禁，Metascore 至今显示 **tbd**（待定）
- Metacritic 用户分**不需要购买凭证**，任何账号都能打

换句话说，11 月 16–17 日打出的分数，评价的可能是预告片、截图、预购收据，或者干脆就是对 Rockstar 的情绪——而不是游戏本身。Men's Journal、Gadgets Now、MSN 等媒体都以「令人震惊的褒贬不一开局」「提前评测轰炸」来报道这件事。

**5 个评分在数学上意味着什么**

几乎什么都没有。5 个样本的平均值，改动其中任意一个就能让显示分数上下跳动一分以上。6.5 分、2 正 3 负，只够说明有人把这个页面当成了表态板——既不足以描述「社区反应」，更谈不上「口碑共识」。

**值得注意的是，轰炸是双向的。** 同一批用户页面在 11 月 19 日解锁后，大概率会涌入一波 10 分刷屏。两种极端都不告诉你游戏好不好。真正有价值的是两个数字：11 月 18 日解禁后出现的 **Metascore**，以及解锁几天后由成千上万真实玩家累积的**用户分**。

本站会在 11 月 18 日评测解禁后第一时间汇总首批媒体评分与共识观点。在此之前，请勿把任何 6.5、9 分之类的「提前读数」当成结论——它们要么是 5 个人的情绪，要么是别人的预测。

## Overview

If you open Metacritic today (November 17), you will see GTA 6's user score sitting at **6.5**. With two days to launch and hype at maximum, that number looks alarming. Scroll down and you will find the catch — **it comes from five ratings, only two of them positive.**

**Why those five ratings mean nothing**

- GTA 6 does not unlock until **midnight on November 19**; the current preload is encrypted and nobody can actually play it
- Critic reviews do not go live until **November 18**, and the Metascore still reads **tbd**
- Metacritic user ratings **require no proof of purchase** — any account can score a game

Ratings posted on November 16–17 therefore reflect opinions about a trailer, a screenshot set, a pre-order receipt or the publisher — not the game. Men's Journal, Gadgets Now and MSN all covered it as a "shockingly mixed debut" and "early review bombing."

**What five ratings mean mathematically**

Very little. With five samples, changing a single score moves the displayed average by more than a point. A 6.5 from two positives and three negatives only proves someone used the page as a protest board — it describes neither a community reaction nor a consensus.

**And the bombing runs both ways.** The same user page will likely absorb a wave of 10/10s once the game unlocks on November 19. Neither extreme tells you whether the game is good. The numbers worth watching are the **Metascore** when the embargo lifts on November 18, and the user score once it is computed from thousands of real players in the days after launch.

We will round up the first critic scores and consensus as soon as the embargo lifts. Until then, do not treat any early 6.5 or 9/10 "reading" as a verdict — it is either five people's sentiment or someone's prediction.
