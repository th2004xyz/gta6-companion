## 中文正文

GameSpot 的这篇评论提出了一个不太好回答的问题：**当 GTA 系列第一次让玩家扮演女性，Rockstar 真的准备好了吗？**

作者的梳理是这样的：

- 二十多年来，GTA 的可玩主角全部是男性，女性角色大多被用来**推动他们的故事**，而不是拥有自己的故事
- 少数亮眼例外：GTA3 里的黑帮头目 **Asuka Kasen**、圣安地列斯里情绪细腻的 **Kendl Johnson**
- 反面案例则明显过时了：Michael 的妻子和女儿、Franklin 那位执着于"推翻父权"的 Denise 阿姨、GTA3 最后背叛 Claude 然后被一个画外玩笑草草处理的 Catalina
- 关键对照是 **RDR2 的 Sadie Adler**——Rockstar 已经证明过自己能写出成熟、有层次的女性角色

Lucia Caminos 的处境因此很微妙：她有可能是这个媒介里最有力的女性主角之一，也可能是又一次"看起来很进步"的包装。作者反复强调的一点很实在——**GTA 世界里"人人都是靶子"这套说法，在主角全是男性、女性角色只是配衬时很难站得住脚**。

距离发售还有两个多月，Lucia 到底会被写成什么样，现在谁也说不准。想了解另一位主角，可以看我们的[Jason 与 Lucia 角色档案](/zh/characters)。

## Overview

Grand Theft Auto has always had a complicated relationship with women. For decades, it's been known as the series where you can visit a strip club, hire a sex worker, and swiftly beat her to death in the nearest alley. With the arrival of player character Lucia Caminos in the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 later this year, Rockstar has an opportunity to completely redefine the franchise's problematic dynamic with women and potentially introduce one of the strongest female protagonists this medium has ever seen — but sticking the landing is easier said than done.

People often claim that everybody is an equal target in GTA's satirical worlds, but it's hard to accept that perspective when all the protagonists are men and most female characters around them exist to advance those men's stories. There are rare bright sparks, such as crime lord Asuka Kasen in GTA 3 or San Andreas' emotionally intelligent Kendl Johnson — but Rockstar has already proven it can write mature, nuanced women like Sadie Adler in Red Dead Redemption 2.
