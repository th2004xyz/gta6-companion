## 中文正文

预载开启的同时，Rockstar 也把外界猜了数周的**技术规格**一次性说清楚了。结合官方「Pre-Load Now」宣传片与官方资料页，以下是发售版本的确定口径。

**帧率：主机版目标 30 帧，无 60 帧模式。** Rockstar 的说法是，游戏从零开始为本世代主机打造，优先保证世界密度、视觉保真度与模拟系统的深度，而不是堆帧率。此前 6 月流传的「PS5 全系跑不到 60 帧」传闻，如今以官方口径坐实——只是官方把它表述为一种主动的取舍，而非能力不足。

**PS5 Pro 独享画质增强。** Pro 机型支持 PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution（PSSR）超分技术以提升清晰度，并有专属的光线追踪阴影。这意味着同样是 30 帧，Pro 上的画面细节会更进一步。

**DualSense 与 3D 音效完整支持。** 官方确认完整整合 DualSense 高保真触觉反馈（可通过无障碍设置调整强度），并全面支持 Tempest 3D 音效，同时大幅缩短载入时间。

**Xbox 一侧：云游戏覆盖 Xbox One。** Xbox Series X|S 是原生版本；Xbox One 用户自 11 月 19 日起可通过 Xbox Cloud Gaming 串流，采用 Series S 的画质设定，流畅度看网络。两个平台都支持远程游玩（Remote Play）。Rockstar 同时重申，游戏在 PlayStation 5 上「体验最佳」。

**两个没有的东西：** 一是 PC 版日期——此次公告只覆盖 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，PC 仍无任何日期、配置或容量信息；二是官方从未公布过「评测解禁时间」，任何 11 月 18 日解禁的说法都来自第三方爆料（详见本站单独的评测解禁追踪稿）。

这些信息直接影响购买决策：如果你在意为 60 帧升级硬件，官方已经明确这不是当前版本的选项；如果你只有 Xbox One，云游戏是唯一通路。

## Overview

As the preload went live, Rockstar also settled the **technical spec** that had been guesswork for weeks. Drawing on the official "Pre-Load Now" video and the official info pages, here is what is confirmed for launch.

**Frame rate: 30fps target on consoles, no 60fps mode.** Rockstar says the game was built from the ground up for current consoles, prioritising world density, visual fidelity and simulation depth over raw frame rate. The June rumour that "no PS5 will hit 60fps" is now confirmed by official wording — though Rockstar frames it as a deliberate trade-off rather than a limitation.

**PS5 Pro gets exclusive visual upgrades.** The Pro model supports PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) for sharper imagery plus dedicated ray-traced shadows. Same 30fps target, but finer detail on Pro.

**Full DualSense and 3D audio support.** The official brief confirms high-fidelity DualSense haptics (adjustable via accessibility settings), full Tempest 3D audio support and significantly shorter load times.

**Xbox: cloud coverage for Xbox One.** Xbox Series X|S runs natively; Xbox One owners can stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming from November 19 using Series S visual settings, with smoothness depending on connection. Both platforms support Remote Play. Rockstar also reiterates that the game plays best on PlayStation 5.

**Two things that are absent:** a PC date — this announcement covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date, specs or file size; and any official review embargo — any November 18 embargo date comes from third-party leaks (see our separate review-embargo tracker).

These details matter for buying decisions: if you were upgrading hardware for 60fps, the official word is that is not an option in this version; if you only own an Xbox One, cloud streaming is the only route.
