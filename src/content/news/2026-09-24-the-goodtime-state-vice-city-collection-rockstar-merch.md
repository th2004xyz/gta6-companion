## 中文正文

发售前两个月，Rockstar 的周边矩阵又多了一档高价选项。9 月 24 日，官方 Newswire 上架 **The Goodtime State Vice City Collection**，一套定价 **399.99 美元**的收藏盒。根据官方说明，盒子里装的是实体周边（服饰、徽章、海报等）叠加一些数字内容，**并不包含 GTA6 游戏本体**——想玩到游戏仍需单独预购。

这延续了 Rockstar 这一轮"发售前营销轰炸"的节奏：9 月 3 日 State of Play 公布两款限量 DualSense 手柄（84.99 美元，11 月 19 日同步发售），如今再推一档面向收藏党的高价套装。对内容站而言，这类官方周边是 `confirmed` 级别的营销节点，吃的是发售前搜索长尾里"GTA6 周边 / 收藏"这类词。

需要注意：399.99 美元只是周边，不是游戏。标准版游戏 79.99 美元、终极版 99.99 美元，周边与游戏分开购买。别把这套盒子的价格误读成游戏售价。

## Overview

Two months out, Rockstar's merch matrix has a new premium tier. On September 24, the official Newswire listed **The Goodtime State Vice City Collection**, a $399.99 collector's box. Per Rockstar, it bundles physical merch (apparel, pins, posters) with some digital extras — but **not the game itself**; you still buy GTA 6 separately.

It extends the pre-launch marketing blitz: after the two limited DualSense controllers ($84.99, shipping November 19) revealed at September's State of Play, this is the highest-priced launch merchandise yet, aimed at collectors. For a content site, official merch is a `confirmed` marketing beat that captures long-tail searches like "GTA 6 collectibles".

Caveat: $399.99 is the box, not the game. Standard edition is $79.99, Ultimate $99.99; merch and game are separate purchases.
