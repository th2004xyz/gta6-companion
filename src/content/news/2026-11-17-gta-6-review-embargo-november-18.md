## 中文正文

发售前最后一周，最热的话题不是游戏本身，而是**「评分什么时候出来」**。综合目前所有爆料，答案指向 11 月 18 日——但请把下文当作「未经证实的第三方消息」来读，因为 Rockstar 从头到尾没有官宣过解禁时间。

**日期与时刻**

- **10 月 26 日**，Insider Gaming 援引爆料人 NateTheHate 称 Rockstar 已开始分发评测码，解禁日为 **11 月 18 日**，并表示已用自己的信源核实
- **11 月 17 日**，爆料账号 @JamesFox_SJF 称解禁在英国时间周三中午 12 点，即**北京时间当晚 20:00**
- 具体时刻还有个更细的说法：ResetEra 用户 BlackBate 称分数在**美东 11 月 18 日 6:01–7:01** 的一小时内出现，换算为北京时间约 **19:01–20:01**
- 另有一批媒体和 YouTube 频道可能被压到 **11 月 19 日 0:01（美东）**——即游戏在美区解锁时——才允许发布，形成「两波解禁」

**为什么 11 月 18 日这个日期可信度较高**

因为它是 R 星的老套路：GTA5 在 2013 年 9 月 16 日解禁、9 月 17 日发售；荒野大镖客 2 在 2018 年 10 月 25 日解禁、10 月 26 日发售——都是**发售前一天**。给媒体充足时间体验，再把解禁压到发售前夜，让讨论直接冲进发售日。

**「低于 9 分都值得怀疑」是什么**

11 月 17 日，爆料人 @JamesFox_SJF 转述一位「提前通关主线、参与前期评测的内部人员」的话：如果有媒体给 GTA6 低于 9 分，这个结果本身就值得怀疑；本作的重心不再只是开放世界自由度，主线叙事的层次和人物刻画会带来「强烈的情感冲击」，有望冲击年度最佳。

这类说法请打个问号：没有可验证身份、没有媒体背书，而且临近发售的爆料历史上经常跑偏。评测人员的主观感受不等于最终评分，Metacritic / OpenCritic 的均分要等数十家媒体出分后才有意义。

**评测不会告诉你的事**：GTA6 首发是**纯单机**，多人模式没有日期，评测只覆盖剧情；评测用的通常是发售前的版本，首日补丁可能改变表现；多数评测者只在 PS5 / PS5 Pro 上游玩，Xbox Series S 玩家要留意平台差异。

本站会在 11 月 18 日解禁后第一时间汇总首批评分与共识。

## Overview

In the final week before launch, the hottest topic is not the game itself but **"when do the scores come out?"** Every leak currently points to November 18 — but read everything below as unverified third-party information, because Rockstar has never officially announced an embargo.

**Date and timing**

- **October 26**: Insider Gaming, citing leaker NateTheHate, said Rockstar had begun sending review codes and the embargo lifts on **November 18**, corroborated with its own sources
- **November 17**: account @JamesFox_SJF said the embargo lifts at noon UK Wednesday — **20:00 Beijing time**
- A finer detail from ResetEra user BlackBate: scores appear in a one-hour window starting **6:01 a.m. Eastern on November 18**, roughly **19:01–20:01 Beijing time**
- A second tier of outlets and YouTubers may be held until **12:01 a.m. Eastern on November 19** — US unlock — creating a two-wave embargo

**Why November 18 carries decent credibility**

It is Rockstar's established pattern: GTA V's reviews went live September 16, 2013 for a September 17 launch; Red Dead Redemption 2's on October 25, 2018 for an October 26 launch — both **one day before release**. Give critics ample time, lift late, and let the conversation run straight into launch day.

**The "anything below 9 is suspect" claim**

On November 17, @JamesFox_SJF relayed words from an "internal reviewer who finished the main story and took part in early evaluation": any outlet scoring GTA 6 below 9 would be suspect, and the game's narrative depth and character writing would deliver "strong emotional impact," making it a contender for game of the year.

Take that with a grain of salt: no verifiable identity, no outlet backing, and pre-launch claims often miss. A reviewer's impression is not a score. Metacritic / OpenCritic averages only mean something once dozens of outlets publish.

**What reviews will not cover**: GTA 6 launches **single-player only**, with no date for multiplayer; reviewers usually play a pre-launch build, so a day-one patch may change performance; and most review on PS5 / PS5 Pro, so Xbox Series S players should watch for platform-specific reports.

We will round up the first scores and consensus as soon as the embargo lifts.
