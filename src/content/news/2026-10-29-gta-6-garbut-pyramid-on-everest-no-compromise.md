## 中文正文

距发售不到三周，Rockstar North 开发主管、长期担任系列美术总监的 **Aaron Garbut** 在接受《Edge》杂志专访时，正面回应了那个玩家最担心的问题：为了赶发售，游戏有没有被削减？

他的原话是：

> 「我们不能只是随便端出一款大家预期中的作品，或者那个最容易做的版本。我们在**规模、细节、互动性、剧情、开放世界**上都没有妥协。」
>
> 「我觉得我们就像是在**珠穆朗玛峰上建造了一座金字塔**。」

Garbut 强调，团队非常清楚外界期待有多高——距离 GTA5 已过去约十三年，玩家等待被拉长到了极限，光是「符合预期」是不够的。

**支撑这个说法的数字**

- 全作约 **60 万条独特动画**（对比：GTA5 约 5.5 万条，荒野大镖客 2 约 30 万条）
- Rockstar 组建了一支**只为背景路人服务**的写作团队，每个路人都有自己的性格、脾气、所处环境与「怎么被你对待过」的记录
- Jason 与 Lucia 会**按跳舞的强度出汗**——这个细节对主线毫无影响，但它说明了 Rockstar 在追求什么：把角色状态绑定到行为上，而不是一个固定的贴图
- 游戏大到**公司内部没有任何一个人掌握全部内容**

**该怎么读这段话**

需要保持距离地看：这是**开发者对自家作品的评价**，不是第三方验证。它无法证明游戏好不好玩，也无法回应关于开发周期与工作强度的争议（那属于另一个话题）。

但有一个信息是实打实的：在发售前三周，两位工作室主管（Garbut 与 Rob Nelson）同时对外表态，且 Nelson 明确说游戏「已经准备好了」——**这本身就是一种降低延期预期的信号**。历史经验是：Rockstar 在宣布延期之前，官方口径通常含糊得多。

**PC 版仍无消息。** Rockstar 至今未公布 PC 版发售日；PC Gamer 在转述时指出 R 星过往通常在主机与 PC 之间留出至少七个月，GTA5 隔了一年半，但那是媒体对历史节奏的归纳，不是本作承诺。

## Overview

Less than three weeks before launch, **Aaron Garbut** — Rockstar North's head of development and the series' long-serving art director — answered the question players keep asking in an Edge interview: was anything cut to hit the date?

His words:

> "We can't just trot out something that's what people might expect, or that was the easy thing to do. We've not compromised on the **scale, the detail, the interactivity, the story, the open world**."
>
> "I feel like we've **built a pyramid on Everest**."

Garbut stressed the team is acutely aware of the expectations: roughly thirteen years have passed since GTA 5, and simply meeting what players assumed they'd get was never going to be enough.

**The numbers behind the claim**

- Roughly **600,000 unique animations** across the game (against an estimated ~55,000 in GTA 5 and ~300,000 in RDR2)
- A **writing team dedicated solely to background NPCs**, each pedestrian carrying a temperament, aggression level, situation and a record of how you've treated them
- Jason and Lucia **sweat based on how intensely they dance** — a detail with zero bearing on the main plot, but one that shows what Rockstar is chasing: character state tied to action rather than a fixed texture
- A game so large that **no single person at Rockstar knows everything about it**

**How to read it**

Worth keeping some distance: this is a **developer assessing their own work**, not third-party verification. It proves nothing about whether the game is fun, and it does not address the separate debate over development crunch.

One signal is nonetheless real: with three weeks to go, both studio heads — Garbut and Rob Nelson — went on the record, and Nelson said outright the game is "ready". In the past, Rockstar's language ahead of a delay has typically been far vaguer.

**Still nothing on PC.** Rockstar has announced no PC date. PC Gamer, reporting the remarks, noted the studio usually leaves at least seven months between console and PC versions and that GTA 5 took a year and a half — a reading of past cadence, not a promise for this game.
