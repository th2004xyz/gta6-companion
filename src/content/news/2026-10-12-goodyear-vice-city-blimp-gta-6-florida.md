## 中文正文

发售前的现实世界营销继续加码。一艘 Goodyear 齐柏林飞艇（Zeppelin NT）被整体包上 GTA VI「Vice City」涂装，从俄亥俄州阿克伦起飞，目的地是佛罗里达州庞帕诺比奇——距离游戏中的 Vice City 原型迈阿密不远。

这是 GTA 系列虚构飞艇头一回以真实品牌涂装的形式出现在现实天空，也呼应了预告片与实机演示里 Vice City 上空那艘标志性的飞艇。此前 Rockstar 还上线了 The Goodtime State 周边套装与 LOVE 杂志联名封面，现实营销节奏明显提速。

## Overview

Real-world marketing is ramping up before launch. A Goodyear Zeppelin NT has been fully wrapped in GTA VI's "Vice City" livery and flown from Akron, Ohio to Pompano Beach, Florida — not far from Miami, the real-world basis for in-game Vice City.

It's the first time the franchise's fictional airship has shown up as real-world branding, echoing the iconic blimp over Vice City in the trailers and the Extended Look gameplay. Rockstar has also shipped The Goodtime State merch box and a LOVE magazine cover tie-in, with real-world marketing clearly accelerating.
