## 中文正文

GTA6 主机版已定于 **2026 年 11 月 19 日**登陆 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S，但 PC 玩家还需耐心。Rockstar 至今**未官宣任何 PC 版本**——目前官方列出的平台仅有 PS5 与 Xbox Series X|S。

GameSpot 基于 Rockstar 历代 PC 版滞后规律给出估算：PC 版大概率在 **2028 年第一季度**上线，比主机版晚约 15–18 个月。这早于 GTA5 的 19 个月等待，晚于《荒野大镖客2》的 12 个月。该估算**并非泄露或内部消息**，而是参照历史。

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick 曾解释，Rockstar 历来优先服务主机核心受众，并否认这与 PlayStation 营销协议有关；他也提到如今 PC 可占大作销量的 45–50%。因此 PC 版几乎必然到来，只是时间问题。

## Overview

Grand Theft Auto 6 is officially scheduled to launch on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, but PC players will have to wait longer. Rockstar has yet to announce a PC version at all — only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are listed as official platforms. GameSpot estimates a Q1 2028 PC release, roughly 15–18 months after the console launch, based on Rockstar's history (earlier than GTA 5's 19-month wait, later than Red Dead Redemption 2's 12-month gap). The estimate is not a leak or insider claim. Take-Two's Strauss Zelnick has said Rockstar traditionally prioritises console first and noted PC can now be 45–50% of a major title's sales — so a PC version is all but certain, just later.
