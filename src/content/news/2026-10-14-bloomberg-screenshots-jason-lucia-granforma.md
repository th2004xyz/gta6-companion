## 中文正文

随 Bloomberg 那篇开发长文，Rockstar 放出了两张此前未公开、但用于媒体配图的官方截图（非玩家可自行截取的游戏画面）。

第一张：Jason 与 Lucia 站在 Leonida 的越野乡野路段，两人满身泥泞、手里拿着啤酒，身旁是一辆卡车。这延续了《荒野大镖客2》的思路——泥泞是会附着在角色身上、直到涉水或更换衣服才掉的「物理图层」，意味着在 Vice City 街道与内陆乡野间穿梭，旅程会「穿」在身上。

第二张：Ocean Drive 日落时分，霓虹灯亮起，一架警用直升机低空掠过，画面焦点是一台敞篷跑车的车尾。媒体 RockstarINTEL 将其读作疑似 Pegassi 的新 trim「GranForma」——但这只是媒体对截图的读图，Rockstar 官方新闻线与游戏主页面都未就此车给出对应说明或命名。

## Overview

Alongside the Bloomberg development feature, Rockstar released two previously unseen, Rockstar-sanctioned stills used for press (not player-capturable gameplay).

Frame one: Jason and Lucia stand in Leonida's off-road countryside, both caked in mud and holding beers, next to a truck. This extends the Red Dead Redemption 2 idea — mud is a physical layer that clings to characters until they hit water or change clothes, so moving between Vice City streets and the rural interior wears the trip on your clothes.

Frame two: Ocean Drive at sunset, neon lit, a police helicopter skimming low, the focus a convertible's rear end. The outlet RockstarINTEL reads the badge as a possible new Pegassi trim, "GranForma" — but that is the outlet's own reading of the still; Rockstar's newswire and the main game page carry no matching post or vehicle name.
