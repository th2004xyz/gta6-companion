## 中文正文

发售前最后十天，最容易被误当成「首发评分」的东西开始冒出来。这里把状态说清楚：**截至目前，GTA6 一分真实评分都没有。**

**那条「7 分测评」是什么**

X 用户 **MrKelsGame** 发文称自己已经玩了 GTA6 三天，说开放世界「令人惊叹」，但抱怨以佛罗里达为原型的世界里暴风雨、飓风来得太频繁，已经让人厌烦，最后给出 **7 分**，并「希望后续剧情能像暴风雨一样猛烈」。

帖子传开后，他自己在评论区补了一句：

> 「我只是在假装自己已经玩上了，直到有一天真的能玩到。」

所以这不是试玩报告，是一次围绕佛州多飓风特点的幽默创作。顺带一提，中文转述里还出现了「仍以洛圣都为背景、离次世代太远」等互相矛盾的版本——**同一条玩笑帖被转出好几个不同说法，本身就说明二手信息有多不牢靠**。

**实际状态**

- **Metacritic** 的 GTA6 页面仍显示 `Critic reviews are not available yet`，媒体评分与用户评分都是 `tbd`，没有任何一条有效评分
- **没有任何媒体或评测者**发布过真实评分
- **Rockstar 未公布评测禁令解除的具体时间**

此前广为流传的 **11 月 18 日解禁**（发售前一天）来自 NateTheHate 的爆料，Insider Gaming 表示独立验证过这个日期，但 **Rockstar 与 Take-Two 均未确认，也没有任何媒体公开承认持有评测码**。本站此前那篇报道已按「消息称」处理，这里再强调一次口径。

**为什么值得单开一篇**

作为对照，11 月 5 日《塞尔达传说：时之笛 重制版》在 Metacritic 拿到 96 分、80 多家媒体无一家低于 8 分，是 2026 年目前最高一档。围绕「GTA6 能否超过 96 分」的预测市场、盘口和讨论已经热起来了——**在真实评分出现之前，所有分数、所有「已试玩」的说法都应当归零看待**。

本站会在 Rockstar 或媒体正式解禁后更新评分汇总页。在那之前，看到任何「GTA6 评分 X 分」「我玩了三天」的内容，先查一下 Metacritic 页面是不是还挂着 `tbd`。

## Overview

In the final ten days before launch, things that look like launch scores start surfacing. The state of play is simple: **as of now, GTA 6 has no genuine review scores at all.**

**What the 7/10 post was**

X user **MrKelsGame** claimed to have played GTA 6 for three days, calling the open world stunning but complaining that storms and hurricanes in its Florida-inspired setting arrived too often and had become tiresome. He gave it **7/10** and said he hoped the story would hit as hard as the weather.

After it spread, he added in his own replies:

> "I'm just pretending I've played it until the day I actually can."

So it was not a hands-on report — it was a joke built around Florida's hurricane-prone weather. Worth noting: Chinese retellings also produced mutually contradictory versions, including one claiming the game was still set in Los Santos and felt short of next-gen. **A single joke post spawning several different summaries is itself a good illustration of how unreliable second-hand information gets right now.**

**The actual state**

- **Metacritic** still shows `Critic reviews are not available yet` on the GTA 6 page, with both critic and user scores at `tbd` and not a single valid rating
- **No outlet or reviewer** has published a genuine score
- **Rockstar has announced no review embargo time**

The widely circulated **November 18 embargo**, one day before launch, comes from a NateTheHate claim with Insider Gaming saying it verified the date — but **neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it and no outlet has admitted holding a copy**. Our earlier report treated it as a claim, and the caveat is worth repeating.

**Why this gets its own page**

For context, on November 5 The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake scored 96 on Metacritic with none of its 80-plus reviews below 8, the highest mark of 2026 so far. Prediction markets and debate over whether GTA 6 can beat 96 are already running — which is exactly why **every score and every "I've played it" claim should be treated as zero until real reviews exist**.

We will publish a score roundup once Rockstar or the press formally lifts the embargo. Until then, if you see a "GTA 6 scores X/10" or "I played it for three days" post, check whether Metacritic still says `tbd`.
