## 中文正文

10 月 2 日，时尚杂志 **LOVE** 公布 GTA6 封面（Issue 27），放出 4 张聚焦主角 **Jason 与 Lucia** 的官方新图——这些图经 Rockstar 审批、与 Game Informer 9 月 29 日的 12 张截图相互独立。Issue 27 在公布后数分钟即售罄，整本于 **10 月 5 日**上架报刊亭。

同期确认：GTA6 拿下 ESRB **「M」（17+）** 与 PEGI **18** 分级，与系列一贯的成人向定位一致。分级确认意味着各地区发行版本的内容边界基本锁定，预购玩家可放心。

从内容策略看，LOVE 封面是 Rockstar 把 GTA6 推入时尚/流行文化层的动作，和此前专辑、周边套装一起构成"发售前营销轰炸"的外圈。对中文搜索真空区，这类跨界物料也容易产出差异化长尾词。

## Overview

On October 2, fashion title **LOVE** revealed its GTA 6 cover (Issue 27) with four official new images centred on **Jason and Lucia** — approved by Rockstar and distinct from Game Informer's 12 screenshots of September 29. Issue 27 sold out within minutes of the reveal and hits newsstands in full on **October 5**.

Also confirmed: GTA 6 secured an ESRB **"M" (17+)** and a **PEGI 18** rating, in line with the series' long-standing mature positioning. The ratings lock down the content boundaries of regional releases, reassuring pre-order buyers.

Strategically, the LOVE cover is Rockstar pushing GTA 6 into fashion/pop-culture territory — part of the outer ring of the pre-launch marketing blitz alongside the album and merch drops. For the Chinese search vacuum, this kind of crossover material also yields differentiated long-tail keywords.
