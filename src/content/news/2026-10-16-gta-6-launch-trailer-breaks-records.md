## 中文正文

Rockstar 的《GTA6》最终上市预告片在 10 月 15 日放出后，数据一路狂飙。据 Leonida Explorer 读取的 YouTube 元数据，发布约 20 小时内播放量突破 1119 万，并在 10 月 16 日读到约 **1265 万播放、110 万点赞**；更早的节点显示，发布 5 小时即破 450 万、半小时左右破百万。评论区几乎 **98% 为正面反应**。

玩家情绪集中在「终于成真」与「等待之久」：从 2023 年 12 月首支预告到 2026 年 11 月发售，有人调侃「GTA5 出来时我还在上中学，现在我有房贷、两个孩子和腰背痛」；也有人留言缅怀未能等到这一刻的亲友。GamesRadar 置顶评论写道：「我以为自己活不到这一刻。」

**索尼同步公布两款 GTA6 限定版 DualSense。** 黑色款取 Vice City 霓虹夜生活、白色款取日间海岸线，共用变色涂层、手柄掌托的棕榈树造型、触控板上的 VI 标志与粉色面键。内部为标准 DualSense（触觉反馈 + 自适应扳机），11 月 19 日与游戏同步发售，美国预购价 84.99 美元，9 月 10 日开启预购，Direct 渠道在「低库存 / 无货」间反复跳动。

小提示：预告片是否带有「模糊滤镜 / 过重 bloom」在 Reddit 引发讨论，但 Rockstar 未作说明，目前属玩家观感差异。

## Overview

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer, released October 15, racked up extraordinary numbers. Per YouTube metadata read by Leonida Explorer, it passed 11.19 million views within roughly twenty hours and stood at about **12.65 million views and 1.11 million likes** when read on October 16. Earlier checkpoints showed 4.5 million in five hours and roughly 1 million in about half an hour. Around **98% of reactions are positive**.

Fan sentiment clusters on "it finally feels real" and the sheer length of the wait: from the December 2023 debut trailer to the November 2026 launch, one viewer joked they were in middle school when GTA V shipped and now have a mortgage, two kids and back pain; others paid tribute to friends and family who didn't live to see the moment. GamesRadar's top comment reads, "I thought I would never live this moment."

**Sony revealed two limited-edition GTA VI DualSense controllers.** The black edition takes Vice City's neon nightlife, the white its daytime coastline; both share a colour-shifting finish, palm-tree moulding on the grips, a VI logo on the touchpad and pink face buttons. Internally they are standard DualSense pads with haptics and adaptive triggers, launching November 19 alongside the game at $84.99 in the US; pre-orders opened September 10 and the Direct listings have been flipping between low stock and unavailable.

Minor note: a Reddit debate over whether the trailer carries a "blurry filter" or heavy bloom is unresolved — Rockstar has not commented, so it remains a viewer-perception difference.
