## Overview

Recently, the gaming giant Electronic Arts was taken private in a $55 billion sale to an investor consortium led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. Could GTA 6 owner Take-Two be the next big company that gets acquired? Don't bet on it, CEO Strauss Zelnick says. Speaking to David Rubenstein , Zelnick said Take-Two's business results have proven that it "can do very well as an independent company." He said Take-Two's business is already "at scale" and healthy enough financially with a "terrific balance sheet," as he put it. "We are not in a place where we need to be part of a bigger organization to be competitive. And in the entertainment business, scale matters," he said. Zelnick said companies that do less than a billion in annual revenue might have issues with developing, marketing, and distributing games at scale. Not only that, but companies that don't generate a lot of money might not be able to survive the realities of the creative business, in which sometimes your product flops.
