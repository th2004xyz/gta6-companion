## 中文正文

Rockstar 于 11 月 12 日在官方社交账号简短确认了一句：**「Code-in-box versions are also available now for pick-up at retailers worldwide.」**——盒装版已在全球零售商处上架，可以提货了。

对已经在 11 月 10 日开启数字预载的玩家来说，这只是一条补充消息；但对坚持「要拿到实体东西」的玩家，有几个现实要点必须说清楚。

**第一，盒子里没有光盘。** GTA6 的盒装版是标准的 code-in-box——内附一张下载码卡，不是蓝光光盘。Rockstar 没有解释为什么跳过实体媒介，业界普遍猜测是为了堵住发售前的泄露口子（Take-Two 今年早些时候确实起诉过泄密者）。

**第二，买盒装版照样要下 212GB。** 这是最容易误解的地方。盒装版和数字版下载的是**同一个文件**，容量、解锁时间完全一致——11 月 19 日各时区零点解锁，没有「插盘即玩」这回事。所以如果你家里网络慢或者硬盘紧张，盒装版并不能帮你省事。

**第三，11 月 20 日前购买还有预购奖励。** Vintage Vice City Pack 包含一辆经典的 '55 Vapid Stanier、Shore Court 车库、两套服装与发型，发放给 11 月 20 日之前购买的所有版本，发售当天买也来得及。PS5 / Xbox 数字预购还附赠 1 个月 GTA+（会自动续费，记得留意）。

**索尼和微软都说这是史上预购最多的游戏**，但两家公司都没有公布具体份数——第三方估算的数字（如 Sensor Tower 的约 645 万份）仍是估计值，不是官方口径，11 月 9 日 Take-Two 财报也没有给出销量。

对还没下预载的玩家：现在离解锁只剩一周，盒子到手后第一件事是尽早兑换码、开始下载，别把 212GB 留到发售当晚。

## Overview

Rockstar posted a short confirmation on its official social account on November 12: **"Code-in-box versions are also available now for pick-up at retailers worldwide."** The boxed edition has landed at retailers globally and is ready for pick-up.

For anyone who started the digital preload on November 10, this is a footnote. But for players who insist on holding something physical, a few realities are worth spelling out.

**First, there is no disc in the box.** GTA 6's boxed edition is standard code-in-box — a download-code card, not a Blu-ray disc. Rockstar has not explained the choice, though leaks prevention is the widely held theory, especially given Take-Two's recent legal action against the leaker.

**Second, the boxed edition still downloads the same 212GB.** This is the most common misunderstanding. Boxed and digital copies pull down the **same file**, with identical size and unlock timing — midnight local time on November 19, with no disc-to-play shortcut. If your connection is slow or your drive is full, the box does not help.

**Third, buying before November 20 still earns the pre-order bonus.** The Vintage Vice City Pack includes a '55 Vapid Stanier, the Shore Court Garage, two outfits and hairstyles, and goes to every copy bought before November 20 — launch day included. A digital pre-order on PS5 or Xbox also adds one month of GTA+ (which auto-renews, so keep an eye on it).

**Sony and Microsoft both call this the most pre-ordered game ever on their stores**, yet neither has published a unit figure — third-party estimates such as Sensor Tower's ~6.45 million remain estimates, not official numbers, and Take-Two's November 9 results gave no sales count either.

If you have not started the preload yet: with one week to go, the first thing to do once the box is in hand is redeem the code and start downloading. Do not leave 212GB for launch night.
