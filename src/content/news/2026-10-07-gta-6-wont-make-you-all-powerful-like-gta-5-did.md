## 中文正文

Rockstar 的 Rob Nelson 表示，GTA6 **不会**像 GTA5 及前作那样让玩家"全能"。在旧作里，除了警察，玩家是唯一藏着"隐形军火库"的人，路人只会惊恐逃窜；而 GTA6 的世界"不是这样"。

按照他的说法，GTA6 的 NPC 会有**自己的冲动**，并可能反过来追杀你——"世界会作用于你，正如你作用于世界"。你依然强大、能干，但你不是唯一能施暴、唯一有武器、唯一疯狂的人。

这延续了《荒野大镖客2》"你不是世界里唯一危险的人"的思路，让世界更不可预测、更有趣。这也呼应了 GTA6 对"亡命情侣"叙事的设计：过度张扬会推高你的犯罪画像。

## Overview

Rockstar boss Rob Nelson has stated that GTA 6 won't make players "all-powerful" like they were in GTA 5 and the games before it. In previous GTA titles, except for the police, the player was the only one with a hidden arsenal while everyone else fled in terror — "this world in GTA 6 is not like that." NPCs will have impulses of their own and may come after you: "the world will act upon you as well as you acting upon the world." You're still powerful and capable, but not the only one with weapons or violence. It extends Red Dead Redemption 2's "you're not the only dangerous person" design, making the world far more unpredictable — and ties into GTA 6's fugitive-couple story, where drawing needless attention shifts your criminal profile.
