## 中文正文

Game Informer 10 月号（数字版 9 月 29 日上线）在封面报道之外，还放出了世界层面的具体细节，梳理如下：

- **动态天气系统**：Leonida 的天气会实时变化，影响驾驶、能见度与场景观感。
- **野生动物约 170 种**：从街头的动物到荒野生态，NPC 与动物会以更动态的方式与世界互动，制造意外遭遇。
- **地图规模更大**：报道暗示 Leonida 的世界比过往作品更野心，探索成为核心体验，散布大量秘密与活动。
- **警察/通缉机制更新**：通缉与逃脱的逻辑被重新设计（具体机制报道未完全展开）。

这些都是 **confirmed** 级（Rockstar 授权的媒体合作预览，即官方口径），不是社区推测。但像"约 170 种"这种量级数字，建议等发售后实机核验——媒体预览的口径可能与最终版有微调。

## Overview

Beyond the cover feature, Game Informer's October issue (digital edition live September 29) laid out concrete world-level systems:

- **Dynamic weather**: Leonida's weather shifts in real time, affecting driving, visibility and scene mood.
- **~170 wildlife species**: from street animals to wilderness ecosystems, NPCs and fauna interact with the world more dynamically, producing chance encounters.
- **A larger map**: the reporting hints Leonida is more ambitious in scale than past entries, with exploration as a core loop and secrets scattered throughout.
- **Revised police/wanted mechanics**: the chase and evasion logic has been redesigned (the specifics weren't fully spelled out).

All of this is **confirmed**-tier — a Rockstar-sanctioned press preview, i.e. official framing, not community speculation. But magnitudes like "~170 species" are best re-verified in-game at launch; preview framings can shift from the final build.
