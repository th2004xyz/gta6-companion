## 中文正文

Bloomberg 记者 **Jason Schreier** 在 10 月 14 日 GTA6 开发长文之后，放出了一段补充视频，讲那些没能写进正文的细节。其中最出圈的一条是：**武器轮回来了**。

Schreier 在 Rockstar North（爱丁堡）采访时，开发负责人 Rob Nelson 告诉他，团队想要一套更接近《荒野大镖客 2》的携带系统——玩家必须选择一套装备、同时只能带有限数量的武器，不能再像 GTA5 那样随手掏出火箭筒。

问题随之而来：武器轮如果大部分格子是空的，会不断提醒玩家「你带不了这些」。于是团队开始找替代方案。Schreier 的原话是：

> 「他们花了几个月——如果不算几年的话——试了无数种替代武器轮的东西。托盘、网状界面、各种各样不同的 UI。」

他们甚至试过**完全取消武器选择界面**。折腾一圈之后，最终成品「还是个武器轮」。

**为什么这不只是个 UI 小故事？** 菜单保留，底下的规则可能变了。GTA5 的轮盘能直接调出整个军火库，《荒野大镖客 2》则把大部分武器放在马背上。GTA6 的取舍据报是：保留轮盘的操作速度，但让玩家必须选择更小的随身配置——留在车里的枪，交火开始时就不在手上。Rockstar 尚未公布具体能带几把，因此**携带限制属于「报道提及但未详述」**。

同一批分享里还有两条背景：GTA6 的筹备始于 **2015 年**（团队当年首次赴迈阿密考察，部分美术参与超过 11 年），内部代号 **Project Americas**；艺术总监 Aaron Garbett 表示 GTA6 的道路比 GTA5 窄得多——GTA5 的宽街道更像赛车游戏，而这个决定从一开始就贯穿整张地图，逼着团队把建筑与环境做得更密。

## Overview

Bloomberg's **Jason Schreier** followed his October 14 GTA 6 feature with a video of details that didn't make the piece. The one that travelled furthest: **the weapon wheel is back**.

Interviewing at Rockstar North in Edinburgh, development lead Rob Nelson explained that the team wanted a Red Dead Redemption 2-style loadout system — you choose a kit and carry a limited number of weapons, instead of pulling a rocket launcher out of thin air as in GTA 5.

The problem followed immediately: a wheel with mostly empty slots constantly reminds you what you can't carry. So the team went looking for alternatives. In Schreier's words:

> "They spent months, if not years, trying a million different things that would replace the weapon wheel. They tried trays and webs and all sorts of different things in the UI."

They even tested **removing the selection screen entirely**. After all of that, the final game "just wound up back at a weapon wheel."

**Why it's more than a UI anecdote**: the menu survives, but the rules underneath may not. GTA 5's wheel offered near-unlimited access; Red Dead 2 left most of the arsenal on the horse. GTA 6 reportedly keeps the wheel's speed while forcing a smaller active loadout — a gun left in the vehicle isn't in your hands when a fight starts. Rockstar hasn't said how many weapons that means, so **the carrying limit is reported but undetailed**.

Two more pieces of context came from the same session: GTA 6 planning began in **2015** (the first Miami research trip that year, with some artists on it for over 11 years) under the internal name **Project Americas**; and art director Aaron Garbett says GTA 6's roads are far narrower than GTA 5's, whose wide streets felt closer to a racing game — a decision made from the start that rippled through the whole map, forcing denser buildings and environments.
