## 现状（confirmed）

**10 月 15 日，Rockstar 无预告放出了 GTA6 的上市预告片（Launch Trailer）。** 这是自 Trailer 2（2025 年）以来的第一支新故事预告，也是社区口中的「Trailer 3」。

- **时长与形式**：约 90 秒，官方称全程为 PS5 实机画面。
- **配乐**：电音双人组 Justice 的全新单曲《Flower in a Hurricane》（feat. Tame Impala、SZA、King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard），Justice 也是 GTA6 原创配乐的作曲者；该单曲同时收录进官方原声带《GTA VI: The Album》。
- **内容主线**：Jason 与 Lucia 的关系。官方简介写道，两人「一直清楚命运对自己不公」，一次简单的行动出错后，他们卷入横跨 Leonida 州的犯罪阴谋，被迫比以往更依赖彼此。

## 关于「Trailer 3」这个名字

**官方从不使用编号预告的叫法。** 「Trailer 3」是社区依据历代营销节奏推断出来的名字——这次官方成品的名字就是「上市预告片（Launch Trailer）」。所以追踪时盯官方渠道比盯编号更可靠：Rockstar 曾经把 26 分钟实机演示《An Extended Look》（2026 年 8 月 27 日）放到 Netflix 独家首播，6 小时后才免费放出，完全跳过了传统预告节奏。

## Overview

**On October 15, Rockstar dropped the GTA 6 launch trailer with no warning.** It is the first new story trailer since Trailer 2 (2025) — the one the community has been calling "Trailer 3."

- **Length and format**: roughly 90 seconds, presented by Rockstar as all PS5 footage.
- **Music**: Justice's new single "Flower in a Hurricane" (feat. Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard). Justice also composed the original score, and the single is included on the official *GTA VI: The Album*.
- **Story**: Jason and Lucia. The official blurb says they "have always known the deck is stacked against them," and after an easy score goes wrong they land in a conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida, forced to rely on each other more than ever.

## About the name "Trailer 3"

**Rockstar never numbers its trailers.** "Trailer 3" is community shorthand derived from past marketing rhythms; the official name here is simply the launch trailer. That's why watching official channels beats watching for a number — Rockstar premiered the 26-minute *An Extended Look* (August 27, 2026) exclusively on Netflix and only put it up free six hours later, skipping the traditional trailer cadence entirely.

## How it performed

The trailer passed 11.19 million views in about 20 hours and read roughly **12.65 million views and 1.11 million likes** on October 16, with around **98% positive** reactions. See our [full records report](/en/news/2026-10-16-gta-6-launch-trailer-breaks-records).
