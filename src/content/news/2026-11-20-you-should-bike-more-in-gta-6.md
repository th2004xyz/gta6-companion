## Overview

I'm several hours into Rockstar's behemoth video game, and as big as the GTA 6 map is, I've seldom needed (and even less often preferred) more than two wheels to get around. Biking in GTA 6 is awesome, and it's a safe bet to say you should be doing it more than you are. Sure, Grand Theft Auto 6 might imply heavy use of automobiles right there in its title, and there are plenty of amazing boats , planes , and cars to find--heck, there's even a tank . But none of these other ways to get from A to B really compare to a good old-fashioned bicycle. I'm an avid biker in real life, so I wasted no time taking Jason's bike (found beneath his back deck in Chapter 1) for a joyride when I was given free rein of the open world. Quickly, I realized GTA bicycles have a number of benefits, many of which mirror real life, but a few are exclusive to the game, too. For one, every time you travel via bicycle, you're saving on gas. Gas prices in GTA 6 are as painful as they are in real life right now, and since you do have to fill up your car with gas once in a while, biking minimizes the number of trips you'll need to take to the nearest gas station. I actually haven't used a gas station yet because of all the biking I'm doing. Jason's bike spawns just behind his shed at his Chapter 1 safehouse. Since one of the best GTA 6 tips we can offer is to establish healthy habits, biking also doubles as a workout. Whenever you're biking, even if you're not speeding up (by pressing A/X on Xbox or PlayStation, respectively), you're contributing to a cardio workout. This is a fantastic way to multitask, since it can be hard to fit a gym routine into Jason and Lucia's busy lives. I was worried at first that I wouldn't be able to listen to music while biking. I didn't want to sacrifice the eclectic list of GTA 6 songs . Thankfully, Rockstar doesn't make me pick one or the other. If you buy in-ear headphones , you can use them even if you're biking and wearing your helmet. On that note, you should definitely wear your helmet; it's in the item wheel, above his hat. Not only can you choose one of three cool colorways--I prefer the highlighter-yellow one, as it's closest to mine in real life--but if you get in an accident, having your helmet on will reduce the damage you take. Biking around the world of Leonida also means you can weave through tight spaces, which is helpful when you encounter traffic jams or want to take a shortcut cars can't use. Plus, though they are slower than cars, they're not slow --and slower is good when it lets you take in more of the world and catch more of the game's emergent moments that often play out as a sideshow to whatever you're focused on. There's nothing like a little wind in your hair on the way to ripoff an illegal animal market hiding in the back of a liquor store. I mean, really, cars suck. They're loud, some of them look really ugly , and they can't do bunny hops or wheelies, which GTA bikes can do. If I'm going on a date , heading toward a timed mission , or know I'll need the stash space in my trunk , I'll drive a car. Otherwise, I've been biking for most of my time in GTA 6 so far, and I plan to keep it that way. There's so much more to see and do in Rockstar's biggest game ever, so be sure to read and bookmark our GTA 6 guides hub .
