## 中文正文

IGN 放出与 **Rockstar North 的 Rob Nelson** 的完整深度专访。在 Netflix 揭晓（《GTA6 扩展前瞻》播出）前夕，他实机演示了 **30 分钟**游戏内容。

采访覆盖了 Vice City 的运作、双主角 **Jason 与 Lucia** 的"亡命情侣"设定，以及开放世界中 NPC 的自主行为等。这也是发售前少有的、由开发者亲自讲解实机玩法的内容。

配合 9 月 Game Informer 的 12 张官方截图与世界观细节，玩家对 GTA6 的系统认知正在快速补齐。

## Overview

IGN published the full in-depth interview with Rockstar North's Rob Nelson, who played through 30 minutes of the game ahead of the Netflix reveal (the GTA 6 Extended Look). The conversation covers how Vice City works, the fugitive-couple setup of dual protagonists Jason and Lucia, and the autonomous behaviour of NPCs in the open world — a rare, developer-led walkthrough of real gameplay before launch. Alongside Game Informer's 12 official screenshots and world details from September, players' systemic understanding of GTA 6 is rapidly filling in.
