## 概述

Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire 在预告片 1 中首度亮相，预告片 2 中再次登场——这次是 Jason 从车窗探身向后射击的动作场面。作为道奇挑战者 Hellcat/Demon 的系列对应车型，它是本作美式肌肉车谱系的中坚力量。

## 已确认细节

- **两次登场**：预告片 1 交通场景 + 预告片 2 动作场面
- **动作定位**：预告片 2 中 Jason 探身车窗外向后射击，直接展示了行驶中射击的操作形态
- **原型**：基于道奇挑战者 Hellcat 系肌肉车，延续 Gauntlet 车系一贯的宽体猛兽造型

## 系列地位

Gauntlet 车系自《GTA5》起便是道奇挑战者的系列对应，Hellfire 变体来自《GTA Online》。它在两支预告片中的持续出镜说明 Rockstar 把它当作「动作感」的视觉速记——宽体、大马力、能挨枪子的美式肌肉车，永远是 GTA 枪战追逐戏的标准配置。

## Overview

The Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire debuted in Trailer 1 and returned in Trailer 2 — this time with Jason leaning out the window to fire backward. As the series' Dodge Challenger Hellcat analog, it anchors the muscle-car tier of GTA 6's roster.

### Confirmed Details

- **Two appearances**: Trailer 1 traffic and a Trailer 2 action beat
- **Action positioning**: the drive-by scene directly previews shoot-from-vehicle combat
- **Basis**: wide-body Challenger Hellcat DNA, continuing the Gauntlet line's aggressive silhouette

### Series Standing

The Gauntlet line has stood in for the Challenger since GTA 5, with the Hellfire variant arriving via GTA Online. Its recurring trailer presence marks it as Rockstar's visual shorthand for action — a wide, powerful, bullet-hungry American muscle car remains the standard-issue star of any GTA chase.
