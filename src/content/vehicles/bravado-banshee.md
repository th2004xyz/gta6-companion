## 概述

Bravado Banshee 在预告片 1 与预告片 2 的车流中均有现身。这台道奇蝰蛇（Viper）原型的双门跑车是 GTA 系列资历最老的车型之一——自《GTA3》起，双白条纹的 Banshee 就是「玩家攒钱要买的第一台快车」，二十余年后它依然行驶在 Vice City 的街头。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：两支预告片的街景车流中均可辨认
- **造型特征**：延续标志性的车身双条纹与蝰蛇式宽矮车身
- **品牌谱系**：Bravado 是 GTA 系列的美式性能车大户（挑战者、战马、蝰蛇均在其名下）

## 系列地位

Banshee 是系列历史叙事的一部分：《GTA3》的 Banshee、《罪恶都市》的 Banshee、《GTA5》的 Banshee——每个时代的 Vice City / Liberty City 都有它的位置。本作中的回归不只是一款车，更是对系列 25 年车系的连续性致敬。对系列老玩家来说，认出车流里的 Banshee 是一种「回家」时刻。

## Overview

The Bravado Banshee appears in traffic across both trailers. The Viper-based two-door is one of the oldest vehicles in the franchise — since GTA 3, its twin stripes have meant "the first fast car you save up for," and it's still prowling Vice City streets two decades on.

### Confirmed Details

- **Sightings**: identifiable in street traffic in both trailers
- **Styling**: the signature twin stripes and low, wide Viper silhouette
- **Lineage**: Bravado houses most of the series' American performance metal

### Series Standing

The Banshee is franchise history in car form — present in the GTA 3 era, the Vice City era, and the GTA 5 era alike. Its return is less a vehicle announcement than a 25-year continuity nod; for longtime players, spotting one in traffic is a homecoming moment.
