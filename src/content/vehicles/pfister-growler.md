## 概述

Pfister Growler 在预告片 2 中获得清晰的出场镜头。作为保时捷 718 Cayman 的系列对应车型，它是 Pfister（保时捷）阵营中定位低于 Comet S2 的中置引擎双门跑车——轻量、灵活、可负担，是性能车阶梯的入门台阶。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 2 中清晰可见的车身与品牌特征
- **原型**：718 Cayman 的低矮中置引擎车身比例
- **品牌谱系**：与 [Comet S2](/zh/vehicles/pfister-comet-s2) 同属 Pfister，构成「Cayman + 911」的完整保时捷产品线映射

## 系列地位

Growler 是《GTA Online》登场的 718 Cayman 对应车型，本作的回归意味着 Pfister 产品线与现实中保时捷的双门阵容高度对齐。在 GTA 的改装文化里，Cayman 类车型历来是「平民价格、赛道底子」的代名词——对于 Leonida 多山多弯的道路环境（卡拉加山及荒野），轻量中置引擎布局预计有充分用武之地。

## Overview

The Pfister Growler receives a clear on-screen appearance in Trailer 2. As the series' Porsche 718 Cayman analog, it sits below the Comet S2 in the Pfister lineup — a lightweight, agile, attainable mid-engine coupe, the first rung of the performance ladder.

### Confirmed Details

- **Debut**: clearly visible bodywork and brand cues in Trailer 2
- **Basis**: the 718 Cayman's low, mid-engine proportions
- **Lineage**: shares the Pfister badge with the Comet S2, mirroring Porsche's real two-door range

### Series Standing

The Growler arrived in GTA Online as the Cayman analog, and its return aligns the Pfister lineup almost one-to-one with Porsche's real catalogue. In GTA tuning culture, Cayman-class cars have always meant "civilian price, chassis of a racer" — promising real utility on Leonida's mountain roads around Mount Kalaga and beyond.
