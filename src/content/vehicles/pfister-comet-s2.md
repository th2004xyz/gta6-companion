## 概述

Pfister Comet S2 在预告片 1 的车流中多次现身，敞篷形态尤其醒目。作为保时捷 911（992 代）的系列对应车型，它是 Comet 车系——GTA 世界观里资历最深的「常青 911」——在本作的现代化续篇。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 1 街景与公路车流，敞篷镜头多次出现
- **造型特征**：992 代 911 的宽体圆润轮廓，软顶敞篷形态
- **品牌谱系**：Pfister 是 GTA 系列的保时捷对应品牌，Comet 自初代便是其当家车型

## 系列地位

Comet 的历史几乎与 Banshee 一样长：《GTA3》的 911 原型、《罪恶都市》的敞篷版、《GTA Online》的 Comet S2 与 Comet Retro Custom（930 原型）。本作同时确认了 Comet S2（992 原型）与经典版（930 原型）的存在——Vice City 的阳光海岸与敞篷 911 的组合，本身就是对 1980 年代南加州/南佛罗里达汽车文化的双重致敬。

## Overview

The Pfister Comet S2 appears repeatedly in Trailer 1's traffic, its convertible top especially conspicuous. As the series' Porsche 911 (992) analog, it is the modern chapter of the Comet line — GTA's longest-running "evergreen 911."

### Confirmed Details

- **Sightings**: street and highway traffic in Trailer 1, with multiple top-down shots
- **Styling**: the 992's wide, rounded silhouette in soft-top form
- **Lineage**: Pfister is the series' Porsche analog, with the Comet as its flagship

### Series Standing

The Comet's history rivals the Banshee's: the 911-based coupes of GTA 3, the convertibles of Vice City, the S2 and Retro Custom of GTA Online. With both the 992-based S2 and the classic 930-based car confirmed here, the pairing of a sun-soaked coastal city and open-top 911s is a double homage to 1980s Southern car culture.
