## 概述

佩嘉西 Zorrusso（Pegassi Zorrusso）在预告片 1 的大桥镜头中登场：Lucia 站在天窗外，车辆与一台 Grotti Furia 并行驶过跨海大桥——这一画面迅速成为本作传播度最高的瞬间之一。Zorrusso 是《GTA Online》中的知名超跑，以兰博基尼与 Italdesign 联合设计的 ZeroUno 等车型为灵感来源。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 1 大桥场面，Lucia 探身天窗外的主车
- **品牌谱系**：Pegassi 是 GTA 系列的兰博基尼对应品牌，Zorrusso 延续其棱角分明的超跑设计语言
- **定位**：本作「炫耀性消费」叙事的具象载体之一——在社交媒体围观一切的世界里，超跑本身就是内容

## 系列地位

Zorrusso 自《GTA Online》首次登场以来便是 Pegassi 阵营的旗舰之一。它的回归延续了 Rockstar 的传统：在预告片中让最华丽的载具与最张扬的角色行为同框——从《罪恶都市》的白色 Testarossa 到本作的 Zorrusso，Vice City 的超跑永远是城市名片的组成部分。

## Overview

The Pegassi Zorrusso stars in Trailer 1's bridge sequence — Lucia standing through the sunroof as it crosses the span alongside a Grotti Furia, one of the most-shared moments of the marketing cycle so far. A flagship supercar from GTA Online, its styling draws on Lamborghini's design language (the Italdesign ZeroUno among its references).

### Confirmed Details

- **Debut**: Trailer 1's bridge scene, as the car Lucia rides through the sunroof
- **Lineage**: Pegassi is the series' Lamborghini analog; the Zorrusso keeps the sharp-edged supercar language
- **Positioning**: an object of conspicuous consumption — in a world where social media watches everything, a supercar is content

### Series Standing

From the white Testarossa of 2002's Vice City to the Zorrusso crossing this bridge, the flagship supercar has always been part of the city's calling card — and Rockstar's marketing instinct to pair its flashiest metal with its boldest character beats remains fully intact.
