## 概述

Grotti Furia 出现在预告片 1 的大桥镜头中：与 [Pegassi Zorrusso](/zh/vehicles/pegassi-zorrusso) 并行，副驾乘客正举着手机拍摄探身天窗外的 Lucia——两台超跑、一次互相围观，把本作「人人都在拍、人人都在被拍」的社交媒体世界观浓缩进了一个镜头。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 1 大桥场面，作为被拍摄视角所在的车辆
- **品牌谱系**：Grotti 是 GTA 系列的法拉利对应品牌，Furia 的设计融合了 Pininfarina Battista 等电动超跑元素
- **细节彩蛋**：乘客拍摄行为本身，即预告片对社会内容生态的暗示之一

## 系列地位

Furia 是《GTA Online》后期的旗舰超跑之一，本作中的回归延续了 Grotti 品牌在霓虹都市叙事中的存在感。它与 Zorrusso 的大桥同框，构成一对「兰博基尼 × 法拉利」式的品牌对话——这种用两台顶级超跑完成的世界观陈述，正是 Rockstar 预告片叙事的经典手法。

## Overview

The Grotti Furia appears in Trailer 1's bridge shot, running alongside the Pegassi Zorrusso while its passenger films Lucia through a phone — two supercars, each watching the other, compressing the game's everyone-films-everyone social media worldview into a single frame.

### Confirmed Details

- **Debut**: Trailer 1's bridge scene, as the car carrying the filming passenger
- **Lineage**: Grotti is the series' Ferrari analog, with the Furia blending in Pininfarina Battista-style electric-hypercar cues
- **Detail**: the passenger's filming is itself part of the trailer's commentary on content culture

### Series Standing

A flagship from the late GTA Online era, the Furia's return keeps Grotti present in the neon-city narrative. Its pairing with the Zorrusso on the bridge stages a Lamborghini-versus-Ferrari brand dialogue — worldbuilding through two supercars and one phone camera, classic Rockstar trailer craft.
