## 概述

Declasse Impaler SZ 出现在 Rockstar 官网发布的 GTA6 官方截图背景中。作为雪佛兰英帕拉（Impala）的系列对应车型，Impaler 车系与 GTA 世界的 lowrider（低趴改装车）文化深度绑定——液压悬挂、艳丽涂装、街头巡游，这套源自加州与佛州拉丁社区的汽车文化，与 Leonida 的设定天然契合。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：官方截图背景中可辨识的车身轮廓
- **原型**：英帕拉系美式全尺寸轿车，SZ 为低趴改装取向的变体
- **文化符号**：Impaler 车系在《GTA Online》中是 lowrider 改装生态的主力车种

## 系列地位

从《罪恶都市故事》到《GTA Online》的 lowrider 更新，低趴改装文化一直是 GTA 世界观中拉丁街头叙事的重要组成部分。Impaler SZ 的出现预示本作改装系统将覆盖 lowrider 玩法——在预告片已确认自定义贴纸、涂装系统的基础上，液压弹跳、贴地姿态等标志性元素值得期待。

## Overview

The Declasse Impaler SZ appears in the background of an official Rockstar screenshot. As the series' Chevrolet Impala analog, the Impaler line is inseparable from GTA's lowrider culture — hydraulics, wild paint, cruising the boulevard — a tradition rooted in Californian and Floridian Latino communities that fits Leonida like a glove.

### Confirmed Details

- **Sighting**: identifiable silhouette in an official screenshot background
- **Basis**: Impala-family full-size American sedan, SZ being the slammed variant
- **Culture**: the Impaler has been the workhorse of GTA Online's lowrider scene

### Series Standing

From the lowrider updates of GTA Online back through Vice City Stories, slammed Impalas have carried Latino street culture through the series. The SZ's presence — alongside the stickers and liveries already confirmed in the trailers — points to a customization system that covers the full lowrider playbook: hydraulics, hop contests, and paint as identity.
