## 概述

Declasse Vigero ZX 出现在 Rockstar 官网发布的 GTA6 官方截图背景中。Vigero 是 GTA 系列的雪佛兰科迈罗（Camaro）对应车型，ZX 版本对应科迈罗的现代化性能形态。它与 Phoenix（火鸟原型）、Ruiner（火鸟/科迈罗混合原型）等一起，构成了预告片与截图反复呈现的美式肌肉车生态。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：官方截图背景中可辨识
- **原型**：科迈罗系美式肌肉车，ZX 为现代性能变体
- **品牌谱系**：Declasse 名下已有 Tulip、Impaler SZ、Vigero ZX 三款确认车型，是本作登场密度最高的品牌之一

## 系列地位

Vigero 自《GTA4》登场以来就是系列美式肌肉车的中流砥柱，ZX 变体来自《GTA Online》的现代化更新。在 GTA6 已确认的车辆管理新系统（燃油、电瓶、追踪装置）下，大马力后驱肌肉车的「养护成本」可能首次成为玩法考量——粗犷的 V8 与精细的资产管理，正是本作「每层系统都加深一层」设计哲学的缩影。

## Overview

The Declasse Vigero ZX appears in the background of Rockstar's official GTA 6 screenshots. The Vigero is the series' Chevrolet Camaro analog, with the ZX representing the modern performance form — joining the Phoenix and Ruiner in the muscle-car ecology the trailers keep foregrounding.

### Confirmed Details

- **Sighting**: identifiable in official screenshot backgrounds
- **Basis**: Camaro-family American muscle, ZX being the modern variant
- **Lineage**: the Vigero sits alongside the Tulip and Impaler SZ in the Declasse stable

### Series Standing

A mainstay since GTA 4 with the ZX variant arriving via GTA Online's modern updates, the Vigero now meets GTA 6's new vehicle-management systems — fuel, battery, trackers. Big-inch V8 power meeting upkeep-as-gameplay is exactly the "every system one layer deeper" philosophy the Extended Look promised.
