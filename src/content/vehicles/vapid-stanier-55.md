## 概述

'55 Vapid Stanier 是 GTA6 预购奖励载具之一，隶属于预购赠品「Vintage Vice City（复古罪恶城）」礼包。这台 1950 年代美式老爷车以加州奥什安车道（Ocean Drive）停放的老款豪华轿车为视觉母题——黄色与白色双拼涂装、宽阔的铬合金保险杠，是「迈阿密黄金年代」的经典符号。

## 已确认细节

- **获取方式**：预购游戏即可获得，属 Vintage Vice City 复古礼包
- **造型**：1950 年代美式全尺寸轿车，双色涂装致敬 Ocean Drive 沿线的复古车景
- **定位**：收藏/巡游属性的老爷车，与街头性能车形成互补的汽车文化切面

## 与预购体系的关系

Stanier '55 与 [Dominator Buggy '67](/zh/vehicles/vapid-dominator-buggy) 等终极版独占载具分属不同奖励层级：前者是「预购即得」的普惠赠品，后者绑定高价位版本的专属内容。对收藏向玩家而言，这台老爷车是入坑 GTA 汽车文化史的起点——Stanier 车系在《GTA4》《GTA5》中一直扮演「国民警车/家用车」角色，'55 版本则把这条血脉延伸到了美式汽车的黄金年代。

## Overview

The '55 Vapid Stanier is one of GTA 6's pre-order bonus vehicles, included in the Vintage Vice City pack. This 1950s American classic channels the vintage luxury sedans parked along Ocean Drive — two-tone yellow-and-white paint and sweeping chrome, a golden-age Miami icon.

### Confirmed Details

- **Availability**: included with any pre-order via the Vintage Vice City pack
- **Styling**: 1950s full-size American sedan, a two-tone homage to Ocean Drive's retro curb appeal
- **Positioning**: a collector's cruiser complementing the street-performance side of the roster

### Where It Sits in the Pre-Order Scheme

The Stanier '55 belongs to a different reward tier than Ultimate Edition exclusives like the Dominator Buggy '67 — it's the everyone-who-preorders bonus rather than premium-tier content. The Stanier line spent GTA 4 and GTA 5 as the series' default cop-and-family sedan; the '55 stretches that bloodline back into America's automotive golden age. For edition and pricing details, see the pre-order guide.
