## 概述

Declasse Vamos 在预告片 1 中与「抡大锤的女性角色」同框出现，是这支传奇预告片里被反复讨论的镜头之一。Vamos 是《GTA Online》中的 1960-70 年代美式肌肉车（原型融合了雪佛兰 Chevelle 与 El Camino 元素），它的出现宣告本作的街头不只是现代超跑的天下。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 1 中与持锤角色同框
- **造型**：经典年代美式肌肉车造型，裸露的引擎舱与粗犷细节
- **品牌谱系**：Declasse 阵营在本作确认的第三款肌肉/经典车型

## 系列地位

预告片 1 的名场面之一——大锤、老肌肉车、佛州阳光——浓缩了 GTA 系列标志性的荒诞暴力美学。Vamos 的回归延续了 Rockstar「年代混搭」的车辆阵容哲学：现代超跑、70 年代肌肉车、50 年代老爷车、lowrider 文化将在同一条街道上共存，共同构成 Leonida 多层移民与世代文化并置的世界观注脚。

## Overview

The Declasse Vamos shared the frame with the sledgehammer-wielding woman in Trailer 1 — one of the most-discussed shots of that legendary reveal. A 1960s-70s American muscle car in GTA Online (blending Chevelle and El Camino cues), its presence declared that this game's streets wouldn't belong to modern supercars alone.

### Confirmed Details

- **Debut**: Trailer 1, in the hammer scene
- **Styling**: period-correct American muscle with exposed, rough-edged detail
- **Lineage**: the third classic/muscle entry confirmed for the Declasse stable

### Series Standing

The hammer, the old muscle car, the Florida sun — that single Trailer 1 frame compressed the series' signature absurd violence into one image. The Vamos' return extends Rockstar's era-mixing roster philosophy: modern supercars, 70s muscle, 50s classics, and lowriders coexisting on the same street — a world where every generation of car culture, like every generation of immigrant story, gets to share the same sunshine.
