## 概述

Bravado Buffalo 是道奇战马（Charger）的系列对应车型，在预告片 1 的车流中首次现身。实机演示进一步确认了它的警用巡逻车版本——在六星通缉回归、执法 AI 全面强化的本作里，它将是从街头追到高速的追逐战常客。

## 已确认细节

- **民用版**：预告片 1 车流中的四门性能轿车
- **警用版**：实机演示中作为警队巡逻/追击车辆出现
- **追逐战定位**：结合演示确认的执法包抄、破门战术，警用 Buffalo 预计是中期通缉等级的主力追击车种

## 系列地位

Buffalo 车系自《GTA4》的 STC 时代起贯穿系列，《GTA5》中 Franklin 的初期座驾即为 Buffalo。它的「民转警」双形态在本作具有双重意义：民用版延续美式四门肌肉轿车的街头传统，警用版则与实机确认的 [Vapid 警用巡逻车](/zh/vehicles/vapid-police-cruiser)共同构成 Leonida 执法车队——玩家在六星通缉下要面对的，正是这些换了涂装与武装的同款性能车。

## Overview

The Bravado Buffalo — the series' Dodge Charger — first appeared in Trailer 1 traffic, with the Extended Look confirming its police-cruiser variant. With six-star wanted levels and smarter law-enforcement AI confirmed, expect this to be a fixture of pursuits from street level to highway.

### Confirmed Details

- **Civilian version**: four-door performance sedan in Trailer 1 traffic
- **Police version**: patrol/pursuit cruiser shown in the Extended Look
- **Pursuit role**: paired with the confirmed flanking and breach tactics, the police Buffalo is likely the mid-tier pursuit workhorse

### Series Standing

The Buffalo line runs from GTA 4 through GTA 5 (Franklin's starter car). Its dual identity here cuts both ways: the civilian car continues the American-sedan street tradition, while the police variant joins the Vapid cruiser in Leonida's enforcement fleet — what you'll be outrunning at five stars is essentially the same performance car, repainted and armed.
