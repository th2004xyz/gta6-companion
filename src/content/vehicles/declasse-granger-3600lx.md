## 概述

Declasse Granger 3600LX 在预告片 1 的城市车流中现身。作为雪佛兰 Suburban 的系列对应车型（3600LX 为加长轴距变体），它是 GTA 世界观中「大即是美」的美式全尺寸 SUV 代表——在以迈阿密为原型的 Leonida，这类车与海滩文化、家庭出行与罪犯押运场景都高度适配。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 1 城市交通场景
- **原型**：Suburban / Tahoe 系全尺寸 SUV，3600LX 为长轴版
- **家族谱系**：Granger 车系在《GTA5》/《GTA Online》中已是登场率最高的 SUV 之一

## 系列地位

全尺寸 SUV 在 GTA 系列中承担「万能配角」功能——家庭的日常、帮派的押运、警队的装备车，都是同一副车身的不同涂装。Granger 的回归意味着本作的车流密度与车型层级继续对标现实美国道路生态。在实机演示确认的车辆燃油/电瓶管理系统下，大型 SUV 的「油老虎」属性也可能成为玩法层面真实存在的取舍。

## Overview

The Declasse Granger 3600LX appears in Trailer 1's city traffic. As the series' Chevrolet Suburban analog (the 3600LX being the extended-wheelbase form), it represents the "bigger is better" American full-size SUV — equally at home with beach culture, family duty, and criminal logistics in a Miami-inspired Leonida.

### Confirmed Details

- **Debut**: Trailer 1 city traffic
- **Basis**: Suburban/Tahoe-family full-size SUV, long-wheelbase variant
- **Lineage**: the Granger was already among GTA 5's most-seen SUVs

### Series Standing

Full-size SUVs are the series' universal supporting cast — family hauler, crew transport, and police utility vehicle are all the same body in different liveries. The Granger's return keeps GTA 6's traffic layered like a real American road. And with the fuel and battery management systems confirmed in the Extended Look, the big SUV's thirst may finally be a genuine gameplay trade-off.
