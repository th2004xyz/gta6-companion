## 概述

德克莱 Tulip（Declasse Tulip）是 GTA6 双主角的标志性座驾之一。预告片 1 的结尾镜头中，Jason 与 Lucia 同乘这辆橙色肌肉车漂移远去——这是玩家第一次看清两位主角同框的「定妆照」级画面。实机演示中该车再次登场：演示了用撬锁工具（slim jim）无声偷车的完整过程，与已确认的潜行偷车系统直接挂钩。

## 已确认细节

- **双主角用车**：预告片 1 结尾由 Lucia 与 Jason 共同驾驶；实机演示中 Jason 用撬锁工具将其据为己有
- **橙色涂装 + 贴纸**：车身可见自定义贴纸，是载具改装系统存在的直接视觉证据
- **肌肉车定位**：经典美式双门肌肉车造型，呼应 Leonida 的南方汽车文化

## 系列地位

Tulip 是 GTA 系列的回归车型（《GTA Online》中的 Declasse Tulip），以 1970 年代美式肌肉车为原型。本作将其提升为「主角座驾」级别——对老玩家而言这是熟面孔的回归，对新玩家则是 Leonida 汽车文化的入门符号。与实机确认的燃油、电瓶、追踪装置等载具管理新系统结合，这辆老肌肉车预计会陪伴玩家度过相当长的早期流程。

## Overview

The Declasse Tulip is one of the protagonists' signature vehicles. Trailer 1 closes with Lucia and Jason drifting off together in this orange muscle car — effectively the duo's first official portrait. The Extended Look brings it back: a full slim-jim lockout sequence ties the car directly to the confirmed silent theft system.

### Confirmed Details

- **Protagonist vehicle**, co-driven by Lucia and Jason in Trailer 1's closing shot; slim-jimmed by Jason in the Extended Look
- **Orange livery with custom stickers** — direct visual proof of the customization system
- **Classic American muscle** positioning, echoing Leonida's Southern car culture

### Series Standing

A returning name from GTA Online with 1970s American muscle in its DNA, the Tulip is promoted to hero-car status here — a familiar face for veterans and an entry point into Leonida's car culture for newcomers. Paired with the fuel, battery, and tracker management systems confirmed in the showcase, this old muscle car looks set to carry players through a long stretch of the early game.
