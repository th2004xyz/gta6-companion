## 概述

Imponte Phoenix 在预告片 2 中多次现身。作为庞蒂亚克火鸟（Firebird）/ Trans Am 的系列对应车型，它的楔形车身与鸡冠引擎盖轮廓是 1970-80 年代美国街头最醒目的剪影之一——而这恰好是《罪恶都市》原作的年代底色。

## 已确认细节

- **出场方式**：预告片 2 的多组街头镜头中反复出现
- **原型**：火鸟/Trans Am 系美式肌肉车
- **品牌谱系**：Imponte 名下另有 Ruiner（火鸟/科迈罗混合原型）也在预告片中被辨认

## 系列地位

Phoenix 参与构成的是一种「年代叙事」：Vice City 的原型迈阿密在 1980 年代由 Trans Am、Testarossa与低趴英帕拉定义街头风景。Rockstar 在本作同时铺开现代超跑（Zorrusso、Furia）与 70-80 年代肌肉车（Phoenix、Vamos、Tulip），把「今天的迈阿密」与「回忆里的迈阿密」叠在同一座城里——这正是 GTA6 世界观的魅力所在：它不复古，它让所有年代的汽车文化共存。

## Overview

The Imponte Phoenix appears several times across Trailer 2. As the series' Pontiac Firebird/Trans Am analog, its wedge body and shaker hood silhouette defined 1970s-80s American streets — precisely the era that colored the original Vice City.

### Confirmed Details

- **Sightings**: recurring across multiple Trailer 2 street shots
- **Basis**: Firebird/Trans Am-family American muscle
- **Lineage**: the Imponte stable also includes the Ruiner, likewise identified in trailer footage

### Series Standing

The Phoenix contributes to a layered-era narrative: 1980s Miami's streets were defined by Trans Ams, Testarossas, and slammed Impalas, and Rockstar lays modern supercars (Zorrusso, Furia) alongside 70s-80s muscle (Phoenix, Vamos, Tulip) in the same city. GTA 6 isn't retro — it lets every decade of car culture coexist, which is exactly the point.
